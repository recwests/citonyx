## Summary
Among ChatGPT's 200 most-cited domains, publications holding OpenAI licensing deals grew from 7.5% of citations in March 2026 to 15.8% in July, then eased to 12.5% in late September. Reddit was the only partner inside the top 150 site:-searched domains, suggesting partner content is preloaded rather than retrieved.

## Details
Ahrefs Brand Radar data, March to September 2026, covering 2.4 to 3.8 million US answers per month and 17 publishers with licensing deals.

- Read it as a distribution channel: brand coverage inside licensed partner titles buys citation share that ordinary link building does not reach.
- Caveat: the share fell 3.3 points between July and September, so this is a moving allocation rather than a permanent moat.
- Pair it with first-party and affiliate placements instead of substituting for them.
