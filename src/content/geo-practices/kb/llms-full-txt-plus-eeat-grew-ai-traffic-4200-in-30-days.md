## Summary
A 30-day GEO program took a SaaS site from 1 to 43 AI referral sessions (+4,200%) by making it discoverable to AI crawlers: llms.txt plus a 5,500-word llms-full.txt, robots.txt explicitly allowing eight AI crawlers, schema markup and author attribution. AI referral traffic became the site's #3 source.

## Details
- Content depth beat volume: expanding llms-full.txt to 5,500 words had more impact than 64 existing blog posts combined.
- Copilot visitors averaged 5 minutes engagement and 12.5 events per session — the highest-quality traffic on the site.
- GEO created a halo effect: Bing organic +825%, Yahoo +3,200%, DuckDuckGo +1,400% and key events +20.8%.
- Caveat: single-site vendor case study; results are directional. Independent data (Ahrefs 137k domains) shows llms.txt files alone are rarely fetched, so depth and authority, not the file itself, likely drove the lift.
