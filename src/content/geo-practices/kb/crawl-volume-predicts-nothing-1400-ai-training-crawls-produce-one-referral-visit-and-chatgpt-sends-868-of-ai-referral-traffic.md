## Summary
The AI funnel is brutal: across 1,100+ WordPress sites, 65.3 million training crawls produced 6.8 million answer-time fetches and only 45,776 referral visits — roughly one visitor per 1,400 crawls (LovedByAI, Jul 2026). Crawl volume tells you almost nothing about traffic. ChatGPT sent 86.8% of AI referral traffic, while Perplexity, which crawled least (0.6M pages), returned the most visits per million crawls (2,269).

## Details
- Per-engine crawl-to-traffic: ChatGPT 27.7M pages crawled → 1,435 visits per million; Claude 15M → 100; Gemini 1.8M → 1,761; Perplexity 0.6M → 2,269.
- Only 0.67% of answer-time fetches produced a click; Meta and Amazon crawled 25M pages and sent zero traffic.
- ChatGPT's exact share varies by dataset: Conductor 87.4%, Previsible 92.4%, SE Ranking 74.8%, Goodie 62.6% (B2B) — the 86.8% figure depends on whose sites are measured.
- Source: LovedByAI (Aug 2026).
