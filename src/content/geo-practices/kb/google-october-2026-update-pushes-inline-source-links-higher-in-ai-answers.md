## Summary
On 15 October 2026 Google updated AI Mode and AI Overviews to show inline source links higher in responses more often, plus a dedicated top-of-answer link block for official pages, prominent article carousels, and clickable creator names in video results.

## Details
Google says inline links are being substantially increased and surfaced earlier, and it is testing a section at the top of a response listing the one or two authoritative pages with context on why each link helps. Timely-article carousels highlighting Preferred Sources are rolling out to more locations and languages. Google also reports that Preferred Sources usage grew more than 7x since the start of 2026.
