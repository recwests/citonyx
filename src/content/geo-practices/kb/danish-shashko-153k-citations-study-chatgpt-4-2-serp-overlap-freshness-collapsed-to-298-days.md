## Summary

Danish Shashko's open dataset (May 2026, 5,000 queries, 153,425 citations across 6 platforms: AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok): ChatGPT cited URLs overlap with Google top-10 at just 4.2%. Gemini fragment analysis: median cited sentence is 10 words, nothing over 18 words. Content freshness collapsed: median cited age dropped from 2.2 years (March 2026) to just 298 days.

## Details

Google AI Mode dropped text fragment exposure entirely (was 70.9% in March 2026, now 0%). Gemini expanded to 84.1% fragment coverage — the only window into Google's sentence-level chunking logic. Cited sentences cluster in the top 37% of the page — front-loaded claims win. Per-platform URL overlap: Gemini 41.1%, Perplexity 39.4%, Copilot 23.7%, AI Mode 22.5%, Grok 14.1%, ChatGPT 4.2%. Freshness collapse signals increasing recency demands. The full dataset is open on GitHub.
