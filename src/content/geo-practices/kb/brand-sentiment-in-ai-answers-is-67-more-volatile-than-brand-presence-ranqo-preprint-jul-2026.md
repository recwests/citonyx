## Summary
A single-author preprint (Ranqo, arXiv 2606.20065, Jun 2026) analyzing 100,000+ AI-search responses across 100+ brands: brand sentiment (positive/negative framing) flips ~6.7× more often than whether a brand is mentioned. ~78% of citations in AI answers go to corporate websites; among non-corporate sources, YouTube leads ahead of Reddit and Wikipedia.

## Details
CAUTION: single-author, non-peer-reviewed preprint by an author affiliated with Ranqo, a commercial GEO vendor. The companion brand-tier stratification claim (73%/44%/11% visibility by brand size) was refuted in verification. However, the sentiment-volatility point is genuinely novel: monitoring whether you're cited isn't enough — how you're framed swings round-to-round. Requires ongoing brand sentiment tracking across AI platforms, not just citation presence.
