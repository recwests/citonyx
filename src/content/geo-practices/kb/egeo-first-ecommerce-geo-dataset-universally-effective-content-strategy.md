## Summary
E-GEO (arXiv 2511.20867v2, updated Jul 2026) introduces the first e-commerce GEO benchmark with 13,747 realistic multi-sentence product queries paired with Amazon listings. Across 5 generative engines and 7 LLM rewriters, the study found that optimized prompts reveal a stable, domain-agnostic rewriting pattern — suggesting a 'universally effective' GEO strategy exists that generalizes across products, query types and engines.

## Details
15 hand-crafted GEO heuristics benchmarked across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and two other engines (Bagga et al., MIT/Columbia, Jul 14 2026). The lightweight prompt meta-optimization algorithm significantly outperforms heuristic baselines. Surprising finding: the optimized prompts share structural patterns that are domain-agnostic — effective GEO is governed by transferable principles rather than ad-hoc prompt engineering. Red-teaming showed that under a simple in-prompt defense, GEO gains reflect genuine content improvement rather than manipulation. The dataset captures Reddit-style long-form queries with rich intent, constraints and shopping contexts that existing datasets miss. Implication for practitioners: e-commerce brands can apply a systematic content rewriting framework rather than testing individual GEO tactics one by one.
