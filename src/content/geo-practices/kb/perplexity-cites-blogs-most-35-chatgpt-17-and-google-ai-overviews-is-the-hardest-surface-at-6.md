## Summary
In Minty Orange's 95-article study, Perplexity was by far the friendliest engine for blogs: 35% of tracked keywords held a Perplexity citation, against 17% on ChatGPT and just 6% in Google AI Overviews. The structural reason is that Perplexity cites a median of 6.4 different domains per answer (ChatGPT 3.1, Gemini 2.4), so independent blogs win more slots. AI Overviews are the hardest surface by a wide margin.

## Details
- Most winners win on one engine: 36 of 44 cited articles were cited on exactly one engine, 7 on two, 1 on all three.
- The most-cited domains in these travel-heavy niches were platforms — YouTube (233 appearances), Reddit (123), TripAdvisor (101), Wikipedia (40) — so blogs compete against platforms, not other blogs.
- ChatGPT was fastest to cite its winners (median 27 days vs 38 for Perplexity).
- Source: Minty Orange (Aug 2026).
