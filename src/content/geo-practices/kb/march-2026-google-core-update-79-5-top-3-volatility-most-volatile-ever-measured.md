## Summary

March 2026 Core Update caused 79.5% Top-3 position movement (most volatile ever). Original data gained +22% visibility; AI-paraphrased content lost 71% of traffic. Google's Information Gain patent became operational.

## Details

SE Ranking's analysis identified three signals re-weighted: (1) Original data and research — sites with proprietary studies gained +22% visibility; (2) Unique perspectives — content with angles not in existing top-10 rose even from lower-authority domains; (3) AI-paraphrased content — pages merely restating existing info lost 71% of traffic. The update was not an 'AI content penalty' — Google doesn't penalize AI content per se — but content that adds zero new information was demoted. 24.1% of Top-10 pages disappeared entirely.
