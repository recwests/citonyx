## Summary
Schema markup effectiveness for AI citations is deeply contested in 2026. Ahrefs' May 2026 study of 1,885 pages found no statistically significant effect: +2.4% AI Mode, +2.2% ChatGPT, -4.6% AI Overviews. However, Causabi reports 41% FAQPage lift, Surfient measured 48% conversion lift on Shopify, and GrackerAI reports 3.7×. Methodology may explain discrepancy.

## Details
The Ahrefs study controlled for page rank and content quality, while vendor studies often compare schema vs no-schema without controlling for page quality differences. Ryan Law (Ahrefs): 'Does adding schema help pages get cited? Probably not.' However, Surfient's 120-merchant Shopify panel found 48% conversion lift from FAQPage specifically. Google removed FAQ rich results in May 2026. Best practice: implement FAQPage for visible on-page FAQs (schema must match visible content) but do not expect automatic citation lift; focus on content quality first.
