## Summary
BrightEdge research (April 2026) shows AI agent requests reached 88% of human organic search activity. OpenAI agents dominate: GPTBot = 51% of training crawls, OAI-SearchBot = 49% of AI search crawls, ChatGPT-User = 96% of AI user traffic. Cloudflare projects AI bot traffic will exceed total human web traffic by 2027.

## Details
BrightEdge now tracks 12+ AI agents including GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, AppleBot, Bytespider, Google-Extended, NotebookLM Bot, and DuckAssistBot. Key recommendation: differentiate robots.txt policies — allow search/user agents (OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot) while optionally restricting training agents (GPTBot, ClaudeBot). 12% of top 1000 websites block OAI-SearchBot. 73% of sites have technical barriers blocking AI crawler access (Yext January 2026).
