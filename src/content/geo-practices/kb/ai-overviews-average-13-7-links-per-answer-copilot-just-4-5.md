## Summary
Ansvisor analysed 103,427 tracked AI answers and 746,702 citations from 15 September to 14 October 2026. Google AI Overviews averaged 13.7 links per answer, Gemini 12.3, ChatGPT 5.2, Google AI Mode 4.8 and Microsoft Copilot 4.5. Fewer citation slots per answer makes each one harder to win on Copilot and AI Mode than on AI Overviews.

## Details
Method: fixed prompt set run on a schedule across six platforms, 18 brands in the AI visibility software category. Numbers are category-specific and should not be generalised to other verticals.

- Practical use: set citation targets and expectations per engine instead of one blended benchmark.
- On Copilot and AI Mode, displacing an existing source matters more than publishing another page.
- Window 2026-09-15 to 2026-10-14; single vendor study, US/English prompts.
