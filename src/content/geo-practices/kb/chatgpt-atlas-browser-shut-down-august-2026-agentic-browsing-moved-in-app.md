## Summary
OpenAI announced on 10 July and shut down the standalone ChatGPT Atlas browser on 9 August 2026, less than a year after launch. Agentic browsing was absorbed into the ChatGPT desktop app and Codex, and bookmarks, history and saved passwords did not migrate automatically for users.

## Details
Atlas launched in October 2025; OpenAI announced retirement on 10 July 2026 and closed it 9 August 2026. Browsing capability now ships inside the ChatGPT desktop application plus a Chrome sidebar or extension. User data, including bookmarks, history, saved passwords and cookies, did not transfer automatically, and OpenAI advised manual export. Prompt-injection risk in agentic browsing remains unsolved across AI browsers. Implication: track in-app and sidebar citation surfaces rather than optimizing for standalone AI browser products that may be deprecated.
