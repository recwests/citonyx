## Summary

The Conductor 2026 AEO Benchmarks Report (13,770 domains, 17M AI responses) found AI referral traffic converts to sign-ups at 1.66% vs 0.15% for organic search — an 11x difference. Perplexity-specific: 10.5% sign-up rate with inline linked citations. ChatGPT referral: 14.2-15.9%. Claude: 16.8%. Microsoft Clarity (1,200+ publisher sites) confirmed: AI referral = 1.66%, social = 0.46%, organic search = 0.15%, direct = 0.13%.

## Details

- Conductor 2026: 13,770 domains, 17M AI responses. AI referral sign-up rate: 1.66% vs organic 0.15% (11x).
- Per-platform: Claude 16.8%, ChatGPT 14.2-15.9%, Perplexity 10.5%, dark AI (no referrer) 10.2%.
- Microsoft Clarity (1,200+ publisher sites, 2025): AI referral 1.66%, social 0.46% (-72%), organic search 0.15% (-91%), direct 0.13% (-92%).
- Adobe Analytics: generative AI traffic to US retail grew 1,200% (July 2024 to Feb 2025).
- Perplexity: 22M MAU, 12-min avg sessions, 90-day return rate.
- Practical takeaway: Perplexity citations are highest ROI per click. Prioritize structured authoritative content for Perplexity first, then AI Overviews for scale.
