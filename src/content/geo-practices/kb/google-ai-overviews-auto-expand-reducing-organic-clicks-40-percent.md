## Summary
Google confirmed on Aug 28 that AI Overviews can dynamically expand into full answers, removing the 'Show More' button. An ISB/CMU field experiment found hiding AI Overviews increased organic clicks by 39.8% and cut zero-click searches by 34.5%.

## Details
Google spokesperson Jennifer Kutz confirmed AI Overviews 'may dynamically expand for topics where our systems determine it's most useful.' The Indian School of Business and Carnegie Mellon University produced the first causal measurement: a randomized field experiment with 1,065 US Chrome users found hiding AI Overviews lifted outbound organic clicks by 39.8% and cut zero-click searches by 34.5%. AI Overviews triggered on ~41% of observed queries, and >87% occupied the top slot. Independent tracking puts AI Overview presence at ~48% of tracked queries as of early 2026, up 58% YoY. Google's deadline to supply publishers with impressions, clicks, and CTR for AI search features is December 3, 2026, per the UK CMA. Simple factual, comparison, and how-to queries are most affected.
