## Summary

WebFX (July 2026) analyzed 590,868 AI sessions across 2,500 URLs in 15+ industries. Homepages drove 31.3% of all AI traffic, followed by product pages 16.8%, service pages 11.4%, blog 11.3%. 92% of AI sessions landed on consideration or decision-stage content — users are evaluating, not browsing. ChatGPT generated 97.5% of all AI referral traffic in the dataset.

## Details

Transactional content drove 70% of AI sessions vs only 18% informational. Blog articles had the highest 'AI Lift' vs organic (+7.3pp), followed by product pages (+7.1pp). Pages cited in AI answers averaged 4.76/5 specificity and 4.15/5 completeness scores. 52.6% of high-citation pages featured original research. Pages with explicit expert credentials averaged 26% more AI traffic. Homepages were the single largest AI destination but accounted for even larger organic share (44%), suggesting AI distributes traffic more evenly.
