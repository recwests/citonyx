## Summary

Texta's analysis of ChatGPT's March 2026 citation update: content under 90 days old gets 3-4x higher citation rates in browsing mode. New citation link formats introduced (inline footnotes + source cards + hover links). Different citation behavior across GPT-4, browsing mode, and SearchGPT. Source card format drives 28% higher CTR; inline footnotes show 22% decrease. Original research and comprehensive guides gained prominence; thin product pages deprioritized.

## Details

Three major changes in the March 2026 ChatGPT citation update: 1) New link formats (inline footnotes 50% of citations, source cards 30%, hover links 20%) — each drives different user behavior; 2) Mode-differentiated citation behavior — GPT-4 mode favors training data (avg 2.1 citations/response vs 3.4), browsing mode favors fresh content; 3) Content freshness weighting — content <90 days old gets 3-4x higher citation probability. Recommended: add visible 'last updated' date with time element, re-publish stale content with substantial updates, prioritize original research over aggregated content.
