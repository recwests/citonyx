## Summary
Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2026: only 20% of global respondents trust AI chatbot answers for news content. UK trust is just 6%, although AI news users report 44% trust. Pew (June 2026): 42% of US adults use AI for info search but 63% say AI is advancing too quickly.

## Details
- The trust gap between general population (20% trust AI) and active AI users (44% trust) indicates a familiarity curve.
- For brands cited by AI, this creates a dual dynamic: AI citation provides visibility, but user skepticism means cited brands may need to invest in trust signals on their own sites (author profiles, methodology transparency, external validation).
- Risk: AI answers may reduce source credibility by association. Brands should link to original research and named authors.
