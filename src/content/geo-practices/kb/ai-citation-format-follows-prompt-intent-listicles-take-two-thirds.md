## Summary
Listicles are the dominant cited format: 66.6% of 519,140 classifiable page citations in OtterlyAI's September 2026 sample, versus 7.9% for head-to-head comparisons. But format mostly mirrors question shape. Ranking prompts returned 73.2% listicles, comparison prompts 62.3% head-to-head pages, and open-ended prompts tripled the how-to share to 11%.

## Details
Scope: 617,985 citations across 85,330 URLs, five US industries, seven AI engines, 1-30 September 2026; page format identified for 84% of citations.

- The 66.6% figure is inflated by prompt design: 295 of 400 tracked prompts asked for best or top rankings, which mechanically pull ranked lists.
- Open-ended prompts dropped listicles to 55.2% while how-to guides rose from 3.6% to 11%.
- Practical read: build a two-column map of your prompt set by intent before commissioning content. A comparison page aimed at best-X demand competes in a listicle slot and usually loses, and the reverse is equally mis-shaped.
