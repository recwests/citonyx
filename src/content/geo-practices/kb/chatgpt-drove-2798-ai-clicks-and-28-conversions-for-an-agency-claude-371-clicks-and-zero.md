## Summary
Omnius' six-month case study treats AI search as its own acquisition channel: 5,050 AI clicks, up 138%, and 36 sales-qualified leads, up 414%. ChatGPT produced 2,798 clicks and 28 conversions, Perplexity 1,480 clicks and 3 conversions, Claude 371 clicks and none, Gemini 321 clicks and 2. Average AI visibility reached 47% at position 4.9.

## Details
- Method: prompt discovery from ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity instead of keyword volumes; modular answer blocks (best for, strong for, not ideal for) in the first 150 words; engine-specific formatting; LLM referral traffic tracked as a GA4 channel group plus a proprietary tool.
- 193 citations were tracked across 15 bottom-of-funnel prompts, and engine-level tracking showed conversion rates differ sharply by engine, so blending them hides the useful signal.
- Stated takeaway: high-intent AI referrals arrive pre-educated and convert better than classic organic ones.
- Caveat: single-agency vendor case study with no control, self-reported metrics and an unverifiable attribution model.
