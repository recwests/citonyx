## Summary
Source citations in US ChatGPT prompts rose from about 1.6% in June 2025 to roughly 6.8% by May 2026 — quadrupling in under a year — but remain highly category-dependent. Travel & Hospitality prompts cite sources ~23% of the time, Automotive ~20%, while Professional Services sits under 4% (Similarweb).

## Details
- The climb wasn't linear: citations jumped sharply in October 2025, eased for four months, then resumed rising in spring 2026. Categories built on comparable, checkable facts (flights, cars, hotels) get cited three to five times more often than advice-based categories.
- Even with fivefold growth, most ChatGPT answers still lack visible source links, so a publisher can appear in an answer without earning a click-through.
- After ChatGPT's May 7 2026 search update, the share of US desktop visits landing on webpages roughly doubled from 25% (March) to nearly 60% (May 30), driven by more prominent blue links — link-display engineering matters as much as aggregate impressions.
- Practical read: prioritize GEO effort in high-citation categories first and track citation rate monthly rather than trusting any single month's data point as the new normal.
