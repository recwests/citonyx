## Summary
Google is rolling out a Universal Commerce Protocol hub in Merchant Center for US merchants, with Australia and Canada next year. Retailers pick capabilities such as cart transfer, native checkout on Google and account linking, test them in a sandbox, and fix feed issues so products can be bought inside AI Mode and Gemini.

## Details
Because it sits on existing Merchant Center infrastructure, adoption can move faster than a new protocol would.

- For GEO teams the implication is a shift from being cited to being transactable.
- Product feeds, availability and account linking become answer-surface assets, and measurement moves from referral sessions toward assisted conversions inside the AI interface.
- Source is Google's own documentation; rollout timing and eligible capabilities vary by merchant account and market.
