## Summary
Presenc AI analysis of 3,800 Claude responses: 11% name a single recommended brand; 54% give shortlist without ranking; 21% shortlist with ranking; 9% list 5+ brands; 5% decline to name brands. Enterprise queries cite peer-reviewed sources at 4.2x consumer rate.

## Details
Claude's safety filters and training data emphasize verifiable high-trust sources. Brands cannot bypass this through aggressive marketing content — Claude's path runs through earned authority. Claude does not expose inline citations in default consumer chat, but API/Enterprise deployments with retrieval enabled show citations. Claude's enterprise-skewed user base means B2B brands face a higher source-quality bar. Hedging should be read as a feature: 'one of the leading vendors with strong privacy posture' positions more credibly than 'the best vendor'.
