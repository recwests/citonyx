## Summary
Perplexity runs a live search for every query — no memory answers — visiting ~10 pages and citing only 3-4 (100M+ queries/month, 450% growth in 18 months). It weights freshness heavily and cites structured, factual, recent, indexable pages; brands cited across G2, Reddit and press earn 3.4x higher citation rates (Erlin, 500+ brands).

## Details
- Selection criteria: direct relevance, clear factual structure, domain authority, freshness, technical accessibility.
- Tactics: answer within the first 5-10 lines; explicit H2/H3 summaries; citable sentences in the pattern [claim + number/qualifier + attribution]; atomic self-contained paragraphs; quantified data over opinion; quarterly updates with visible dates; sub-2s load times; Article/FAQPage/HowTo/Organization schema; llms.txt as a low-cost positive signal.
- Erlin: a brand cited across G2, Reddit and press hits 78% AI coverage vs 18% for own-content-only brands; keyword density has near-zero impact (Seer Interactive, 7,000 citations).
- Contrast with ChatGPT (training-data based, Wikipedia-leaning) and AI Overviews (draws ~97% from top-20 organic).
- Caveat: vendor-published guides; growth figures are Perplexity's own. Track the perplexity.ai referrer and PerplexityBot in logs.
