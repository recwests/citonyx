## Summary

Google AI Overviews appeared in ~43% of tracked searches as of July 2026 (Similarweb), up from 15% a year earlier. 2.5B+ monthly users across 200+ countries. AI Mode merged with AIO at I/O 2026, running on Gemini 3.5 Flash. Top organic result loses 58% of clicks when AIO is present (Ahrefs).

## Details

France was the last major market to get AIO on July 22, 2026. Google claims AI features send 'billions of clicks' weekly (Nick Fox, July 2026). Common Sense Media rated AI search 'Unacceptable Risk' for children after 2,624 test interactions. Pew: users click a result on 8% of visits with AI summary vs 15% without. AI Mode surpassed 1B monthly users. Gemini 3 -> Gemini 3.5 Flash at I/O 2026.
