## Summary

Fractl's 2026 AI Search Consumer Trust Study found trust in AI search fell from 82% to 54% year-over-year. While 80% of users feel confident AI gives unbiased information, 85% still double-check AI answers elsewhere — the two-step verification journey is now standard consumer behavior.

## Details

Only 28% of Americans trust AI search (Search Engine Journal, July 2026). Eight Oh Two study (500 users): 80% confident AI is unbiased but 85% verify. 61.8% trust their own judgment more than AI, yet 58.8% believe AI will become their main search method. 47% start search on Google/engines vs 37% on AI tools. AI for purchase decisions is common despite lower trust — a key tension for brands.
