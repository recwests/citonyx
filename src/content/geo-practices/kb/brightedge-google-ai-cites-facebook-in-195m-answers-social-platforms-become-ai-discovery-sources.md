## Summary
BrightEdge July 2026 research found Google's AI cites Facebook in 19.5 million answers, marking social platforms as emerging AI discovery sources. B2B technology queries now trigger AI Overviews at 82% rate. The findings signal expanding AI source diversity beyond traditional web content.

## Details
The BrightEdge report documents a broader trend of AI citation sources diversifying beyond traditional publisher and brand content. Social platforms, community content, and user-generated sources are increasingly appearing in AI-generated answers. This complements earlier findings that Reddit now accounts for 20-24% of all Perplexity citations (Tinuiti Q1 2026) and GPT-5.5 Instant's dramatic shift toward citing Reddit.
