## Summary
Research logged by Known & Cited in October 2026 found only 1.7% of question-and-website pairs were cited by all four engines, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, across 72 prompts and three B2B brands. Separately, only one in four brands appeared consistently across persona variants of the same question.

## Details
Orbit Media, September 2026: 72 prompts across three B2B brands yielded just 1.7% four-engine agreement, a directional sample. Only one in four brands are shared between answers generated for different buyer personas on the same topic. Ten Speed, September 2026, found 88% of B2B evaluation-stage citations pointed to brand-controlled content versus 84% earned media overall. Implication: per-engine and per-persona monitoring is mandatory because a single-engine snapshot systematically misleads. Caveat: small, agency-run prompt sets, so treat figures as directional rather than benchmarks.
