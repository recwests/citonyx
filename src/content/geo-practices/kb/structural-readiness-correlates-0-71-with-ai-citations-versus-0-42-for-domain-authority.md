## Summary
An analysis of 6.8 million citations reported in 2026 found structural readiness, meaning clean headings, lists, tables and extractable passages, correlates +0.71 with AI citation rate versus +0.42 for domain authority. A separate study, GEO-SFE in March 2026, measured a 17.3% citation lift from structural changes alone.

## Details
Digital Applied's 6.8-million-citation analysis places structural readiness at +0.71 correlation with citation rate against +0.42 for domain authority. GEO-SFE (Yu et al., March 2026) produced a 17.3% citation improvement from restructuring alone, without changing a single word of copy. Naive unstructured edits can backfire: SAGEO-style structural work reported a 4.54 point retrieval loss on body-only edits. Implication: fix scanability, question-style headings, short paragraphs, tables and lists before chasing authority. Caveat: correlations vary by engine and query type and no technique is uniformly stable.
