## Summary
A dedicated GEO/AI-visibility tool category has matured by 2026: Otterly.ai from $29/mo, Ahrefs Brand Radar tracking six engines via 260M+ monthly prompts, Peec AI at $499/mo for enterprise, and entry-level add-ons around $99/mo (Semrush AI Visibility). Five-engine coverage is now the baseline, not the premium.

## Details
- Sources: Okara.ai tool comparison (June 2026), Surferstack buyer's guide, Conductor AEO/GEO Benchmarks.
- Choose by engine coverage (five engines is the 2026 baseline; single-engine tracking misses 40-60% of mentions), pricing model, and whether the tool only monitors or also optimizes.
- Track mention rate, citation rate, share of voice, sentiment and competitive overlap.
- 50% of AI answers are unstable at 20 runs — most tools sample once/day, creating false precision; three 'ChatGPTs' exist (API, logged-out UI, logged-in UI) and most tools measure the cheapest one.
- Caveat: scoring algorithms are proprietary and not cross-comparable — pick one tool and stay consistent.
