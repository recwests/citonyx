## Summary
Anthony Lee's study of 19,556 queries across 5 AI platforms found only 1.4% URL overlap (Jaccard similarity). Within-platform consistency: ChatGPT 61.9%, Claude 58.3%, Perplexity 64.7%, Gemini 60.1%. ChatGPT is most Wikipedia-dependent (47.9% of top domains). Perplexity most Reddit-dependent (24%). Gemini shows highest YouTube citations (22%).

## Details
ChatGPT URL overlap with Google Top-3: 7.8%; Domain overlap: 28.7%. Claude: 24.2%. Perplexity: 29.7% URL, 49.6% domain. Gemini: 32.4% URL (highest). Google rank is a strong AI citation predictor across all platforms (34x odds gradient top-3 vs rank 31-100). Perplexity easiest to get cited on (more domains cited per answer); Claude hardest (fewer sources). YouTube citations: concentrated on Google AI Mode and Perplexity; near-zero on ChatGPT and Claude.
