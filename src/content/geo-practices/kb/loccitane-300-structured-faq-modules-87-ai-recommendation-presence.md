## Summary
L'Occitane en Provence rebuilt content for AI readability across 22 EMEA markets, deploying more than 300 FAQ modules in structured data and analysing 5,000 prompts weekly across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. The brand now appears in 87% of tracked prompts that produce a product recommendation, earns 73% positive mentions and reports +154% organic revenue.

## Details
It also fixed review accessibility: pagination, robots.txt blocks and incomplete JavaScript rendering meant AI engines saw only part of the review corpus.

- It detected a product-selection error, with engines recommending body care for hair-care queries, and restructured content accordingly.
- Vendor case study with self-reported metrics and no control group.
- The +154% revenue reflects SEO and GEO work together, not FAQ schema alone.
