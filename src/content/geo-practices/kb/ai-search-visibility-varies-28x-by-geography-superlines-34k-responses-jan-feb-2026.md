## Summary
Superlines analyzed 34,234 AI responses across 10 platforms over 30 days (Jan-Feb 2026): US brand visibility 2.49% and citation rate 10.31% vs non-US markets at 1.15-1.90% visibility and 3.73-6.58% citation rate — a 2.8× US advantage.

## Details
- AI search is not uniformly global. US markets receive disproportionate citation coverage, likely reflecting training data bias, US-centric indexing depth, and language processing quality.
- Non-US brands need to pursue localized content strategies: produce content in the local language with locally relevant citations, build mentions in regionally authoritative domains, and ensure hreflang and locale-specific structured data are implemented correctly.
