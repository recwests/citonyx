## Summary
ChatGPT Shopping builds product cards through a specific retrieval pipeline. Profound analyzed 201,137 prompts containing 812,190 product cards: card commercial information is pulled from the rank-1 offer, GPT tags give a +144% top-rank lift, and higher median review counts add +123%. Reddit supplies roughly a third of shopping citations.

## Details
Profound (Jul 23, 2026): 201,137 unique prompts, 812,190 product cards, June 18-25, 2026; 87.3% of cards from web crawl, 12.7% from direct merchant product feeds. Rank-1 offer wins: when offers are retrieved, the card's commercial info always comes from the rank-1 offer; otherwise ChatGPT falls back to its own product search. Top vs rank-4+ cards: GPT tag presence +143.97%, median review count +123.58% (787 vs 352), promotional price +13.3%. Reddit is ~1/3 of shopping citations (top-100 source analysis, ~3.3M citations). Action: win rank-1 in the offer list, accumulate reviews, and frame products around common GPT tags (budget, premium, best value, style).
