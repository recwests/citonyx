## Summary
AI citation likelihood compounds with third-party mention breadth: brands mentioned across 25+ trusted sources are cited at roughly 4x the rate of brands with under 10 sources, controlling for industry. Magna's aggregation also found a 3x+ rise in monthly mention velocity typically lifts citation rates within 8-12 weeks.

## Details
- Seven source types account for 73% of all AI citations: Wikipedia/Wikidata 47% (factual), Reddit+Quora 24% (up 8 points YoY), editorial 12%, industry sites 10%, .gov/.edu 9%.
- Mentions are multiplicative, not additive: each high-quality mention compounds citation likelihood.
- Listicle inclusion is the most consistent velocity driver, generating 5-15 downstream mentions within 60-90 days.
- Brands with comprehensive schema on every page (Organization, Article, FAQ, Person) are cited at 2.3x the rate of thin/homepage-only schema.
