## Summary
An analysis of Ahrefs' 1,885-page study concludes that Google's Knowledge Graph, ChatGPT's entity database and Perplexity's source ranking each assemble entity profiles from cross-domain signals, including earned media, Wikipedia references, consistent business data and executive profiles. A brand with ambiguous entity signals gets skipped regardless of on-page quality, so markup alone cannot resolve an unclear entity.

## Details
- The same review lists the signals engines cross-check: consistent name, address and phone data, Wikipedia and Wikidata references, executive bios with linked profiles, and independent coverage.
- This aligns with schema guidance published in October 2026: pick the most specific entity type that is true, assign a stable ID, pull values from the visible page, verify against third-party sources, validate, then scale, because scaling before validating multiplies one error across a thousand pages.
- A separate analysis reported pages with three or more applicable schema types earned roughly 13% more Perplexity citations, and that structured content had about 2.5 times higher odds of appearing in generated answers.
- Caveat: the cross-domain signal list is a practitioner synthesis, not a controlled experiment.
