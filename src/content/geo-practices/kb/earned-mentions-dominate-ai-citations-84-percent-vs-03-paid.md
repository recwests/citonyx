## Summary
Two GEO experiments tracked 775 citation events across 10 platforms. Third-party placements accounted for 85.8% of all mentions, with earned media at ~84% and paid/advertorial at just 0.3%. Owned content was dramatically slower and less effective.

## Details
The first test targeted 15 commercial-intent keywords across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity over several months. A comprehensive listicle on Indeed SEO generated 190 citations — more than all other sources combined. Government, education, and news domains consistently outperformed generalist outlets. Peer associations mattered: listicles including recognized industry experts like Lily Ray and Aleyda Solis saw citation jumps that dropped immediately when those names were removed. The second experiment (30-day cold start for a SaaS agency) showed 298 appearances with Gemini and Google AI Mode comprising two-thirds. A key finding: updating the owned listicle to include leading competitors triggered a 12-fold increase in mentions (4→49). Half of all sources stopped being cited within 30 days, requiring ongoing maintenance.
