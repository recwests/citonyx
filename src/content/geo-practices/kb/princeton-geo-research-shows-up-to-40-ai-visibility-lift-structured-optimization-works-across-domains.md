## Summary

The Princeton KDD 2024 paper that defined GEO showed 40% visibility lift in generative engine responses using citation optimization strategies. 2026 case studies confirm the framework across industries: manufacturing (2,300% AI traffic growth via E-E-A-T), fintech (7x AI visibility via owned+earned), e-commerce (200% monthly growth via schema+llms.txt), SaaS (52.6% traffic growth via structured content). Effectiveness varies by domain — domain-specific optimization is essential.

## Details

The Princeton study demonstrated that AI engines strongly favor earned media (authoritative third-party sources) over brand-owned content. The 2026 GEO framework follows: Assess → Optimize → Measure → Iterate. Key finding from follow-up research: AI citation bias toward authoritative sources means DOI-based referencing and academic-style citation formats improve extraction rates. A Princeton study on citation bias in AI search (2025) confirmed this pattern. Brands must treat GEO as ongoing discipline, not one-time fix.
