## Summary
AI crawlers behave nothing like Google: they visit most pages exactly once and largely skip homepages. Trakkr analyzed 575,788 crawler visits and found 88.5% of URLs receive a single visit, GPTBot reaches homepages only ~3% of the time, and 21% of ChatGPT Search sessions start on blog pages. Content must be crawl-ready before the first visit.

## Details
Trakkr Study 003 (Feb 2026): 575,788+ server-log crawler visits, 7 major AI crawlers, June 2025-February 2026, 84 brands. One-and-done crawling: 88.5% of URLs visited exactly once, only 2.4% earn a third visit — optimize the first crawl, not ongoing tweaks. OpenAI controls ~72% of AI crawler traffic (ChatGPT Training + Search); GPTBot homepage rate ~3% vs ClaudeBot ~19%. Blog is the new front door: 21% of ChatGPT Search sessions start on blog pages vs 1% on homepages; product pages 3%. 3-click rule: ~52% of crawled URLs sit within 3 clicks of the homepage; keep key content shallow.
