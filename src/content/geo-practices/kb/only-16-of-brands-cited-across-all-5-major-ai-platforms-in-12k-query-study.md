## Summary

Only 16% of brands are cited across all five major AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot) based on 12,000 brand-relevant queries across 23 industries.

## Details

The AgentVisibility.ai 2026 State of AI Visibility study submitted 12,000 brand-relevant queries across 23 industries to the five major AI platforms over 90 days. Only 16% of brands appeared on all five. The first-mentioned brand receives 47% of click-through. Hallucination rate stands at 8.3% (down from 14.1% in mid-2025). Businesses with validated schema markup had a hallucination rate of just 2.1% vs 12.7% without — a 6x reduction.
