## Summary
The UK's Competition and Markets Authority imposed a legally binding conduct requirement on Google in June 2026 letting publishers opt out of having their content power AI Overviews and AI Mode without losing normal search rankings, and requiring clearer attribution links. It is the first enforceable rule of its kind.

## Details
- The CMA imposed the Publisher Conduct Requirement on Google on 3 June 2026 under its digital-markets regime, after designating Google with strategic market status in search (gov.uk).
- Publishers can prevent their content being used to power AI features in Google Search, including AI Overviews, AI Mode and AI Overviews in Discover, without losing normal search rankings, and must also be able to opt out of AI fine-tuning.
- Google must add clear attribution links in AI-generated results and expose engagement metrics such as impressions, clicks and CTR via a platform like Search Console, with nine months to comply and reports every six months.
- A related Fair Ranking requirement takes effect 17 December 2026.
