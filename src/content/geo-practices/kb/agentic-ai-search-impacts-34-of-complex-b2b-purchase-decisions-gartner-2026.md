## Summary
Gartner's 2026 Digital Marketing Report: 34% of complex B2B purchase decisions now involve AI agents completing preliminary research phases before human engagement. Pages with 'Agent-First' structure (immediate value proposition, structured specifications, comparison-ready details) receive 67% more AI agent citations per Search Engine Journal 2026.

## Details
Three search paradigms now operate in parallel: traditional (click and browse), answer-based AI (consume summary), and agentic AI (accept recommendation). For agentic AI, the goal shifts from ranking or citation to recommendation frequency. Content must prioritize extractable data points over persuasive copy — product pages should lead with specifications, pricing, compatibility. Schema markup focus shifts from rich snippets to AI agent data extraction.
