## Summary

65.9% of users report citations increase their trust in AI answers, even if they don't click the links. Only 27% 'often' click citations. 43.9% click to double-check facts, 29.3% out of curiosity. 58.5% of Google searches end in zero clicks. AI answer CTRs remain below 1% on average. This creates a trust-but-verify dynamic where citations serve as credibility signals rather than navigation tools.

## Details

96% of AI search discussions happen on X (Twitter). 13M Americans regularly use AI search tools. 48.7% of respondents in the Eight Oh Two study start searches on traditional search engines vs 37% on AI. ChatGPT ~800M weekly active users, Perplexity ~780M monthly queries (239% YoY growth). AI assistants now handle estimated 1.5B+ queries daily across all platforms.
