## Summary
Advanced Web Ranking tracked more than 1.75 million US SERPs monthly in July 2026. An AI Overview appearing alone cut organic click-through rate at position 1 by 36%; when stacked with local packs, product grids, People Also Ask and videos, the loss reached 99%. Zero-click share hit 74.3% on AI Overview SERPs versus 53.6% without one.

## Details
Sample: roughly 1 million desktop and 750,000 mobile US SERPs with full feature tracking (advancedwebranking.com). AI Overviews appeared on 43% of sampled desktop SERPs in July 2026. An isolated AI Overview reduced position-1 CTR by 36%; combined with a local pack, shopping grid, People Also Ask and video the reduction reached 99%. 74.3% of AI Overview SERPs produced no organic click versus 53.6% for SERPs without an AI Overview. Implication: measure intent clusters rather than average CTR, because commercial and visual SERPs lose the most clicks.
