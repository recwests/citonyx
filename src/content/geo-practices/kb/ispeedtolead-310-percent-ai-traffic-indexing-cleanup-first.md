## Summary

iSpeedToLead grew AI search clicks 310%, reached #1 citation share in its market (7.79%, ahead of HousingWire at 5.68%), and routed AI clicks to revenue pages (leads, signup) — not blog — by first fixing a 45K-page indexing crisis, then rebuilding content for AI extractability.

## Details

Five-phase approach: (1) Google indexing cleanup — not-indexed pages from ~45K to ~1,650, indexed from 180 to 260; (2) core service page repositioning around buyer intent; (3) 30 LLM-optimized blog posts (middle/bottom funnel only, with conversion CTAs and authority signals); (4) external authority building on third-party publications; (5) ongoing consulting. Results: +310.8% AI search click growth, 7.79% citation share (#1 in competitive set), +2,900% AI clicks to my-leads page, +543% to /leads, +200% to /signup. AI visitors spend 4:36 on leads page. ChatGPT clicks +276%, Claude clicks +2,200%.
