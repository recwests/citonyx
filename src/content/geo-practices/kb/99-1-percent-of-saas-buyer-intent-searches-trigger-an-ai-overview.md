## Summary
ClearBrand tested 453 SaaS searches and 400 AI answers: 99.1% of buyer-intent keywords showed an AI Overview citing 10.6 links on average. YouTube took 20.9% of those citations and appeared on 72% of keywords, Reddit took 4.2%, and only 36.3% of cited domains also ranked in the organic top ten.

## Details
Method: 453 SaaS software searches on Google US desktop, October 12-13 2026, plus 100 buyer prompts each on ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. AI Overviews cited 4,587 links across 432 keywords, 10.6 per answer. YouTube appeared on 311 of 432 keywords, ahead of Reddit (149), Zapier (77), G2 (71), Gartner (59), Trustpilot (53), PCMag (47). Source mix: other blogs and vendors 40.2%, community and UGC 25.9%, SaaS vendors 18.3%, review directories 8.7%, publishers and media 6.9%. Only 36.3% of citations pointed to a domain ranking in the organic top ten. Caveat: single-vendor study, one market, one week, one run per keyword.
