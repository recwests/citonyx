## Summary
Across 66.6 million logged-out GPT-5.6 Luna responses in August and September 2026, only 14.4% triggered a web search, and those averaged about 1.6 fan-out queries. For most prompts, visibility depends on what the model already knows about the brand rather than on pages it can retrieve that day.

## Details
Ahrefs analysed 66.6 million free, logged-out responses and 15 million resulting fan-out queries.

- Free sessions are the right proxy because more than 90% of weekly ChatGPT users are on the free plan.
- Caveat: studies using paid or reasoning models report 7.6 fan-outs per prompt, or two-plus fan-outs in 56.5% of prompts, so retrieval rate is model- and tier-specific.
- Practical consequence: brand familiarity and consistent entity facts matter as much as crawlable pages, and retrieval-only audits will misread most ChatGPT traffic.
