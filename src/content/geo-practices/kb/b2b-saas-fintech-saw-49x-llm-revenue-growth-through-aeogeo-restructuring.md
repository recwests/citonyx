## Summary
Documented B2B GEO case studies show 49x LLM revenue growth (B2B tech), 13x LLM revenue (retail), 8x conversions from LLMs (fintech). The citability checklist: clear definitions in opening sentences, data in tables, explicit claims with named sources, modular content blocks. Consistency across pages defining what the brand does, who it helps, and why matters.

## Details
Key GEO tactics that work: answer-first writing (2-3 sentence direct answers at section start), semantic chunking making each section independently citable, original data and statistics heavily favored, expert attribution with verifiable credentials, multi-format coverage. What underperforms: opinion pieces without factual backing, generic case studies lacking quantitative data, press releases without unique info. Content velocity + semantic structure matters more than comprehensive depth.
