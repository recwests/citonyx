## Summary

Brand web mentions show a 0.664 Spearman correlation with AI Overview visibility vs 0.218 for backlinks — off-site brand presence matters 3x more than link building for AI citations.

## Details

Ahrefs analyzed 75,000 brands using Spearman correlation. Branded web mentions (0.664), branded anchors (0.527), and branded search volume (0.392) are the top 3 factors — all off-site. Domain Rating (0.326), referring domains (0.295), and backlinks (0.218) show moderate-to-weak correlations. The top 25% of brands by web mentions average 169 AI Overview mentions — 10x more than the next quartile (14). 26% of brands have zero AI Overview mentions.
