## Summary

Global AI agents market hit $10.91B in 2026, projected $50.31B by 2030 at 45.8% CAGR. 68% of SEO teams now use AI agents. Multi-agent systems outperform single-agent by 90.2% on complex tasks. Marketing teams using AI agents report 73% faster campaign development and 68% shorter content creation timelines.

## Details

Key agentic SEO tools: SearchAtlas Orion (full agentic platform), Surfer AI (content optimization), Alli AI (bulk technical on-page), RankMath AI (WordPress). Agentic workflows defined by multi-step autonomous reasoning + tool use through standardized protocols (MCP, WebMCP, ACP). Prompt injection is the primary security threat (88% incident rate). Human-in-the-loop controls at critical decision points are main defense.
