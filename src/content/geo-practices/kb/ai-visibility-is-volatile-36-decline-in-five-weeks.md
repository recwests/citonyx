## Summary
AI visibility is volatile: a brand can lose a third of its AI presence in a month. Superlines tracked brand visibility down 35.9%, citation rate -34.4% and share of voice -34.8% over just five weeks (Jan 11 - Feb 8, 2026). Quarterly audits cannot catch this — weekly monitoring is the new minimum.

## Details
- Source: Superlines original tracking (Jan 11 - Feb 8, 2026).
- AirOps/Kevin Indig's 2026 State of AI Search (45,000 citations) found only ~30% of brands maintain consistent visibility from one AI answer session to the next.
- Track citation rate, mention rate, share of voice and sentiment per platform; correlate drops with model updates, content refresh and competitor moves.
