## Summary
Google's Top Stories carousel inside AI Overviews went fully live for US mobile users on developing-topic queries (July 17, 2026, per Search Engine Land). It highlights a reader's preferred sources and hands over plain article links instead of a summary, potentially improving clicks to news publishers — though Search Console still reports only impressions.

## Details
This is one of the few spots where an AI Overview hands over ordinary article links, which Search Engine Land reads as something that can improve clicks to news publishers. It is the productized version of the developing-topics carousel announced May 27, 2026, first spotted in testing in late June, with more regions planned. Impact is unmeasured because Search Console reports impressions, not clicks, for AI features, so publishers cannot isolate what the carousel sends them.
