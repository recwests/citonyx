## Summary
Yozigo recorded 37,176 answers across six engines from 4-10 October 2026. ChatGPT cited a source in 96.2% of answers with a median 39 sources and 618 words; Perplexity cited in 100% with 15 sources; Google AI Overviews gave the shortest answers at 248 words and named the fewest brands, 8.3.

## Details
Copilot named the second-most brands, 14.7, and showed a visible search in 99.9% of answers; Perplexity named the most brands, 16.1, but showed a search in only 27%. Most-cited domains diverge sharply: ChatGPT leaned on github.com (9.5%) and reuters.com (9.3%), while AI Overviews leaned on linkedin.com (31.8%) and youtube.com (29.3%). Google AI Mode and Copilot samples covered only two brands.
