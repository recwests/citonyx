## Summary
The Loganix synthesis of six studies covering 680M citations found AI search traffic converts at 14.2% compared to Google organic 2.8% — a 5.1x advantage. By platform: Claude 16.8%, ChatGPT 14.2%, Perplexity 12.4%. ConvertMate 2026 GEO benchmark reports 4.4x higher conversion from AI traffic. AI-referred visitors arrive with higher purchase intent from synthesized, comparison-rich answers.

## Details
- Seer Interactive: ChatGPT referrals convert at 15.9%, Perplexity 10.5%, Claude 5%. ChatGPT referrals spend 15 min/site vs 8 from Google, 12 pageviews vs 9.
- SimilarWeb measures ChatGPT conversion at 7.1% (likely different methodology/conversion definition).
- Conductor 2026 Benchmarks: AI referral traffic = 1.08% of all site traffic, growing ~1% month over month, with ChatGPT driving 87.4%.
- Caveat: Schulze-Kaiser 12-month study (973 sites, ~$20B revenue) found ChatGPT referrals underperform on transactional purchases by ~13%; overperform in B2B SaaS, finance, electronics.
- For B2B SaaS, AI-referred leads show 40% higher lifetime value per HubSpot 2026.
