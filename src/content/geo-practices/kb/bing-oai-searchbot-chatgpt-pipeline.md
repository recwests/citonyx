## Summary
ChatGPT Search draws live results from Bing's index, not Google's: Seer Interactive found 87% of ChatGPT Search citations matched Bing's top organic results, and Mersel AI 2026 confirms 87% of cited pages correspond to Bing top results. If a page isn't in Bing Webmaster Tools, ChatGPT cannot cite it regardless of Google ranking — a cheap technical win most SEO teams still overlook.

## Details
- Four-gate funnel: Bing index → OAI-SearchBot access → retrieval → citation selection; only ~15% of eligible pages get quoted.
- SE Ranking: 43.2% of ChatGPT citations go to Google's #1 ranking page — Google top-10 positions still matter for commercial queries.
- Bing Webmaster Tools offers a free AI Performance dashboard (public preview Feb 2026) showing actual citation events, cited URLs and grounding sub-queries — the only first-party AI-citation analytics tool.
- IndexNow (Bing/Yandex/Naver) pushes new/updated URLs into Bing within minutes, so fresh content can reach ChatGPT in 1-3 weeks; keep pages server-rendered and indexable.
- Caveat: retrieval architecture shifts — GPT-5.5 changed citation behavior (Michael Patrick Cortez analysis of 353K pages) — re-audit quarterly.
