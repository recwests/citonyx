## Summary
AI engines match a query to a heading that signals what sits beneath it; a clever but opaque heading like 'Say Hello' gives a model nothing to match. LovedByAI's optimizer rewrote headings as the questions they answer, lifting clarity scores from an average of 5.12 to 8.87 across 660,000+ changes (2026). The rewrites add no information — they make existing information addressable.

## Details
- Example rewrites: 'Say Hello' became 'What is the phone number for the studio?' and 'Step 1' became 'What is the first step to start your financial independence planning?'.
- Attribution caveat: headings ship together with JSON-LD, answer-first copy, FAQ and llms.txt, so the standalone effect is not isolated — but headings are the only change scored individually.
- Start with money-page headings that would mean nothing to someone who cannot see the page.
