## Summary
Profound's 2026 analysis of 240M citations: median cited-source half-life is ~4.5 weeks. 40-60% of cited domains rotate month-to-month for identical queries. 70-90% rotate over six months. Foundation Inc: AI citations have 11-15 day shelf life. Earned content decays ~2× slower than single-site content.

## Details
This is the most counter-intuitive finding in GEO: won citations are not permanent assets. A citation earned today is statistically more likely than not to be gone within two months unless the underlying content stays fresh. The replacement game means AI answers have a fixed number of seats — when a new brand enters, another exits. No growing pie. Monthly monitoring is the minimum cadence for any active GEO program.
