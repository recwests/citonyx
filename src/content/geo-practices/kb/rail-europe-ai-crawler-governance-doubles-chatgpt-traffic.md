## Summary

Rail Europe doubled ChatGPT referral traffic by refining bot governance rules to prioritize AI crawlers like GPT, reducing crawl waste from Bing, and shifting from volume-led to value-led SEO. Google crawl coverage went from 22% to 67%.

## Details

Rail Europe, a travel operator with hundreds of thousands of journey pages, partnered with Botify to build AI Readiness. Key moves: (1) curated sitemap strategy — high-value pages strongly linked and included, low-value pages removed from sitemaps; (2) bot governance — prioritized AI crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot) while reducing Bing crawl waste; (3) expanded structured data across the platform; (4) redesigned internal linking to create broader networks between key page types. Results: pages crawled +61%, pages indexable +66%, pages ranked +38%, pages clicked +33%, Google crawl coverage 22%→67%, ChatGPT traffic 2x.
