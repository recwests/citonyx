## Summary

On April 23, 2026, OpenAI released GPT-5.5. SISTRIX tracked 3.8M German-language ChatGPT responses and found a 47% shift in cited domain distribution within 48 hours — magnitude of a Google Core Update. Average sources/response dropped from 30.9 to 28.4. Model now cross-references domain authority signals before citing. Thin content and volume strategies stopped working; author credibility and external validation became primary signals.

## Details

GPT-5.5 revealed a structural recalibration: it cites differently, not less. Authority concentration emerged as the key pattern — established institutional sources (government, academic, B2B platforms with deep vertical expertise) gained citations. Sites that relied on volume over authority absorbed the hit. Writesonic's controlled comparison (50 identical prompts across GPT-5.4 and 5.5) confirmed cross-platform mentions became a key citation signal. Paid-tier ChatGPT cites brand websites 56% of the time vs 8% for free tier.
