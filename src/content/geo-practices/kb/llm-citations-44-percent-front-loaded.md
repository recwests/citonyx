## Summary
Analysis of 1.2M AI answers and 18,012 verified citations (matched to source sentences with sentence-transformer embeddings) found a consistent 'ski ramp' pattern: 44.2% of citations come from the first 30% of content, 31.1% from middle, 24.7% from final third (Kevin Indig, Search Engine Land, Feb 2026). Cited content was 2x more likely to include question marks, 78.4% of citations came from headings.

## Details
Kevin Indig's team used sentence-transformer embeddings to match ChatGPT responses to 18,012 verified source sentences. Cited passages used definitive language at nearly 2x the rate of non-cited. Conversational Q&A format was heavily favored. At paragraph level: 53% from middle, 24.5% from first sentences, 22.5% from last sentences. Heavily cited content averages 20.6% proper nouns (vs 5-8% in typical English) and Flesch-Kincaid grade 16 vs 19.1 for lower performers. Practical implication: lead pages with a plain, answer-first definition; structured briefing-style sections outperform narrative 'ultimate guide' writing.
