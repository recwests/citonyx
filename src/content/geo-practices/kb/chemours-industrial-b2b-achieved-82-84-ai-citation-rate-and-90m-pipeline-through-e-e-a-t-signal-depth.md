## Summary

Chemours partnered with an ABM agency to optimize for AI citations using deep E-E-A-T signals: content authored by recognized industry experts with visible credentials, technical papers citing patents and peer-reviewed research, and authoritative backlink profiles from industry-specific publications. Result: 82-84% AI citation rate across target queries, over $90M in pipeline attributed to AI-assisted discovery. A case proving enterprise GEO works even in narrow, highly technical B2B verticals. (Source: Stackmatix March 2026)

## Details

- Chemours (industrial chemicals): 82-84% AI citation rate across target query set.
- Pipeline attributed to AI-assisted discovery: $90M+.
- Methodology: deep E-E-A-T signal optimization — expert-author credentials, patented technical papers, peer-reviewed citations, industry-specific backlink profiles.
- Why it matters: industrial B2B is a hardest-case test — narrow audience, highly specialized content.
- Caveat: single ABM agency case study with publication bias. Results may not generalize to less specialized industries or smaller budgets.
- Practical takeaway: even in highly technical verticals, systematic E-E-A-T signal building produces measurable AI visibility and pipeline.
