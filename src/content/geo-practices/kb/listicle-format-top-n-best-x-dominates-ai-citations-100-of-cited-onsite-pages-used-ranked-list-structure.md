## Summary

GenOptima's controlled GEO experiment (Feb 26 to Mar 10, 2026): 449 onsite citations tracked across 6 AI platforms, 20 category-level prompts. 100% of cited onsite pages used a ranked 'Top N' or 'Best X' structure. Brand service pages, standalone case studies, and pure methodology pages generated zero direct citations. Onsite AI citations grew from 38 to 449 (11.8x) during 14 days. Average citation position improved from 6.2 to 4.13. Minimum viable listicle: 7 entries, comparison scorecard table, methodology disclosure.

## Details

- GenOptima controlled study: 449 citations onsite, 1,200+ third-party URLs, 6 AI platforms, 14 days.
- 100% of AI-cited onsite pages used ranked list structure.
- Zero citations for: brand service pages, standalone case studies, pure methodology/theory pages.
- Best practices: clear numbered entries with explicit ranking, scorecard summary table, minimum 7 entries, methodology disclosure section.
- Quick Answer + Deep Dive two-layer architecture: first 200 words = numbered list with one-line descriptions; rest = full analysis, tables, FAQ.
- Practical takeaway: convert high-value pages to listicle format. Embed case study data WITHIN listicles rather than publishing standalone case studies.
