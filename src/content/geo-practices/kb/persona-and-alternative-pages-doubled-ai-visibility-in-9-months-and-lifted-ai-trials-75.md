## Summary
Grow and Convert mapped a global B2B SaaS client's verticals, features, jobs-to-be-done and pains, then published persona pages for low-or-zero-volume long-tail keywords plus customer case studies with before-and-after detail. AI visibility more than doubled between January and September 2026, and AI-attributed free trials rose about 75%.

## Details
- AI traffic rose about 1,900% overall and 750% to articles over the same period; coverage went from visibility on about 100 tracked topics in January to nearly every tracked topic by September.
- Stability, not only level: on one vertical prompt the brand appeared in 82-91% of runs across six surfaces and in each of the last 12 weekly ChatGPT runs; brand stability scored 79 against a 30 all-brands baseline.
- Citation strength was highest on competitor-pricing and jobs-to-be-done prompts (93-100% of runs on several engines).
- Caveat: agency case study about its own client and its own tracking tool, no control group, and AI traffic undercounts users who re-Google the brand instead of clicking.
