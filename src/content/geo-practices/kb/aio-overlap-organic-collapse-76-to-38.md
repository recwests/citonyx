## Summary
AI Overview citations have decoupled from Google top-10 rankings. Across 680 million tracked citations (CiteMetrix), the share of AI Overviews' cited URLs that also rank in Google's top 10 fell from 76% to 38% in a single year. AI Mode appears in 25-30% of US desktop Google searches as of late 2025 (up from ~13%), cites sources 84.9% of the time and mentions brands in 61% of answers. Being #1 in Google no longer guarantees AI citation.

## Details
The 38 percentage point collapse represents the fastest decoupling of traditional and AI search metrics ever documented. The mechanism: AI Overviews use query fan-out (8-16 sub-queries per query), naturally diversifying sources beyond what traditional ranking serves. Pages ranking for underlying fan-out queries see 161% higher citation odds (AirOps). AI Overviews and AI Mode share only 13.7% of their cited URLs even within the same company. Ahrefs updated analysis (863K keywords, 4M AIO URLs, July 2026): only 38% of AI Overview citations come from top-10 organic results; positions 11-100 account for 31.2%, beyond rank 100 another 31%. DerivateX study (100 software-buying queries, 1,259 citations, June-July 2026) confirmed 35% source overlap — YouTube cited in 51/100 AIO queries but ranked in only 7, while Reddit ranked in 82 but cited in only 43; 63.4% of AIO citations came from third-party listicles. Google's May 2026 Core Update prioritized 'content structure and extractability signals', correlating with a 34% increase in citations from positions 11-50. The strategic implication: citation share and organic rank are distinct KPIs with only 0.34 correlation.
