## Summary
Community and UGC sources supplied 25.9% of Google AI Overview citations for SaaS in October 2026, but only 0.2% to 3.9% of citations inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. ClearBrand's 453-keyword, 400-answer study shows AI Overviews and chat engines draw from materially different source pools.

## Details
- ClearBrand ran 453 US SaaS buyer-intent keywords on Google plus 100 buyer prompts on each of ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude on 12-13 October 2026.
- 1,045 domains appeared in AI Overviews; chat engines cited 1,213 distinct domains, of which only 409 also appeared in AI Overviews, leaving 804 chat-only.
- Practical read: keep two target lists, UGC and review-heavy for AI Overviews, vendor and review-directory heavy for chat engines, instead of one blended outreach plan.
- Caveat: rule-based source classifier, one run per prompt, agency-published study.
