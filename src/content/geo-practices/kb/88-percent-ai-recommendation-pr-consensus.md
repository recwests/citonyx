## Summary
Industrial lighting brand Amico achieved 88.6% AI recommendation rate across Google AI Overview, Gemini, Perplexity, and Copilot. Key insight: brand website contributed only 4.5% of citations — PR reprints (barchart.com 59.8%) and retail listings (Home Depot, Amazon, Lowe's combined 101.4%) drove recommendations, not the own site.

## Details
Amico had zero AI presence despite strong Amazon/Home Depot listings. GenOptima tracked 350 AI responses across AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Mode and Copilot on 10 commercial buying prompts (Dec 2025 - Mar 2026). Cross-platform consensus from PR reprints and retail listings outperformed website optimization alone. Per model: Google AI Overview 100%, Gemini 97%, Perplexity 94%, AI Mode 91%, Copilot 60%. Average recommendation position was 1.05. Caveats: single ecommerce category, vendor-led program, and PR reprint distribution can look like link schemes if done poorly.
