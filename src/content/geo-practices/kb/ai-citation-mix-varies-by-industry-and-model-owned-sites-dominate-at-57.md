## Summary
AI citation sources are not monolithic — the mix shifts sharply by industry and model. Profound analyzed 11.8 billion citations across 29 industries and 8 engines: 57% come from brand sites overall. Gemini cites brand sites 69% of the time versus ChatGPT at 47%; pharma earns 59% of citations from earned media, SaaS only 11.4%.

## Details
Profound study (30 Jul 2026): 11.84 billion citations, 29 industries, 8 models, April 16-July 16, 2026; 3.02M-domain classification map covers 98.3% of citation volume. Buckets: brand (company-owned sites) ~57% globally; earned (editorial/PR) and social (Reddit, YouTube) fill the rest. Model split: Gemini 69% brand-site citations (most), ChatGPT 47% (least); AI Overviews earned-media share lowest at 17.0%. Industry extremes: cybersecurity 74% brand share, government/nonprofit 15.9%; pharma earned-media 59%, SaaS 11.4%. Action: benchmark your brand's earned/brand/social mix against your industry median, then push the under-weighted bucket.
