## Summary
Rankability's August 2026 snapshot of 1,645 AI citations found topic coverage, not length or exact-match keywords, predicts AI citation. Pages covering a topic thoroughly hit a 27.6% top-10 citation rate versus 14.5% for thin pages, and introducing the topic within the first 100 words lifted the rate to 22.1%.

## Details
- Rankability analysed 1,645 AI query-page observations and 916 traditional results across 31 topics (August 2026 snapshot; rankability.com).
- High-coverage pages had a 27.6% top-10 AI citation rate versus 14.5% for low-coverage pages, and appeared on 2.16 platforms versus 1.28. Introducing the topic within 100 words gave a 22.1% rate versus 8.7%.
- Median cited-page length was 2,681 words, yet top-10 rates stayed between 17.0% and 20.7% across six length groups, so extra words added little.
- Only 4.8% of citations came from pages repeating the full query in the title. Caveat: observational, and 24 of 31 queries contained "best" or "top".
