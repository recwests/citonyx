## Summary
Zenith's ChatGPT citation study (via Averi) shows ChatGPT favors direct sources over intermediaries for non-branded B2B queries: competitor sites +11.1 points vs Google, own website +3.0, encyclopedic +3.1, while industry publications -4.8 and discussion forums -7.9. Product docs and comparison pages beat third-party commentary.

## Details
Analysis of ChatGPT's citation patterns for non-branded B2B queries. Direct-source premium: competitor websites 11.1 points higher citation rate than Google baseline, company's own website +3.0, encyclopedic +3.1, academic +1.4; industry publications -4.8 and discussion forums -7.9, both declining as primary sources. Interpretation: ChatGPT prefers getting information from the source or objective factual content rather than intermediary commentators. For B2B SaaS that means product documentation, comparison pages and official guides matter more than third-party reviews. Caveat: single-vendor study (Zenith) summarized by Averi; query-class limited; treat as directional signal.
