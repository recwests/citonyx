## Summary
Foglift's 2026 research report (29,274 successful AI answers, Mar-Aug 2026) found 79.3% of answers include at least one surfaced citation, averaging 5.82. Per-engine citation density varies about 4x: Perplexity surfaces 9.04 citations per answer, Google AI Overviews 7.27, Gemini 7.17, ChatGPT 2.97 and Claude 2.34. Citation inclusion rates: ChatGPT 89.1%, Perplexity 86.2%, AI Overviews 82.3%, Gemini 77.3%, Claude 61.6%.

## Details
- Each source query produced 26.91 query fan-outs on average; youtube.com was the most-cited domain at just 3% of 170,351 citations — a heavily distributed source pool.
- Crawler mix (60-day window): 53.3% training, 32.5% indexing, 14.2% user-triggered fetch.
- The 4x spread in citations per answer explains why blog-focused visibility is easiest to win on Perplexity (most slots) and hardest on Claude and ChatGPT.
