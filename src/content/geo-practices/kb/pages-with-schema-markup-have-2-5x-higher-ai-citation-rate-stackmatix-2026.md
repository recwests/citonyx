## Summary

Content with proper schema markup has 2.5x higher chance of appearing in AI-generated answers. Sites with complete Tier 1 schema see up to 40% more AI Overview appearances. FAQPage schema alone improves AI citation rates by 30%. 65% of pages cited by Google AI Mode include structured data, and 71% of ChatGPT-cited pages include it.

## Details

Tier 1 schema for AI: FAQPage (30% citation boost), HowTo, Article, Organization. The March 2026 Google update shifted schema purpose from SERP display trigger to AI trust/entity verification signal. Organization + SameAs is now the highest-leverage type for AI Mode citation rates. JSON-LD is the preferred format. Tier 1 schema types alone generate 3:1 improvement in AI citation rate vs unstructured content. Entity graph connections (@id links between Article, Person, Organization) are critical.
