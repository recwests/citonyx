## Summary
Visiby's June 2026 benchmark (2,443 prompt-runs, 7 brands, 3 engines) put the median brand citation rate at 11% on ChatGPT, 9% on Perplexity, and 4% on Google AI Overviews, with the same brand's rate swinging by up to 24 points between engines. On 11% of shared prompts, engines cited no common domain at all.

## Details
Source preferences invert by engine: Reddit appeared in 45% of ChatGPT answers but 0% of Perplexity's, while YouTube appeared in 66% of Perplexity answers and 0.6% of ChatGPT's. Domain authority is not decisive: tryprofound.com scored 10/40 on Visiby's CITE-40 rubric yet was cited in 27% of prompts, out-citing higher-authority domains. Practical takeaway: track all engines, match content format to the engine you are losing (video for Perplexity, community answers for ChatGPT), and win the exact question before chasing authority.
