## Summary

ConvertMate GEO Benchmark 2026 (12,500+ queries, 8,000 domains, corroborated by BrightEdge, Semrush, and Growth Memo): pages above 20,000 characters average 10.18 AI citations vs 2.39 for pages under 500 characters (4.3x). Comparison articles account for 32.5% of all AI citations — the highest-performing content type.

## Details

- ConvertMate GEO Benchmark 2026 (12,500+ queries, 8,000 domains): >20K chars = 10.18 avg citations, <500 chars = 2.39 avg (4.3x difference).
- Comparison articles = 32.5% of all AI citations — best-performing content type (Digital Agency Network/Previsible 2025).
- Stat-dense content visibility advantage: 30-40% higher over unoptimized (Princeton KDD 2024, corroborated by Similarweb).
- Flat heading hierarchies hurt: only 11% of AI-cited pages use flat H1→Paragraph structure (Foundation Marketing, March 2026).
- Practical takeaway: create comprehensive comparison guides and data-backed pillar pages rather than thin top-funnel content.
