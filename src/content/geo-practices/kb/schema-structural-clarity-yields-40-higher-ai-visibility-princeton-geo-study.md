## Summary
Pages with nested schema AND structural clarity — structured lists, statistics, direct quotes and prose aligned with the markup — achieve 30-40% higher visibility in AI answers. Princeton's KDD-2024 GEO paper and a 2026 AlphaSol Labs trend analysis report this range; Wellows observed a +73% correlation between structured-data presence and AI citation rate.

## Details
- Princeton (Aggarwal et al., KDD-2024): schema divorced from aligned prose has limited citation impact; prose organized to confirm schema claims is the underexplored half.
- AlphaSol Labs 15 AI Search Trends (2026) and ConvertMate GEO Benchmark 2026: pages with structured lists, statistics, quotes and schema outperform unstructured prose by 30-40%.
- FAQ rich results were retired from the SERP on May 7 2026, but FAQPage markup remains useful for AI indexing.
- Practical: JSON-LD with Organization, Article, FAQPage, HowTo and BreadcrumbList; answer-first blocks; run pre/post GEO audits to quantify impact (Signal & Noise reports +15-25 score points).
- Misaligned implementation (FAQ schema with answers not visible in text) reduces citation probability; entity graph links (@id) are critical but often deferred.
