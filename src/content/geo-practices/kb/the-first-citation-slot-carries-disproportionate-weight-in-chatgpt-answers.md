## Summary
Citation position matters: the first ChatGPT citation captures the baseline (100) of relative click-through, the second 68, the third 44 and the fifth 13. Presenc derived these from 5,200 ChatGPT Search responses — earning the first slot is worth roughly 7x the fifth slot.

## Details
- ChatGPT cites fewer sources per answer than Perplexity (4.1 vs 5.8) but each citation carries proportionally more synthesis weight.
- Target the top citation slot for maximum CTR impact; later citations suffer severe attention decay.
- Citation position data is relative CTR from Presenc's Q1 2026 monitoring of 5,200 ChatGPT Search responses.
- Combine with first-third-of-page content placement (44% of citations) to maximize the chance your page earns the top slot.
