## Summary
In July 2026 Google began surfacing AI visibility impression data in Search Console (GenAI performance reports launched June 3, 2026, now becoming widely available) and Bing Webmaster Tools added an AI Citation Share metric — the first native windows into AI citations, closing the measurement gap that made AI search strategy speculative and opening competitive share-of-voice analysis for the first time.

## Details
- GPO (July 13, 2026) reports Search Console's AI reporting is impression-only for now (AI Overviews and AI Mode impressions, segmentable by page, country, device and date; excludes queries, clicks, CTR and position); Bing's AI Citation Share frames visibility as share-of-voice and enables competitive analysis per grounding query.
- Same period: 68% of US Google searches ended without a click in early 2026, up from 60% in 2024.
- Related July moves: Google connected Google Business Profile data into GA4, and Lighthouse began surfacing agentic-readiness checks (whether AI agents can parse and act on a site).
- Counting semantics differ from classic search: at property level two URLs from one site in one answer count as one impression; in AI Overviews a link must be scrolled or expanded into view to count; in AI Mode a follow-up question is a new query.
- Diagnostic use: high organic with low AI visibility often means the page does not give a clean answer (hidden in tabs or JavaScript, indirect headings); modest organic with high AI visibility points to useful definitions, statistics or comparisons worth studying.
- Caveat: tools are early (impressions, not clicks); use them as a baseline layer, anchor reporting on qualified traffic and conversions, and keep tracking separately per platform.
