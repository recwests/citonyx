## Summary
SOCi's 2026 Local Visibility Index analysed 350,000 locations across 2,751 multi-location brands. ChatGPT recommended just 1.2% of locations, Gemini 11.0% and Perplexity 7.4%, against a 35.9% appearance rate in Google's local 3-Pack, making AI local visibility roughly 30 times more selective than traditional local search.

## Details
- SOCi's 2026 Local Visibility Index reviewed about 350,000 US multi-location brand locations across 2,751 brands in January 2026, asking ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to recommend businesses for a category and market, counting a location only when it appeared in the first five results (soci.ai via PRNewswire, January 28 2026).
- Recommended locations averaged 4.3 stars for ChatGPT. Industry splits vary: 3.1% of financial-services locations were recommended by ChatGPT versus 16.4% by Gemini.
- Caveat: vendor-run study, and profile-data quality (68.3% accurate on ChatGPT) is both a driver and a limitation.
