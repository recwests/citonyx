## Summary

At Google I/O 2026, AI Overviews and AI Mode merged into a single unified AI Search experience running on Gemini 3.5 Flash. Previously separate citation pools are now unified. AI Mode surpassed 1B monthly users. The unified surface produces faster, longer, more citation-rich answers than prior Gemini 1.5 Pro default.

## Details

March 2026 Core Update made AI Overviews the default search experience for logged-in users. Google Search Console now reports AI Overview and AI Mode as separate 'Search Appearance' filters. Citation behavior favors structured, authoritative, frequently updated content. AI Mode impressions ≠ visits — an 'impression' means your page was cited, not visited. Google claims AI referrals have strong engagement (8% longer stays, 12% more pages, 23% less bounce).
