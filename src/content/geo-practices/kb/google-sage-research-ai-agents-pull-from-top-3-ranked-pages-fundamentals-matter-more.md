## Summary

Google's SAGE research paper (Jan 26, 2026) shows how AI agents are trained for multi-step search tasks averaging 4.9 steps (up to 7). AI agents pull from top 3 ranked pages per query. Content comprehensiveness, clear structure, and strong internal linking are the three pillars of agentic search readiness. Mean task completion rate across platforms: 75.3%.
