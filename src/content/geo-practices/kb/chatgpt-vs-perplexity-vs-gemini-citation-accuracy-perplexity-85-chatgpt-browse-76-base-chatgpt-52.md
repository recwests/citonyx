## Summary
Talkory.ai (May 2026, 50 factual queries, 14 hours of manual verification): Perplexity leads at 85% citation accuracy (URL resolves AND source supports claim). ChatGPT with Browse scores 76%, base ChatGPT without browse only 52%. Base model fabricates 30-40% of citations.

## Details
- For brands pursuing citation accuracy (rather than just citation frequency): Perplexity is the most reliable citation engine for accurate source attribution, with an 89% valid-URL rate and 85% source-supports-claim.
- ChatGPT without browsing is unreliable for citation-dependent queries — critical for factual/medical/financial content.
- Browsing materially helps: ChatGPT's fabrication drops from 30-40% to 8-12% with the Browse tool enabled.
- Gemini with Search Grounding scores 80% accuracy, ungrounded Gemini 55%.
- Legal citations were alarmingly low across all models (>40% error rate).
- Brands should ensure their content is structured for accurate AI extraction to minimize hallucination risk.
