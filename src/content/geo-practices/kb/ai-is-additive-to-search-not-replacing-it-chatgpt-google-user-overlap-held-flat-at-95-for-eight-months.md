## Summary
Similarweb's 2026 Generative AI Landscape data shows worldwide gen-AI web visits hit 9.5 billion a month (+70% YoY) and app downloads 4.4 billion (+58%), yet the share of ChatGPT users who also use Google stayed flat at 95% from September 2025 to May 2026. Users are adding AI to search habits, not swapping them; growth and substitution produce opposite signatures in this overlap number.

## Details
- ChatGPT's share of worldwide gen-AI web traffic fell from about 76% to 53% as Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and DeepSeek grew — redistribution within AI, not a shift away from search.
- Gemini app MAU rose from about 401M (Jun 2025) to 716M (May 2026).
- Caveats: overlap measures same-user presence, not query-level substitution, and the report excludes API, desktop and in-app usage where substitution would be likeliest.
- Source: Similarweb (Aug 2026).
