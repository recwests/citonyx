## Summary
3RD tracked 111 brands across 732,359 AI answers from 19 September to 18 October 2026: the median brand's daily AI visibility moved 12.5 points between its best and worst day with no prompt change. Typical day-to-day movement was 2.1 points, so weekly dips are mostly noise.

## Details
- Median 30-day mean visibility was 19.6% (middle half of brands 8.7%-39.6%); the largest single-day gap from that mean was 7.2 points.
- Prompt-set size drives the noise: brands collecting 24-94 answers a day swung 16.7 points, versus 8.7 points at 230+ answers a day.
- Practice: judge trends on 30-day windows, treat sub-5-point moves as noise unless they persist for weeks, and aim for 100+ answers a day for a readable daily line.
- Caveat: observational vendor data, sample leans Nordic, no control, and model-version switches break comparability.
