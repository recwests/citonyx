## Summary
Minty Orange stored the Google position at optimization time for 95 articles. Every one of the 14 articles already ranking in Google's top 10 earned an AI citation after optimizing; articles at positions 11-30 converted at 48%; articles below 30 at 26%. The middle tier — articles around position 15 with solid content — is the best optimization candidate. Existing Google authority and AI citations pull in the same direction.

## Details
- The top-10 group is small (n=14), so the 100% is a strong signal, not a law.
- Read together with Semrush's finding that top-10 pages appear in AI answers only 44.3% of the time — ranking helps but does not guarantee citation; structure and extractability still decide.
- Complements the finding that only about 12% of AI-cited URLs overlap a domain's own Google top-10 ranking.
- Source: Minty Orange (Aug 2026).
