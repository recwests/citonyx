## Summary
BrightEdge February 2026 data: B2B technology sector saw AI Overview trigger rate surge from 36% to 82% year-over-year (Feb 2025→Feb 2026). Education: 83%, Healthcare: 88%. For any B2B content site, the probability that a relevant query triggers an AI Overview approaches near-certainty in 2026.

## Details
- Industry variation: Healthcare 88%, Education 83%, B2B Tech 82% trigger rates. Finance lower at ~11% top-10 citation overlap (most divergent from organic).
- Entertainment grew from 3% to 19% top-10 overlap over the same period.
- Implication: B2B tech brands cannot afford to ignore GEO — 82% of their target queries now generate AI answers. Without GEO optimization, the brand is invisible for the majority of its addressable search landscape.
