## Summary

Geoperf's B2B fintech benchmark across 30 prompts and 4 LLMs: Stripe, Plaid, Brex consistently cited 60-85% of the time. Mid-market brands (Mercury, Ramp, Pilot) underindex despite strong tech press. Cumulative English editorial volume matters as much as recent press for LLMs. Well-sourced English Wikipedia articles systematically over-represent brands in AI citations.

## Details

US Asset Management benchmark (14 brands): BlackRock 88%, Vanguard 74%, Fidelity 61%. Long tail (Charles Schwab, T. Rowe Price) plateau at 25-40%. Key differentiator: well-sourced Wikipedia + regular coverage in WSJ, Bloomberg, Pensions & Investments. US Digital Agencies: WPP and Publicis Sapient dominate (70-80%), followed by Huge, R/GA at 30-40%. Sector-specialized agencies rarely emerge without targeted prompts. For independent agencies, visible sector specialization is the #1 lever.
