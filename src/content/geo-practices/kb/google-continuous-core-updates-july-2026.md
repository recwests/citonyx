## Summary

Google confirmed on July 9, 2026 that smaller core updates now roll continuously without public announcements. The Search Status Dashboard now shows only large named updates (March, May 2026). Semrush and Sistrix detected elevated ranking movement through June–July outside any announced window.

## Details

The diagnostic framework: ranking drop vs AI Overview squeeze require different fixes. Ranking drop = rewrite for depth, sourcing, E-E-A-T. AI Overview squeeze = restructure for extractability (answer-first, comparison tables, FAQ schema). Google's May 2026 Search Central guidance: 'optimizing for generative AI features is part of search experience optimization.' Practical takeaway: switch from quarterly to weekly Search Console reviews. Separate AI visibility as a distinct KPI — a page can rank #3 on Google and be absent from ChatGPT.
