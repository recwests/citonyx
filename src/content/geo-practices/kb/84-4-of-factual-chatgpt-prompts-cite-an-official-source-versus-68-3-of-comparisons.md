## Summary
In a 20,000-prompt US test, 84.4% of factual prompts about pricing, features or company details cited at least one official source, against 68.3% of comparison prompts. Official sites supplied 63.4% of factual citations but only 46.4% of comparison citations, so comparisons are where third-party coverage decides the answer.

## Details
Ahrefs ran 20,000 US ChatGPT prompts and split results by question type.

- Factual questions resolve toward the owner of the fact; comparison questions resolve toward whoever has tested both options.
- Two implications: keep factual claims machine-legible on owned pages, and treat independent reviews and head-to-head tests as the acquisition surface for competitive queries.
- The study does not separate search-mode from parametric answers, so read the split as directional rather than as a per-query probability.
