## Summary

Everything-PR Research synthesis of Semrush's 325,000-prompt cross-engine study (Q1 2026) and Evertune's 200 million-prompt analysis found Perplexity is the only major AI engine where LinkedIn Company Pages outpace individual member posts (59/41 split). On ChatGPT and Google AI Mode, individual posts dominate at 59%. Perplexity's 5.3% LinkedIn citation rate is concentrated on brand-controlled Company Page content. (Source: Everything-PR June 2026, Semrush Q1 2026, Evertune)

## Details

- Semrush 325K-prompt cross-engine study (Q1 2026): Perplexity LinkedIn citations — 59% Company Pages, 41% individual posts.
- ChatGPT and Google AI Mode invert: 41% Company Pages, 59% individual posts.
- Perplexity overall LinkedIn citation rate: 5.3% of all citations.
- Perplexity brand citation rate is 13.05% vs ChatGPT 0.59% — a 22x gap that makes it the most brand-friendly AI surface.
- Practical takeaway: maintain a long-form structured content cadence on your LinkedIn Company Page with dated thought leadership and executive-quoted posts. This content is directly citeable by Perplexity.
