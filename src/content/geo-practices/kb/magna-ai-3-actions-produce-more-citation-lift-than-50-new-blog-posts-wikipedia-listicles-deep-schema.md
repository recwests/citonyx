## Summary
Magna AI's 2026 report across 150+ clients identifies three highest-leverage actions: (1) earn Wikipedia/Wikidata entry (compounds across every platform), (2) 90-day listicle inclusion campaign (each inclusion generates 5-15 downstream mentions), (3) deep schema across every commercial page — not just homepage. Together these outperform 50 new blog posts.

## Details
Magna AI runs monthly visibility probes across all clients using standardized prompt sets (40-80 per industry) across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. The rolling 12-month data through May 2026 shows citation frequency is concentrated: 54% of brands that rank well on Google are never cited by AI. 80% of products in AI Overviews do not rank in the top 10. HubSpot reports 60% of AI-cited content includes proprietary data or unique insights. Wikipedia matters specifically for ChatGPT (~12% of citations) but far less for Claude (0.1%) and Perplexity (0%).
