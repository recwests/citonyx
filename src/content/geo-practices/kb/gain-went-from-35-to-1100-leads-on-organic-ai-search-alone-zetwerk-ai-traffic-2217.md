## Summary
GAIN Servicing, a B2B company with 489 monthly visits and 35 leads, moved to 280 leads/month and 1,100+ campaign leads without paid acquisition through an 8-month integrated SEO+GEO program (Qoulomb). Zetwerk grew AI-sourced traffic 2,217% over 9 months; Elchemy's AI-cited pages rose 368 to 4,500+.

## Details
Qoulomb (India-based B2B GEO/SEO agency) documented: GAIN Servicing — competitive healthcare/legal-tech niche, no content strategy, no AI presence; over eight months monthly leads grew 35 to about 280 and organic became the primary channel with 1,100+ total leads and zero paid. Zetwerk — AI-sourced traffic +2,217% over nine months after a full-funnel SEO then GEO phase; US traffic share 10% to 38%. Elchemy — AI-cited pages 368 to 4,500+ and AI prompt rankings 18 to 9,000+ in five months. Caveat: agency press release, self-reported and not independently verified; figures may overstate due to tracking definitions. Use as directional proof that integrated SEO+GEO with explicit AI citation tracking can drive pipeline, not as a controlled result.
