## Summary
An SE Ranking experiment published 93 articles for a brand-new, zero-authority brand and tracked 165 questions daily across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Perplexity and Gemini. Articles published on other websites earned roughly ten times more AI citations than the brand's own site; visibility stayed near 1% for four months.

## Details
- SE Ranking launched a brand from zero reputation, no backlinks and no reviews, published 93 content pieces, then tracked 165 questions a day across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Perplexity and Gemini (AI SEO & GEO Online Summit, 7 October 2026).
- Articles placed on other websites earned about ten times more AI citations than the brand's own site, and visibility hovered near 1% for the first four months before climbing to roughly 8%.
- The transferable lesson: for new brands, third-party and community placements move AI visibility faster than owned content.
- Caveat: single branded experiment, conference presentation rather than peer review.
