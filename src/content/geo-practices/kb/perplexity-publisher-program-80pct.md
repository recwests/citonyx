## Summary
Unlike OpenAI's flat-fee licensing deals, Perplexity pays per usage when content is cited in an answer or via Comet Plus subscription split. Premium-tier citations count ~3x; quality multiplier up to +50% for entity density/recency.

## Details
Comet Plus launched formally in January 2026: a $5/month subscription that pools subscriber revenue and pays 80% to participating publishers (20% retained for compute) across three traffic types — human visits, search citations, and agent actions. This is the first model where a citation without a click contributes to payout. Initial pool $42.5M; enrollment exceeded 2,400 publishers by Q1 2026. Analyst estimates: ~$8–15 per 1,000 citations; mid-tier publisher ~$5k–15k/mo. Onboarding includes a content-quality + structured-data review; Perplexity can remove publishers for manipulation. Small publishers can join by emailing publishers@perplexity.ai. Implication: build quotable, structured content that earns citations — the same GEO work pays if citation-payment models spread.
