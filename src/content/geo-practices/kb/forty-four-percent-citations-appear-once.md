## Summary
First large-scale study of citation durability across 23M cited sources: 44% are one-hit wonders appearing in exactly one AI answer. Only durable citations compound into brand authority. Perplexity retains sources ~2x longer than ChatGPT.

## Details
Writesonic's index of 2B AI conversations tracked 23M cited sources across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and AI Overviews (Apr–Jun 2026). Median citation lifespan: 11-15 days. After the May 2026 model update, ChatGPT's share of citations to third-party sites jumped to 96%, but the average vendor site captured just 4.3%. In 90% of cases, when a new brand enters an AI answer, another brand gets dropped — fixed seats.

A second, independent confirmation (Pixel Tools, 14.57M citations across 1,155 projects on Alice AI, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, one year, Russian market) found 48.1% of external URLs appeared in only one snapshot and 83.3% in five or fewer, while domains stayed far more stable. Projects with rising visibility had a median core of 13 recurring domains against four; a domain counted as core when present in at least half of a project's snapshots. The brand's own site was a top-5 source for 64.0% of growing projects versus 32.7% of flat ones, and covering 80% of citations needed about 50 domains and 225 URLs. Both studies are observational with client-chosen prompts, so the takeaway is directional: durable citations, not one-off hits, are the KPI, and refresh cycles should be aligned with per-engine half-lives.
