## Summary
Agentic AI searches in 2026: autonomous AI agents from Claude and GPT-5 now execute multi-step purchasing and comparison tasks without human intervention. Content optimized for AI retrieval (structured specs, clear pricing, comparison tables) becomes critical vs persuasive narrative-driven content.

## Details
- The distinction between informational AI answers and autonomous agent execution is critical. Agent-first content must be machine-readable first, human-readable second.
- Practical shifts: product pages should expose pricing as structured data via Product schema, compatibility via sameAs links, and comparison data via ItemList.
- Agents favor pages with clear decision matrices and zero ambiguity in value propositions.
