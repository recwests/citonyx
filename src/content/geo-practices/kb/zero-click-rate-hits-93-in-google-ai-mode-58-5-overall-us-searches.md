## Summary

Zero-click rate in Google AI Mode is 93% (Semrush). Overall US zero-click searches hit 58.5% (SparkToro). AI Overviews reduce clicks by 34.5% to pages below them. Top organic result CTR drops 58% with AI Overview present.

## Details

Data from SparkToro, Ahrefs, Semrush, and Marketech APAC. Zero-click rate averaged 83% when AI Overviews appeared. AI Overviews now appear in ~50% of all search results per Google's own data (Marketech APAC, Feb 2026). Gartner predicted 25% traditional search volume drop by 2026. 94% of B2B buyers used generative AI during purchase (6sense). 68.94% of websites now receive some AI traffic (SE Ranking). For brands, optimizing for citation visibility becomes a primary KPI separate from click-through rate.
