## Summary
AI Overviews hit industries unevenly: BrightEdge February 2026 data shows B2B tech trigger rate surged from 36% to 82% year-over-year, while only ~4% of product searches trigger AIOs (SEO Discovery, July 30, 2026). Brands cited in AI answers earn ~120% more clicks per impression (Seer), and ~68% of US searches are zero-click.

## Details
- Industry variation: Healthcare 88%, Education 83%, B2B Tech 82% trigger rates; Finance lower at ~11% top-10 citation overlap (most divergent from organic); Entertainment grew from 3% to 19% top-10 overlap.
- Technology/software and publishing/media are most exposed; automotive, finance, and e-commerce transactional queries hold up because users still need prices, availability, and reviews.
- Seer Interactive data cited: organic CTR on AI Overview searches fell ~65% (1.76% to 0.61%) from mid-2024 to September 2025, recovering to ~2.4% by early 2026; cited brands earn ~120% more organic clicks per impression than uncited brands.
- Implication: B2B tech brands cannot afford to ignore GEO — ~82% of their target queries now generate AI answers, so without optimization the brand is invisible for most of its addressable search landscape.
- Recommended fix: publish original research, unique data, and expert insights AI cannot easily copy; restructure highest-impression/lowest-click pages to lead with a direct answer plus schema. Agency case: after answer-first restructuring, a client's content appeared in AI answers for eight target keywords and organic CTR rose within 90 days.
- Note: the 82% figure is consolidated from BrightEdge (Feb 2026) and SEO Discovery (Jul 30 2026) reporting; verify per-data-source.
