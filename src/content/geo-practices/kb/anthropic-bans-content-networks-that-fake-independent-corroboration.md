## Summary
Anthropic updated its Usage Policy, effective 12 November 2026, to ban using Claude to manipulate the sources search engines and AI systems draw answers from. Prohibited tactics include seeding content that misrepresents its origin, authorship or independence, and distributing material through networks of fake or ostensibly independent outlets to hide a common source.

## Details
This follows Google's May 2026 clarification that spam policies cover manipulation of generative AI responses, and matches practitioner warnings that paid mentions and self-promotional listicles are detectable.

- Practical effect: strategies built on synthetic third-party corroboration now carry platform-level risk, not just search risk.
- Legitimate substitutes such as first-party data, named authors and verifiable citations remain unaffected.
- Enforcement is likely to lag the written rule, so the policy change is a risk signal rather than an immediate deindexing event.
