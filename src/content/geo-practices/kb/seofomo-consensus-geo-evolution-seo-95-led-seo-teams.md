## Summary
SEOFOMO's 2026 Organic Search Trends survey (60+ SEO pros) and Aleyda Solis's poll (n=1,212) converge on one conclusion: 95% of AI search optimization is led by SEO teams, and GEO is an evolution of SEO skillsets, not a separate vertical. The consensus: 'AI SEO isn't a new vertical; it's an exaptation of the SEO skillset.' Teams need new KPIs (citation share, brand visibility), not new headcount.

## Details
Respondents pushed back against new acronyms (GEO, AISEO, AIO) marketed as entirely new strategies: 'AI search optimization is an evolution, not a new species' (SEOFOMO, May 2026). Budget reality: most respondents reported flat or reduced SEO budgets, with clients diverting spend to AI tools. 'Cutting foundational SEO to fund AI tools feels proactive, but it's usually just a budget reshuffle.' The biggest challenge of 2026 is not technical optimization but educating leadership about attribution model changes. Top performing teams are 'changing their KPIs, shifting their channel mix, and building AI capacity' — not just adding GEO tasks to existing SEO workflows. 86.6% of marketing leaders are prioritizing AI search, but only 43% got bigger budgets to hit higher targets. Implication: position GEO as an expansion of SEO, not a replacement — this framing gets budget buy-in more effectively than 'we need a new discipline.'
