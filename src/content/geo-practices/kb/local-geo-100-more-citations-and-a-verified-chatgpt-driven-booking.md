## Summary
Local businesses win AI visibility with consistent digital consensus: Valpo Agency doubled Plumbing Pilots' citations and cited pages (to 12 and 6) over six months via directories, geographic specificity and reputation signals. Brand mentions rose 28.6%, and a customer called to book an estimate saying they found the plumber on ChatGPT.

## Details
- LLM recommendations for local queries hinge on structured authority: consistent NAP across directories and geographic specificity (cities served) in content.
- Reputation signals carry weight: surfacing positive reviews and brand mentions in relevant contexts lifted brand mentions 28.6%.
- Direct ROI proof: a live customer booking arrived during a strategy call, explicitly attributed to ChatGPT.
- Vendor-published single-client case; treat the numbers as directional evidence for local GEO tactics.
