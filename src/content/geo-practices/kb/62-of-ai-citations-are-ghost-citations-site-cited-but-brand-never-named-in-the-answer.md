## Summary

Semrush + Kevin Indig study (June 2026, 3,981 domain appearances, 115 prompts, 14 countries, 4 engines): 61.7% of citations are 'ghost citations' — the site appears as a source link but the brand name never appears in the answer. Only 13.2% are both cited and mentioned. Gemini mentions brands 83.7% of the time (high mention, low citation). ChatGPT cites 87% of the time (low mention). Short conversational queries produce 30-50x more brand mentions than long structured prompts.

## Details

- 3,981 domain appearances tracked across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Google AI Mode, 14 countries.
- Three buckets: ghost citation (61.7%), both cited+mentioned (13.2%), mention without citation (25.1%).
- Per-engine: Gemini — 83.7% mention rate, 21.4% citation rate. ChatGPT — 20.7% mention, 87% citation.
- Comparative queries produce 2.4x more brand mentions than informational ones; short conversational prompts hit near-100% mention rates vs 2-3% for long structured prompts (30-50x difference).
- Practical takeaway: track citations AND mentions separately per engine — visibility in one does not transfer to another. Citations require authority, mentions require brand clarity.
