## Summary
ChatGPT strongly favors fresh content: an SE Ranking study found 95% of ChatGPT citations come from content published in the last 10 months, and pages with a visible 'last updated' timestamp receive 1.8x more citations. A quarterly refresh cycle with visible dates is now table stakes for AI visibility.

## Details
- Source: SE Ranking study 'What Drives AI Citations' (2.3M pages), summarized by AEO Vision.
- SE Ranking also found domain traffic is the strongest citation predictor (SHAP 0.63); high-traffic sites earn 3x more citations.
- Recency weighting is strongest on time-sensitive queries.
- Caveat: freshness complements rather than replaces authority — a stale high-authority page still beats a fresh low-authority one.
