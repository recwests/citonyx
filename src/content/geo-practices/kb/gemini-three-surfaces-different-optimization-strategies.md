## Summary

Gemini is not one thing — it powers three surfaces with different mechanics: the Gemini app (answers from training data, needs broad consistent web presence), AI Overviews (grounded in search results, needs classic SEO), and AI Mode (query fan-out, needs coverage across sub-questions). Visibility in one doesn't predict the others.

## Details

The Gemini app is a standalone assistant that answers mostly from training data and only searches the live web when grounding triggers — entity consistency and broad web presence matter most. AI Overviews synthesize from pages Google already ranked, so classic SEO fundamentals (crawlable HTML, clear headings, answer-first content) are the prerequisite. AI Mode adds query fan-out — splitting one question into several parallel sub-searches — so a cluster of narrower supporting pages outperforms one broad page. Google announced in May 2026 that AI Overviews and AI Mode are merging into a single AI Search experience.
