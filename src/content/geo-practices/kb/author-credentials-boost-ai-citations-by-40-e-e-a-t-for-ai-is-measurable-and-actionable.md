## Summary

Multiple 2026 studies converge: named authors with credentials + Person schema + linked bios produce 40% higher AI citation rates. AI citation architecture prioritizes: inline citations (48%), entity verification (31%), domain authority (12%) — inverse of Google. Google's March 2026 core update amplified 'Experience' signal above all others. Single fastest E-E-A-T fix: add named authorship with bios to highest-traffic pages.
