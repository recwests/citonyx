## Summary
ChatGPT citation decisions are driven by structure and recency more than authority: answer-first pages (direct answer in the first 100-150 words) earn 67% more citations, and content published within six months earns 2.8x more than pages older than a year. Texta tracked 50,000+ queries and 180,000+ citations across 1,000 websites.

## Details
- Answer-first structure is the single strongest predictor in the study (r=0.67, p<0.001); content under three months earns 182% more citations than 12-month-old content.
- FAQ pages earn 89% more citations than average; FAQPage schema adds +38% and well-implemented combined schema +47%.
- Credited authors with credentials receive 43% more citations than anonymous content.
- Original research and data studies earn 3.2x more citations than aggregated content (58.4 vs 18.5 citations/1K queries).
