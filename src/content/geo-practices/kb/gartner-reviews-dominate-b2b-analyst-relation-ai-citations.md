## Summary
Gartner Reviews captures 81.7% of analyst-relations-site AI citations in B2B. OtterlyAI's 2026 study of 1M+ cited URLs found 96% of Gartner citations come from its user-review product, not gated research reports, making an open Gartner Reviews listing an AI-citation source.

## Details
- OtterlyAI's Analyst Relations AI Search Study 2026 analyzed 1M+ URLs cited across major AI engines.
- Gartner review pages (gartner.com/reviews/) capture 81.7% of all analyst-relations-site citations.
- Critically, 96% of Gartner citations originate from the user-review product rather than gated reports, so the lever is an open Reviews listing, not a research subscription.
- Implication for B2B SaaS: a verified Gartner Reviews profile functions as a third-party citation source generative engines trust, alongside G2, Capterra and industry publications.
