## Summary
Glen Allsopp tracked over 70,000 ChatGPT and AI Overview responses across 1,300+ prompts daily for 28 days. Core brands changed just 11-13% day-to-day while tail brands churned 65-78%. Two random days matched exactly on brands only 0.3-1.1% of the time, so short monitoring windows mislead.

## Details
- Method: 70,000+ responses, 1,300+ commercial prompts checked daily in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews over four weeks (detailed.com, September 23 2026).
- Core brands, named on 80%+ of days, changed 13% (ChatGPT) and 11% (AI Overviews) day-to-day; the tail changed 78% and 65%. Cited domains behaved the same way: core 14%/12%, tail 74%/61%.
- The leading brand appeared on 82% (ChatGPT) and 89% (AI Overviews) of days, and the most-cited domain was cited first on only 43% and 28% of days.
- Caveat: one practitioner dataset, commercial prompts only.
