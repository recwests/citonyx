## Summary

SEO Vendor published 4 GEO case studies applying a 5-module pipeline (Retrieval → Candidate Selection → Citation → Summarization → Answer Dominance). Results: 50 event signups from 549 ChatGPT referral sessions (42.26% engagement) with near-zero Google organic; LLM-native visibility index climbed to ~380; 4-month sustained ChatGPT visibility through Jan-Feb 2026 volatility; SME pilot reached 100 on all brand metrics in 6 months.

## Details

The case studies validate the 'systems view of GEO' where SEO authority signals still influence RAG-based results but the full probability-engineering pipeline introduces metrics traditional SEO does not emphasize. Key takeaways: map every piece of content to the 5-module pipeline; score for extractability (lists, tables, fact boxes); build governance pages for citation safety; track LLM-native metrics alongside traditional rankings (Discovery → Confidence → Visibility sequence); avoid mass-content shortcuts as volatility patterns correlate with scaled spam detection. The SME pilot proved even smaller sites can achieve Fortune-500-level presence inside LLMs without massive organic traffic.
