## Summary
SEOFOMO's 2026 State of AI Search Optimization survey (171 respondents, 36 countries, Aug 17-27 2026) shows AI search optimization has gone mainstream: 98% of decision-makers asked about it (up from 91%), 69% have dedicated budgets (up from 38%), and 75% have a formal strategy (up from 51%). Yet only 23% feel confident measuring AI visibility — the measurement gap is the main bottleneck.

## Details
61% of respondents saw AI search investment grow during 2026; only 1% saw cuts (SEOFOMO, Aug 30 2026). 92% track AI visibility and citations (up from 78%), and the share saying nobody owns this work dropped from 11% to 4%. ChatGPT leads as the primary platform to optimize for, followed by Google AI surfaces and Gemini. The consensus: measurement and business attribution haven't kept up with investment — 'the biggest challenge right now' (Mark Williams-Cook, Candour). A parallel poll by Aleyda Solis (n=1,212) found 95% of AI search optimization is led by SEO teams, confirming this is an evolution of SEO skillsets, not a new vertical. Practical: start tracking AI visibility with prompt sets of 30-50 buyer-intent queries across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini; report per-engine, not blended.
