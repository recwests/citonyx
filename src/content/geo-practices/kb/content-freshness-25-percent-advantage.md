## Summary

Across 16.975M citations on 7 platforms, AI-cited URLs average 1,064 days old versus 1,432 for Google organic — 25.7% fresher (Ahrefs). ChatGPT shows the strongest freshness bias, citing URLs 393-458 days newer; AI Overviews is the exception at ~16 days older. The viral '4.3x more cited for fresh content' claim traces to no primary study. Freshness is a moderate factor, not a dominant one.

## Details

Ahrefs computed publication age of cited URLs from its crawler and Content Explorer databases; Perplexity and ChatGPT appear to order in-text references from newest to oldest. Caveats: the average cited page is still 2.9 years old, so AI favors long-lived content, and Google's John Mueller warns against fake date-only updates. Practical cadence: quarterly refresh of cornerstone pages with substantive new data and a visible 'last updated' date, then resubmit via Search Console and Bing WMT. Amsive (2026) found 50% of AI-cited content is under 13 weeks old. Fix crawlability and brand presence before refresh cadence.
