## Summary
Perplexity cites at least one source in 60.6% of its answers versus 32.6% for ChatGPT, 32.4% for Claude and 43.5% for Gemini, per ZenoX Media's study of 543 buying-intent answers across four engines (June-July 2026). About a third (34%) of citation losses happened because the AI never searched the web at all.

## Details
- 543 real answers to 193 commercial-intent queries, run in fresh consumer browser sessions (not APIs), June 13 - July 16 2026.
- Loss reasons on 265 tagged answers: 34% no web search at all (answered from memory), 23% page not retrieved, 17% forum consensus won (Reddit trusted over brand sites), 14% cited but not recommended.
- Practical split — two games: Perplexity and Gemini are retrieval games — win with fresh, crawlable, source-shaped pages; ChatGPT and Claude are reputation games — win with entity presence, consistent naming and being discussed where models train.
- Fix retrieval before polish: 23% of losses were pages that never entered the answer set despite good copy.
- Reddit out-cited YouTube, Wikipedia and social platforms, coming within two citations of Google's own pages.
- Caveat: single-vendor sample; engines change retrieval behavior between passes, so treat the snapshot as dated.
