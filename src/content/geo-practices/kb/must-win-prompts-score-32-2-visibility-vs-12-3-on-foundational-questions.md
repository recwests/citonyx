## Summary
3RD expected broad educational questions to be the easiest visibility win and found the opposite: across about 656,000 answers, median visibility was 32.2% on must-win prompts, 24.2% on core prompts and 12.3% on foundational definition questions. Must-win beat foundational for 83 of 96 brands.

## Details
- Proposed reason: must-win prompts sit near the brand's own product terms, where its site and comparison pages are already retrievable, while definitional prompts are contested by every publisher in the category.
- Practitioner read: leave definitional content to earned media and own the product-adjacent, comparison and use-case prompts on your own domain.
- The report's own caveat: customers define the prompt tiers themselves, so this is not a like-for-like comparison and should be re-benchmarked per account.
- Vendor-collected and observational: treat it as a default hypothesis, not a universal law.
