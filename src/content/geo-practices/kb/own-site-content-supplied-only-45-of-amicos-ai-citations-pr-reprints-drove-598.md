## Summary
AI citations are built off-site: Amico Lighting's own website supplied only 4.5% of its citation sources while PR reprints on barchart.com (59.8%) and retail listings (Home Depot, Amazon, Lowe's combined 101.4%) carried the brand to an 88.6% AI recommendation rate across five models. Cross-platform consensus, not on-site optimization, won the answers.

## Details
- Among 350 monitored model responses, Amico reached 100% mention in Google AI Overview, 97% in Gemini, 94% in Perplexity, 91% in AI Mode and 60% in Copilot.
- The program combined prompt taxonomy, entity consistency across product pages and listicles, PR through high-authority channels and comparison pages with feature tables.
- Own-domain citation share was only 4.5%, showing GEO success requires third-party validation, not just website optimization.
- Sustained 80%+ mention rates were achieved by week 12, with first appearances within 4-6 weeks.
