## Summary

Consumer trust in AI search dropped 28 points (82% to 54%) YoY while usage rose 70%. 95% double-check AI results. Baby Boomers find AI more helpful (63%) than Gen Z (47%).

## Details

Fractl partnered with Search Engine Land surveying 1,008 U.S. consumers and 150 marketers (Q2 2026). Only 13% completely trust AI (Klaviyo). 77% say AI reduces authenticity (Clutch). 54% of Gen Z would reduce trust in brands using AI heavily. 60% of U.S. consumers say 'AI' in brand messaging is a turnoff (WordPress VIP). Skyword found 54% seek third-party validation when AI and brand messaging disagree. Gartner: 'trust scarcity is rewriting the rules of brand growth.' Brands need multi-platform presence — consumers check 2.4 platforms before buying.
