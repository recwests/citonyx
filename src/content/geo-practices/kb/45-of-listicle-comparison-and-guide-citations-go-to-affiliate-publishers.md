## Summary
Nearly half of all citations to listicles, comparisons and how-to guides, 44.8%, go to publishers that disclose they may earn commission. Finance reaches 53.1%, sports 51.4%, technology 50.7% and retail 46.4%, while education sits at 27.8%. Affiliate and sponsored placements are therefore a measurable route into AI answers rather than a legacy channel.

## Details
OtterlyAI classified publisher business models by reading each domain's disclosure, advertising and editorial-policy pages, across 617,985 citations in September 2026, US, seven engines.

- Forbes leads compensated publishers at 1.77% of all citations, then Bankrate at 1.04%.
- Caveat: 27.1% of citations could not be classified, mostly sites blocking automated crawling, so 44.8% is a floor among verified publishers.
- Finance and technology teams should budget affiliate partnerships alongside owned pages; education brands should weight owned rankings instead.
