## Summary

AI-cited content is 25.7% fresher than organic top-10 (1,064 vs 1,432 days average). ChatGPT shows strongest freshness bias (958 days avg cited age). The viral '4.3x more cited for fresh content' claim traces to no primary study. Freshness is moderate evidence (7.0/10 Zyppy score), not a dominant factor. This conflicts with vendor studies claiming 3.2x–4.3x multipliers.

## Details

Ahrefs measured content age across 16.975M cited URLs on 7 platforms. ChatGPT cites freshest (958 days average), Perplexity also recency-sensitive, Google AI Overviews has smallest freshness bias. Content updated within 30 days is cited 3.2x more (NinjaPromo/ConvertMate multiple studies converge). Zyppy scores freshness 7.0/10 — below accessibility (9.5), search rank (9.4), fan-out (9.3), and brand mentions. Fix crawlability and brand presence before refresh cadence.
