## Summary

Princeton GEO research identified two tactics that measurably improve LLM citation rates: direct expert quotations (+41%) and quantified statistics (+32%). These are earned authority tactics, not SEO tricks.

## Details

The Princeton GEO study (Similarweb reference) found that content containing direct expert quotations improves LLM citation rates by 41%, and content with quantified statistics improves them by 32%. These are not on-page SEO tactics — expert quotes require placing a human expert in credible publications; quantified stats require original research or being cited in someone else's. Fractl's study found 50% of marketers reported decreased organic traffic since AI Overviews launched. Only 12% have GEO strategy with measurable results. 49% monitor LLM visibility (up from 22% in 2025).
