## Summary
3RD scored five AI visibility drivers across 57-60 brands on a 0-100 scale: crawlability and agent readiness had a median of 99, outreach 53, content coverage 49.5, content quality 46 and authority 43.5. Authority was the weakest driver for 22.5 of 54 brands, content quality for 19.5, crawlability for none.

## Details
- Read: for most sites AI can already fetch the pages; what is missing is proof hosted on third-party sites and pages that answer the question plainly.
- Budget implication: audit access once, then spend on outreach to sources that cite competitors but not you, and on content quality, rather than another technical audit.
- A 99 median does not clear every host: one robots.txt on a single subdomain can still block agents, so run readiness per host.
- Caveat: vendor-defined driver scores, self-selected customer sample, Nordic-leaning; read the scores as relative within that sample.
