## Summary
Brands are the single largest cited publisher type: 38.9% of 450,473 classified citations in OtterlyAI's September 2026 study, and 35.6% of cited listicles. First-party pages are not locked out of the largest format; they compete against affiliate publishers rather than being excluded by them.

## Details
Publisher type was classified for 72.9% of citations: brand 38.9%, affiliate or compensated 36.6%, editorial 11.7%, community 8.6%, institutional 3.9%, personal blogs 0.3%.

- Product and e-commerce pages skew most brand-owned, holding 50-88% of citations in those formats, but together they are only about 10% of cited pages by volume.
- Listicles are the contested ground, since roughly half of cited listicles come from publishers that may earn commission.
- Owned listicles therefore need stronger evidence, first-hand testing and methodology disclosure to hold the slot against affiliate competition.
