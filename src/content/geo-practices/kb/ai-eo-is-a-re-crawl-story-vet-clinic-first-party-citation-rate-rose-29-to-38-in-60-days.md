## Summary
Adpharm's 60-day AI-EO case on sixteenmilevet.com raised first-party citation rate from 29% to 38% across ChatGPT, Claude, and Google AI. Bing's AI report logged 1,544 citations with a 6.5x ramp. Critically, nothing moved for five weeks: Google and Bing had to re-crawl and re-index rebuilt pages before engines could quote them.

## Details
The clinic shipped robots policy, router-driven sitemap, /llms.txt, reviewer-aware E-E-A-T, schema hygiene, and Core Web Vitals. Informational posts that were invisible on day 0 began getting cited by name (heartworm post cited by ChatGPT and Google; tick post by Claude). Human visitors tripled and bot traffic jumped ~14x; indexed pages rose 35 to 60. Laggards: 'best vet in Oakville' stayed a wall and one content bet (Powassan) never landed. Budget four to eight weeks before the needle moves (Adpharm, July 2026).
