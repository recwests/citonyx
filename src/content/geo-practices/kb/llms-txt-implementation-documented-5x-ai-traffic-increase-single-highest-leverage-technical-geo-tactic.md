## Summary

The Concurate case study documented a 5x increase in AI-referred traffic after implementing an llms.txt file at the domain root. The file lists important pages with one-line descriptions organized by topic cluster. Erlin data (500+ brands, 2026) confirms llms.txt drives +32% coverage lift in 11-17 days. (Sources: Stackmatix/Concurate 2026, Erlin 2026)

## Details

- Concurate case study (cited by Stackmatix, March 2026): 5x AI traffic increase from llms.txt implementation.
- Erlin (500+ brands, 2026): llms.txt drives +32% coverage lift in 11-17 days median.
- Implementation: place file at domain root (yourdomain.com/llms.txt), list most important pages with one-line descriptions, organize by topic cluster, update with significant new content.
- Check Cloudflare/robots.txt: Cloudflare recently changed default config to block AI bots.
- Caveat: single case study with publication bias — brands that saw no lift are less likely to publish.
