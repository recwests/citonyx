## Summary
Brand visibility differs by AI model. Trakkr's analysis of 797,644 comparisons across 8 models found all models agree on a #1 brand only 4.0% of the time, and 60% of queries show under 50% agreement. Comparison queries are most predictable (50.4% agreement); "best of" queries offer the most opportunity with 14.8% high divergence.

## Details
Trakkr Study 004 (Mar 2026): 44,088 reports, 797,644 valid comparisons, 8 models, 8,902 unique prompts, Aug 2025-Mar 2026. Average #1-brand agreement 43.3%; perfect consensus 4.0%; 14.6% of queries have under 25% agreement. Meta AI and Perplexity are outliers, correlating only 10-17% with other models — each is effectively a separate channel. Comparison queries are the most stable (50.4% agreement); "best of" queries have the highest divergence (14.8% high-divergence, 43.4% agreement) — room for underdogs. Action: track each model separately; prioritize comparison content for predictable wins.
