## Summary
In September 2026, review and directory sites accounted for 21.6% of the citations going to Perplexity's top 50 domains, and four statistics aggregators another 6.9% (64,004 citations). Reddit still led with 176,005 citations, 2.86x LinkedIn and 8.55x YouTube, per Surfer's 10.37 million-citation scrape.

## Details
- Surfer AI Search Analytics, 1-28 September 2026: 57,327 prompts, 978,402 Perplexity answers, about 10.37 million citations scraped from the consumer UI.
- Directories: Clutch 45,596 citations, G2 19,437, Yelp 14,049, Capterra 12,436, Tripadvisor 10,108. Stat aggregators: worldmetrics 19,990, gitnux 19,584, wifitalents 13,168, zipdo 11,262.
- Because the top 50 domains hold only 9.0% of all Perplexity citations, niche and local outlets matter more than any global list.
- Caveat: panel skews toward SEO and Polish domains; validate against your own prompt set.
