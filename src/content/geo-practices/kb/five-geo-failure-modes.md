## Summary

Daily Geo Insights (June 2026) documents five systemic GEO failure modes: (1) fabrication — AI generates plausible false outcomes outside brand control; (2) foundationless optimization — surface tactics without authority produce zero results; (3) negative representation persistence — correction takes months; (4) citation volatility — weekly churn; (5) attribution gaps — 70.6% of AI traffic arrives without referrer.

## Details

Failure mode 1: AI fabrication is outside brand control — Olympics study showed 1 in 5 correct AI responses still wrong 3+ weeks post-event. Mode 2: brands doing 'GEO keywords' without authority or schema see no measurable change. Mode 3: a brand appearing in negative context is worse than invisibility; correction takes months. Mode 4: citations appear and disappear weekly due to training updates, retrieval shifts, and model non-determinism — quarterly snapshots miss this. Mode 5: analytics show AI traffic as 'direct' — proving ROI requires brand search lift and counterfactual modeling. GEO programs need maintenance discipline, negative-representation monitoring, and measurement beyond GA4.
