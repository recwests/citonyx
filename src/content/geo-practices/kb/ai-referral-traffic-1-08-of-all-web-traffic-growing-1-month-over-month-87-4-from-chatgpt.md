## Summary

AI referral traffic accounts for 1.08% of all website traffic (Conductor 2026, 3.3B sessions), growing ~1% monthly. ChatGPT drives 87.4% of that traffic. IT industry sees highest at 2.8%.

## Details

Conductor's 2026 AEO/GEO Benchmarks Report analyzed 3.3 billion sessions across 13,000+ domains. AI referral traffic is 1.08% of all traffic, growing ~1% MoM. ChatGPT drives 87.4%. AI Overviews appear in 25.11% of Google searches (up from 13.14% in March 2025). AI visitors convert at 2x the rate in 1/3 the sessions vs organic (Knotch/Conductor). Semrush found AI-driven visitors convert 4.4x higher than standard organic. Ahrefs reported 23x conversion rate in some cases. AI visitors spend 68% more time on site (SE Ranking).
