## Summary

The Perplexity brand citation rate is 13.05% versus ChatGPT's 0.59% — a 22x gap. Perplexity averages 8.2 sources per answer (3.4x ChatGPT). Reddit alone accounts for 20-24% of all Perplexity citations. LinkedIn Company Pages receive 59% of Perplexity's LinkedIn citations.

## Details

- Source: Profound (680M citations), Evertune (200M prompts), Tinuiti Q1 2026, Semrush (325K prompts).
- Perplexity's three-layer ML reranking system favors earned media from tier-1 publications.
- Platform passed 230M MAU in Q1 2026; 780M monthly queries.
- Perplexity-referred traffic converts at 3.1x rate of Google organic.

- 34,234 AI responses analyzed (Leapd, April 2026): ChatGPT cites brands 0.59%, Perplexity 13.05%, Grok 27%.
- Perplexity averages 8.2 citations per response vs ChatGPT's ~2.5 — structural opportunity: more slots per answer (Everything-PR, June 2026).
- Perplexity cites content published within last 30 days at 82% rate vs ChatGPT's reliance on training data (Seer Interactive, June 2025).
- Visible year signals in titles improve Perplexity citation rates by ~30%.
- Only 11% domain overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity citations (Profound, 680M citations, March 2026).
- Practical takeaway: run separate audits per platform. A brand ranking well on ChatGPT can be invisible on Perplexity and vice versa.

This is the single most important insight for GEO measurement: platform-level citation rates are not interchangeable. A brand invisible on ChatGPT may be prominent on Perplexity and vice versa. The same content, same brand, same period yields dramatically different outcomes. Multi-platform tracking is not optional. The 615× gap also suggests that publisher content distribution strategies should be platform-specific: earn Reddit mentions for Perplexity, Wikipedia citations for ChatGPT, and press coverage for Grok/Gemini.
