## Summary
Machine Relations Index (28,870 source events, 7,124 domains, 6 engines, June 2026): 71% of all cited sources appear in exactly one AI engine's responses. Even the most overlapping pair (Google AI Mode + Grok) agrees on just ~20% of sources. SurfacedBy's parallel study (16,400 answers, 127,198 citations) corroborates the finding: 69.6% of cited domains appear in only one engine and just 2.7% in all five.

## Details
Wellows analyzed 22.7M citations from 1.149M questions (441,946 websites, 84% US, Jan-Jun 2026): 79.6% of cited websites appear on only one of ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, AI Overviews or AI Mode, while just 0.31% reach all five. ChatGPT is the most isolated: other engines never touch 89.1% of what it cites. Per-engine unique-source share: ChatGPT 76.3%, Perplexity 69.9%, Gemini 63.7%, AI Overviews 50.4%, AI Mode 49.7%. Brands co-occur (30.3%) far more than exact pages (6.8%): a shared page is a 42x-above-chance event, a shared brand 17x. Prompt-level data corroborates the same pattern: Leads Now AI's 5,051 polls across 68 real B2B buyer prompts found 74% of ever-cited prompts were cited by only one engine — DuckDuckGo easiest (66%), Gemini middle (39%), ChatGPT hardest (18%), with only 5 prompts cited by all three (Aug 2026). The 71% single-engine figure is the most striking measure of platform divergence: Perplexity cited Gartner 0 times while Google AI Mode cited it 98; Claude cited Crunchbase 51x vs Grand View 5x. SurfacedBy adds volume: Gemini cites 11.0 sources per answer vs 3.7 for ChatGPT. Caveat: Wellows' own commercial dataset; agreement rose 33% in six months. Strategic implication: track and optimize for each engine independently.
