## Summary
France launched AI Overviews and AI Mode on July 22, 2026, the last major market to get them. Google holds neighboring-rights contracts with ~450 French publishers and told them AI answers count toward those payments — the first market where an opt-out and compensation coexist side by side, making it the test case for press markets everywhere.

## Details
Both surfaces went live on desktop, mobile web, and the Google app with Gemini 3.5 powering AI Mode, two months inside the September 23 commitment Google gave French publishers. Google pointed site owners at the Search Console control that keeps a site out of Search's generative AI features, available in France, and reiterated it is not a ranking signal outside those features. No collective agreement has been announced; negotiations continue. For publishers: AI answers now carry explicit payment value in at least one market (July 2026).
