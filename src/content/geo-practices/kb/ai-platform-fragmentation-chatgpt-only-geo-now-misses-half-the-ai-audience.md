## Summary
ChatGPT's share of generative-AI web traffic fell from about 76% in June 2025 to roughly 53% by May 2026, while Gemini climbed to 27-28% and Claude to ~9% — so optimizing for ChatGPT alone now reaches only about half the AI audience.

## Details
Similarweb's 2026 Generative AI Landscape report (worldwide traffic panel): category-wide web visits grew 70% YoY to 9.5 billion monthly (Jun 2025-May 2026). Gemini absorbed most of ChatGPT's lost share through its distribution (Search, Android, Workspace); Claude quadrupled share to ~9% but from a small base. Roughly 26% of ChatGPT responses now carry an ad. Practical implication: multi-platform GEO is now required — ChatGPT for reach, Gemini for the distributed search surface, Claude for high-intent technical audiences. Panel-based estimates are directional, not audited.
