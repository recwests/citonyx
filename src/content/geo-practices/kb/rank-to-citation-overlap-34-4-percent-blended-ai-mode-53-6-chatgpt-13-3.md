## Summary
In a pre-registered blind test of eight buyer-intent queries, the share of AI-cited domains that also rank in Google's top-10 was 34.4% blended: Google AI Mode 53.6%, Perplexity 36.4%, ChatGPT 13.3%. The viral 'under 20%' figure therefore describes ChatGPT, not AI search as a whole.

## Details
GeoParrot locked predicted bands before collection (AI Mode 55-75%, Perplexity 25-45%, ChatGPT 15-35%) and required blended overlap under 50% plus an engine spread of at least 25 points; the observed spread was 40.3 points. ChatGPT completed only 3 of 8 queries because of login walls, so its contribution is data-limited. Limits: one intent type, a single logged-out snapshot, and overlap is correlation rather than causation.
