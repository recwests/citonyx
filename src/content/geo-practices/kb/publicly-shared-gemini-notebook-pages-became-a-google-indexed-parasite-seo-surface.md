## Summary
From 21 September 2026 Google indexed publicly shared Gemini Notebook pages hosted on notebook.google.com, letting SEOs inherit Google's own domain authority. Gagan Ghotra flagged it as a parasite-SEO surface; practitioners measured it working within days, though black-hat parasite pages typically survived only six to eight weeks in 2026.

## Details
- On 21 September 2026 Gagan Ghotra reported that Google indexes publicly shared notebooks from Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM); those pages live on notebook.google.com and inherit the parent domain's authority.
- A practitioner check on 22 September 2026 confirmed indexing, a Googlebot response and recurring spam patterns (seo-kreativ.de).
- The tactic is a variant of site-reputation abuse, where the host platform - not the poster - owns the domain, and black-hat parasite pages lasted six to eight weeks on average in 2026.
- Expect Google to noindex the surface or block it in robots.txt, as it did with public Gemini chats. Treat as a short-lived, high-risk play.
