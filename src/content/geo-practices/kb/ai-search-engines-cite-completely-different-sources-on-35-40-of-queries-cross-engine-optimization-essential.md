## Summary

Search Atlas study of 5.5M LLM responses across 748K unique queries found Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Gemini share zero cited domains on 35-40% of queries. Perplexity (RAG-based) cites 2-3x more unique domains than parametric models. Gemini-ChatGPT domain overlap is 42%. BrightEdge found 45.5% of AIO citations come from pages outside organic top 10.

## Details

Each engine uses fundamentally different source selection logic: Perplexity = live RAG search with freshness bias; ChatGPT = training data frequency + optional browsing; Gemini = training data + Google Search grounding. Strategy implication: single-platform optimization misses most citation opportunities. Brands must optimize for each engine's architecture. Perplexity favors dense, factual, extractable content with inline citations. ChatGPT training data frequency favors brand frequency in authoritative sources. Gemini favors Google-ranked + YouTube content.
