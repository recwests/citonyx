## Summary

Pages with comprehensive FAQ schema show 67% higher citation rates in AI-generated answers, per Orion GEO Index analysis of 50,000+ pages. Product Schema yields +52% for e-commerce.

## Details

WhatsMyGeoScore's analysis of 50,000+ pages in Orion's GEO Index found FAQ schema delivers highest citation impact (+67%). HowTo schema delivers +44% for instructional queries. Organization Schema provides +28% authority signals with 19% higher domain authority in AI assessments. Product Schema yields +52% visibility in AI product recommendations. Pages show 15-25% GEO Score improvement within 30 days of comprehensive schema implementation. Google recommends JSON-LD; AI engines parse it most reliably.
