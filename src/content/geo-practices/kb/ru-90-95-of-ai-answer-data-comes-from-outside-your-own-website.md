## Summary
According to a РБК (RBC) guide, only 5-10% of sources in ChatGPT, YandexGPT and GigaChat answers are the brand's own corporate sites; the remaining 90-95% are external reviews, ratings and structured data. GEO shifts focus from site positions to the reputation and data that make an AI agent recommend the brand itself.

## Details
- Five-step methodology: visibility audit across 50 queries in YandexGPT/GigaChat/ChatGPT; schema.org + JSON-LD markup (2-4 weeks); content hubs (2-3 months); off-site reputation — 2-4 expert articles/month plus one study per quarter (3-6 months); weekly mention monitoring.
- Seven typical mistakes: keyword overuse, ignoring external sources (~70% of effort should go there), missing microdata, bureaucratic language, rare updates, no monitoring, unrealistic speed expectations.
- First results in 4-6 weeks, stable effect in 3 months; budget 200-500 thousand RUB per 3 months for small business.
- Source is an agency expert publication, not an independent study.
