## Summary

Foglift's Q2 2026 AI Readiness Study: 29.6% of websites still have zero JSON-LD schema markup. Median AI search subscore was 46/100 vs 86/100 median SEO readiness — a 40-point gap. This represents a massive opportunity for sites that implement proper structured data to gain AI citation advantage over the nearly 30% of competitors with no schema at all.

## Details

Even among sites with schema, most implement only basic Article or Organization, missing FAQPage, HowTo, Product, and entity relationship markup. The Princeton KDD 2024 study: pages with nested schema + structural clarity have up to 40% higher visibility in AI responses. Foglift recommends starting with Organization + SameAs identifiers for entity foundation, then adding page-level types. Misaligned schema (markup that doesn't match visible prose) has limited AI citation impact.
