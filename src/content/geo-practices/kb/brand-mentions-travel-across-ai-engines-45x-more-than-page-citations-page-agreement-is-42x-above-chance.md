## Summary
Wellows analyzed 22.7 million citations (1.15M questions, Jan-Jun 2026) and found exact-page agreement across five engines is just 6.8% while brand-name agreement reaches 30.3% — a 4.5x gap. Measured against chance, page overlap (42x) is stronger than brand overlap (17x). ChatGPT is the outlier: rivals never touch 89.1% of the sources it cites.

## Details
Two practical readings follow. Brand is the easier cross-engine target (30.3% co-occurrence), so track brand mentions as a separate line from page citations. Page wins land one engine at a time and are rarer but more informative — a shared page citation is a 42x-above-chance event. Cross-engine agreement rose 31% in six months (8.6% to 11.3%), and category matters more than intent: settled categories agree 10.1-15.5% of the time versus 6.3% for emerging AEO/GEO. The window for owning an engine-specific niche is closing.
