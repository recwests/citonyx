## Summary

Microsoft Clarity's 2025 analysis of 1,200+ publisher sites found 70.6% of AI-referred traffic arrives without a referrer header, classified as 'direct' in GA4. Rankability analysis (July 2026) confirmed: AI referrals are invisible in standard analytics. Adobe Analytics: generative AI traffic to US retail grew 1,200% (July 2024 to Feb 2025). Yet AI referrals remain <1% of total sessions. Teams optimizing for AI citation cannot measure impact through standard dashboards.

## Details

- Microsoft Clarity (1,200+ publisher sites, 2025): 70.6% of AI traffic without referrer.
- GA4 classifies unrecognized referrers as 'direct' — AI traffic merges into the same bucket as typed URLs.
- Adobe Analytics: generative AI traffic to retail grew 1,200% July 2024 to Feb 2025.
- AI referrals: <1% of total sessions but growing 155.6% over 8 months.
- Practical takeaway: set up custom GA4 filters for known LLM referrer domains (chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, claude.ai, gemini.google.com). Monitor direct traffic spikes with unusually high engagement and conversion rates. Use dedicated AI visibility tracking tools.
