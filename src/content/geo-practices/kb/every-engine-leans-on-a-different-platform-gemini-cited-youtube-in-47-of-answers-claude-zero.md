## Summary
In Prefer's September 2026 study of 960 answers, Gemini cited YouTube in 113 of its 240 answers (47.1%), while ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude cited YouTube in none. Perplexity cited LinkedIn in 73 answers; Reddit appeared in 98 Perplexity and 93 Gemini answers, in 16 ChatGPT answers and zero Claude answers.

## Details
- ChatGPT and Gemini cited LinkedIn in none of their answers; Claude cited it twice.
- Reddit was cited in more answers than any other site overall (207 of 960), but Claude never cited it and Perplexity carried its own skew toward LinkedIn Pulse articles.
- These splits are not fixed: in Prefer's July 2026 study with the same Perplexity model, Perplexity cited YouTube 28 times, here zero.
- Practical use: build platform-specific assets (video, community threads, professional-network posts) and measure each engine separately rather than assuming one content type travels everywhere.
