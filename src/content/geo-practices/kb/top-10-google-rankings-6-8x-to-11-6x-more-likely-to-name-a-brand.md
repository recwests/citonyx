## Summary
A 24,067-prompt study across AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity found brands ranking on Google's first page were 6.8x to 11.6x more likely to be mentioned. Separately, only 38.5% of AI Overview citation domains ranked in the same query's organic top 10, so ranking helps brand naming but does not guarantee citation.

## Details
Each prompt was run once, English, US desktop, so results are associations rather than causation.

- The effect was strongest on alternative prompts (AI Overviews 11.6x, Gemini 10.5x) and weaker on category prompts (6.8x-8.7x).
- Planning implication: treat organic top-10 as a prerequisite for being named, and track citation overlap as a separate metric.
- More than 58% of cited domains sat outside the top 10, so SEO presence explains naming better than it explains sourcing.
