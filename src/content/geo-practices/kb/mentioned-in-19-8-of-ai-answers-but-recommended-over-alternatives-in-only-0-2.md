## Summary
Across 60 brands and 493,856 non-branded answers, the median brand was mentioned in 19.8% of AI answers but recommended over the alternatives in just 0.2%, roughly one recommendation for every 65 mentions. Fifteen brands were never recommended in the month at all.

## Details
- 3RD counts a recommendation only when the answer prefers the brand over named alternatives; being listed, or listed first, does not count, so absolute levels are low by design.
- By prompt tier the median recommended rate was 0.3% on must-win, 0.15% on core and 0% on foundational prompts; 44 of 60 brands sat under 1%.
- Action: report recommended rate beside visibility, then read the answers where a competitor is recommended and you are only listed - those show the proof AI is missing.
- Caveat: strict definition, vendor data, Nordic-leaning sample; compare against competitors rather than the absolute 0.2%.
