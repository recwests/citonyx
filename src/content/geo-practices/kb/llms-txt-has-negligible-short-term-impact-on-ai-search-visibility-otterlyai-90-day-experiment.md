## Summary

OtterlyAI's 90-day controlled experiment (published March 2026): only 84 AI bot visits to /llms.txt out of 62.1K total AI bot hits (0.14%). Ahrefs (June 2026) confirmed no major LLM provider currently supports llms.txt. Search Engine Land: just over 2% of 1M sites have a valid llms.txt. Cloudflare: AI user-agent crawling +15x in 2025, but llms.txt is not a primary touchpoint. Google's official GEO guide (May 2026) does not mention llms.txt as a ranking factor.

## Details

- OtterlyAI experiment: 90 days, 62.1K AI bot hits, only 84 to /llms.txt (0.14%).
- Ahrefs (June 2026): 'no major LLM provider currently supports llms.txt.'
- Search Engine Land: ~2% of 1M sites analyzed have a valid llms.txt file.
- Cloudflare: AI user-agent crawling increased 15x in 2025, but this does not correlate with llms.txt usage.
- Google official GEO guide (May 15, 2026): llms.txt is not mentioned among optimization factors.
- Practical takeaway: don't prioritize llms.txt. Invest in crawlable server-rendered content, schema markup, and clear page structure instead.
