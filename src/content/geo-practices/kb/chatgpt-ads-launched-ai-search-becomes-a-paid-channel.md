## Summary
AI search is becoming a paid channel. OpenAI launched ChatGPT Ads in February 2026, initially on Free and Go tiers in the US; reports cite $60 CPM, a $200K minimum, and new product-feed ads in the Ads Manager beta (June 2026). Google is simultaneously testing sponsored ads inside AI Mode.

## Details
- Sources: segwise.ai (Feb 2026 launch), aitooldiscovery (June 2026 product-feed ads, $60 CPM, $200K minimum), Search Engine Land (AI Mode as Google's next ads engine), eMarketer (US AI search ad spend $2.08B in 2026, ~$26B by 2029).
- Google tested sponsored ads in AI Mode (Feb 2026).
- Implication: establish organic AI visibility now before paid placements crowd the answer; track ChatGPT recommendation positions; evaluate early-adopter tests in travel, retail and tech verticals.
