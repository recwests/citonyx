## Summary
Google is testing a 'Source preferences' button in the AI Mode citation sidebar that opens the user's preferred sources list for adding or removing domains. Combined with Preferred Sources usage growing more than 7x during 2026, a user-maintained allowlist is becoming a real distribution channel alongside retrieval.

## Details
Spotted by Search Engine Roundtable on 19 October 2026; Google confirmed the 7x growth figure in its 15 October update.

- The goal shifts from one-off citations to being a domain users deliberately pin.
- That rewards recognisable brand names, a single stable domain rather than rotating subdomains or campaign URLs, and repeat exposure in the categories a user researches.
- Measurement caveat: preference lists are not exposed to publishers, so effects appear only as baseline lifts in impressions and branded search.
