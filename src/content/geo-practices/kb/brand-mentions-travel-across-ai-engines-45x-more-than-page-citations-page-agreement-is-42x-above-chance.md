## Summary
Wellows analyzed 22.7 million citations on 531,889 questions where all five engines answered (Jan-Jun 2026): 79.6% of sources were cited by exactly one engine, exact-page agreement is just 6.8% vs 30.3% for brand mentions — a 4.5x gap. Measured against chance, page overlap (42x) is stronger than brand overlap (17x). ChatGPT is the outlier: 76.3% of its sources are unique to it.

## Details
- Brand is the easier cross-engine target (30.3% co-occurrence) — track brand mentions as a separate line from page citations; page wins land one engine at a time but a shared page citation is a 42x-above-chance event.
- Google's two Search surfaces agree 24.5% (raw) but only 1.06x above chance once shared candidate pools are accounted for.
- Cross-engine agreement rose 33% in six months (8.49% to 11.25%); emerging AEO/GEO categories are lowest at 6.3%.
- Category matters more than intent: settled categories agree 10.1-15.5% of the time.
- Practical takeaway: report per-engine plus a separate brand-mention line, never a blended score alone; the window for owning an engine-specific niche is closing.
