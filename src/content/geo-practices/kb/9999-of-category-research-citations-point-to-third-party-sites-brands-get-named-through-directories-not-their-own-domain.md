## Summary
Victorious's Q2 2026 study analyzed 49,391 citations from category-research prompts: 99.99% pointed to third-party websites rather than the brand's own domain, and only 4 of 150 brands earned a citation to their own site. Buyer-journey stage decides the source type: problem-awareness answers pull educational content yet name the brand in just 0.10% of answers; category-research answers shift to directories and comparison sites and name brands more than 12x as often.

## Details
- Actionable split: publish educational content that answers early-journey questions (it gets cited even if unnamed), and build presence on the directories and comparison sites AI consults at consideration — that is where brands actually get named.
- Vertical differences: legal citations concentrate in a few prestige directories; SaaS citations scatter across 10,000+ unique domains.
- Caveat: sponsored study by Victorious. Source: SEJ (Jul 2026).
