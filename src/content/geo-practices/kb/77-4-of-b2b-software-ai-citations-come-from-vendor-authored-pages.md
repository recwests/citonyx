## Summary
Broadcastwell ran 860 buyer answers for 85 B2B software companies and traced 5,160 citations. Among the 100 most-cited domains, 77.4% resolved to vendor-authored comparison posts, category guides and listicles; review platforms took 10.2%, independent media 6.5%, analyst firms 5.9% and community platforms 0%. The median company was named in only 20% of its own category answers.

## Details
Single engine (Claude with live web search), one run per prompt, July 2026; method and CSVs are public.

- 35% of companies were never named, while the median category leader appeared in 80% of answers.
- Reddit leads third-party citations in other categories and studies, so this is a B2B-software benchmark, not a universal rule.
- Actionable read: fund owned reference content (comparisons, category guides, listicles) before assuming only third parties get cited.
