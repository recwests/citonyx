## Summary

Target 15+ named entities per page. The median cited page contains 20.6 named entities per 1,000 words vs 5-8 for non-cited pages. Below 10 entities, citation probability drops sharply regardless of other quality signals.

## Details

Wellows study of Google AI Overview ranking factors. SE Ranking 129K domain study confirmed: pages cited by ChatGPT contain 2.4x more named entities than uncited pages covering the same topics. A paragraph naming Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, and Pipedrive with pricing outcompetes a paragraph discussing 'leading CRM platforms' without naming any. The 10-entity mark functions as a binary gate — below it, citation probability drops sharply.
