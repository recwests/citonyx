## Summary
Ansvisor grouped the 24 most-cited competitor URLs in its AI visibility software category by page type. Together they earned 22,884 citations: eight best-tools listicles took about 30%, five product, feature and home pages about 30%, six how-to guides about 22%, two original research pages about 8% and two free tools about 6%.

## Details
- The single most cited competitor URL was a listicle of AI Overviews trackers with 1,988 citations; a narrow how-to guide came second with 1,792.
- Why listicles win: a best-tool-for prompt asks for a shortlist, and a page that already contains one with stated criteria is trivially reusable.
- Free tools and checkers were cited too, and product pages were plain text stating what the product does, for whom and how it works.
- Caveat: one category (AI visibility and SEO tools), six platforms, 18 brands, single vendor dataset; page-type shares will differ by vertical.
