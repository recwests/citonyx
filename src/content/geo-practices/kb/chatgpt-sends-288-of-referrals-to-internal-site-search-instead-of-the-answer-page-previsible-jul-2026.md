## Summary
Previsible's July 2026 study of 6.77M LLM-driven sessions: ChatGPT sends 28.8% of its referrals to internal search results pages rather than the actual answer page. The model trusts a domain enough to send a visitor but can't identify the right page, so it defaults to the site's own search box. Internal site search is now an acquisition surface.

## Details
This means internal site search optimization is a new and largely unaddressed GEO tactic. Most sites have not optimized their internal search experience for AI-referred visitors. Fixes: ensure internal search returns relevant results for LLM-style queries, add analytics tracking for internal search as an acquisition channel, structure content so the most important page for each query is unambiguous to AI crawlers through clear topic clustering and internal linking.
