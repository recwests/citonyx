## Summary
Peec researcher Metehan Yesilyurt built stateglobe.com with 50,000 fabricated statistics for about $50. Google deindexed it within weeks, yet seven months later ChatGPT still fetched and cited it, sending over 10,000 sessions, with 72,000 ChatGPT-User requests in 30 days and links from 330+ domains including Shopify and DHL.

## Details
The fake numbers reached an academic paper and a bank's investment research, illustrating citation laundering: each citation makes an unsourced number look verified.

- Claimed mechanism: LLMs fan out to statistics queries, prefer specific numbers, and rerank for relevance rather than truth.
- It is an N=1 experiment by a vendor researcher, so treat it as directional.
- Defensive use: monitor whether your brand's numbers are misquoted, and expect deindexing to lag citation removal by months.
