## Summary
Meltwater's June 2026 report (8M+ citations, 8 LLMs) ranked YouTube #1 at 188,863 citations (+56.4% MoM), ahead of Reddit 111,860 (+13%), NIH 91,227 (+41.3%) and Wikipedia 83,191 (+55.2%). Each model prefers different sources: Gemini institutional, Perplexity research, Claude data-centric — a single blended score hides these per-model differences.

## Details
- Per-model ecosystem: Claude favors Statista/NIH (data-centric), ChatGPT Wikipedia/NIH (institutional), Copilot Forbes/LinkedIn (B2B), AI Mode YouTube/Reddit/Yahoo (reach engine), Gemini balanced.
- Channel shifts: Reddit citations +5.89%, Instagram +167%, Facebook +76.8%, YouTube -2.29%; earned/news stays stable at ~35-37.6% of citation domains.
- Press releases contribute almost nothing to AI citation rates (0.2% Meltwater; 0.04% per MR Research).
- YouTube optimization emerges as a core GEO lever — transcripts, descriptions, chapters and metadata directly influence citation rates.
- Action: measure visibility per model, per source type and per citation pathway; earn trusted editorial coverage; get data into authoritative databases; publish structured research-led content.
