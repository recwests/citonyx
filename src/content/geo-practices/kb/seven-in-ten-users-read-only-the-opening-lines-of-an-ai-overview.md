## Summary
Behavioural research compiled in 2026 shows roughly seven in ten Google users read only the first few lines of an AI Overview before deciding whether to click, scroll or leave. 88% click 'show more' even without intending to read the expanded content, so the opening sentence carries outsized influence.

## Details
The dominant pattern is scan-and-decide: about 7 in 10 users stop at the opening lines of an AI Overview (omnibound.ai, 2026). 88% expand 'show more' without actually reading the revealed content. This matches Seer Interactive and Advanced Web Ranking findings that AI Overview presence suppresses below-the-fold clicks. Implication: lead every section with a self-contained, quotable answer sentence, since only the first line is reliably consumed. Caveat: aggregated secondary reporting rather than one controlled experiment, so treat as directional.
