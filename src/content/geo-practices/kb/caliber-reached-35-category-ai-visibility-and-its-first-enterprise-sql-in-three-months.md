## Summary
Rankshift's case study reports that Caliber, an enterprise stakeholder-intelligence platform, reached 35% category AI visibility, ahead of Reputation.com at 25%, RepTrak 17% and YouGov 16%, with high-intent prompt visibility peaking at 53% and 100% positive sentiment. A first large enterprise SQL was attributed to bottom-funnel AI search exposure within three months.

## Details
- Program combined a technical SEO cleanup, AI-structured content with clearer entity signals and answer-first summaries, digital PR placements in already-cited sources, and Reddit/community reinforcement.
- Content shifted from awareness topics to bottom-funnel landing pages built around solution and comparison queries.
- Measurement framework: AI visibility, citation share, grounding rate and sentiment, and commercial impact (SQLs attributed to AI search).
- Vendor case study: no control group, self-selected client, metrics reported by the agency's own tool, so treat the multiples as directional rather than causal.
