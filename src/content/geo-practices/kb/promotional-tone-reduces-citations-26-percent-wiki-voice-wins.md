## Summary

Eliminate promotional language and keyword stuffing. Write in wiki-voice: neutral, specific, fact-dense. AI models have learned to distinguish reference-quality content from marketing content — reference content wins citations.

## Details

Growth Marshal 50,000-article analysis. Rand Fishkin (SparkToro): 'AI models are trained on the entire web, which means they've learned to distinguish reference-quality content from marketing content. The same language patterns that signal this is an ad to humans signal the same thing to LLMs.' Keyword stuffing reduces citation by ~10%. Keyword-heavy URLs average 6.4 words in non-cited pages vs 2.7 in cited pages. Readability sweet spot: Flesch-Kincaid grade 16 (professional white paper level) earns citations at 2x the rate of grade 19+ and 1.6x the rate of grade 10.
