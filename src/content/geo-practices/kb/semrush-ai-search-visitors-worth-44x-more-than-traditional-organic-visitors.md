## Summary
Semrush found AI search visitors 4.4x more valuable by conversion rate than traditional organic visitors. AI referral visits have 27% lower bounce rate, 38% longer sessions. Wix AI Search Lab reports AI search visits grew 42.8% YoY (15.6B→27.4B in Q1 2026). Semrush projects AI search visitors will surpass traditional search visitors by 2028. However, AI traffic is still only 0.14% of total web visits (Semrush channel mix study, Apr 2026).

## Details
The value gap and volume gap must be considered together: AI traffic is tiny (0.14% of visits) but far more valuable. For most sites, optimizing for AI visibility is a long-term future-proofing move, not an immediate traffic driver. GA4 undercounts AI referrals significantly — 35-70% of AI referral sessions arrive without referrer headers (Adobe Analytics). Custom regex channel groups in GA4 catch what native AI Assistant channel misses.
