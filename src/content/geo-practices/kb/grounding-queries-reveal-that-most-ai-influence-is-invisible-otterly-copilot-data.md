## Summary
Otterly's analysis of its own Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance data (Nov 2025-Feb 2026) found 647 unique grounding queries drove 30,398 Copilot grounding events across 173 pages — with only 169 visible citations, meaning 99.6% of AI influence is invisible to users.

## Details
Five pages carried 74.6% of all citations; homepage took 32.3% and one blog post 22.1%. Blog posts dominated (42.7% of citations) but followed a power law — three posts accounted for 72% of blog citations, while programmatic pages produced just 4.8%. Grounding queries revealed buyer intent and competitor names, pointing to vertical landing pages and comparison content. Practical workflow: export grounding queries from Bing WMT, find high-grounding/low-citation pages, and cross-reference with visible citation trackers to prioritize fixes. Single-vendor data, so treat as directional.
