## Summary
AI referral traffic accounts for 1.08% of all website traffic (Conductor 2026, 3.3B sessions across 13,770 domains), growing ~1% monthly, and ChatGPT delivers 87.4% of it. 25.11% of 21.9M Google searches triggered an AI Overview, highest in Health Care at 48.75%.

## Details
- IT (2.8%) and Consumer Staples (1.9%) have the highest AI referral share.
- In Financials, publishers like NerdWallet (6.73%) win more AI citations than traditional banks; Zillow dominates real-estate brand mentions (7.36%) without making the top-5 cited domains — brand mentions and citations diverge.
- Gemini drove 21% of AI traffic to Utilities.
- AI visitors convert at 2x the rate in 1/3 the sessions vs organic (Knotch/Conductor); Semrush found 4.4x; AI visitors spend 68% more time on site (SE Ranking).
- Caveat: data window is May-Sep 2025 and AIO analysis Sep 15-Oct 12 2025, so figures predate the mid-2026 AIO/AI Mode merger. Authors recommend a holistic AI-plus-SEO presence rather than a per-engine strategy.
