## Summary

For SaaS product recommendations, SEO isn't dead — good SEO is the bare minimum for good GEO. ~80% of AI recommendations trace to search rankings. But 12-15% of citations come from an internal 'labrador' channel (Wikipedia, arXiv, tech press) that SEO can't reach.

## Details

Master's thesis at TUM analyzed 79 prompts × 3 runs across ChatGPT Plus, Business, and Gemini. ~80% of AI product recommendations trace directly back to search rankings — a page has to rank and rank high to be cited. The 'labrador' internal channel (Wikipedia, arXiv, major tech press) accounts for 12-15% of citations and is unreachable through SEO alone. Listicle position is decisive: #1 item selected at 1.7-2.5x chance, top 5 take 76-81% of all selections, position 7+ usually not mentioned. Year-injected queries grew from 5% to 80% of runs, making year-tagged listicles the dominant citation surface.
