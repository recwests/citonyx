## Summary
After ChatGPT's May 2026 homepage-link update — which replaced footnote-style citations with clickable in-answer brand names — homepage referrals jumped from 26-29% to about 62-63% of all ChatGPT referral traffic and has held steady afterward, according to Similarweb's weekly referral data. The homepage is now the AI landing surface.

## Details
Similarweb's weekly referral data shows the shift persisted rather than fading, signalling a permanent behavior change, not a novelty spike. Users increasingly treat the brand name inside the answer as the endpoint of research and land on the homepage already decided, making the traffic behave less like a search click and more like the tail of an ad impression. Practical implications: verify homepage content (now the main AI landing surface), review 'direct' traffic spikes — roughly 70% of AI referrals are misclassified as direct in standard GA4 setups — and treat homepage conversions as an AI performance signal.
