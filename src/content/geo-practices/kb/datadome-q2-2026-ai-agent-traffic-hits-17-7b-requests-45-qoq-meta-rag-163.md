## Summary

DataDome's Q2 2026 report (400+ company network): AI agent requests reached 17.7 billion in April-June 2026, a 45% increase from Q1's 12.2 billion. June alone had 6.6B requests. Meta's RAG crawler grew 163% QoQ. Decodo/Cloudflare analysis: AI traffic grew 187% in 2025, 8× faster than human traffic. Automated systems now generate 57.4% of web requests.

## Details

Meta now generates the majority of AI agent traffic on DataDome's network across both training and RAG crawlers. Meta's training crawler grew 74% QoQ. ChatGPT-User visits dropped 6% QoQ in Q2, though human ChatGPT click-throughs grew 17% (ChatGPT accounts for 80%+ of AI referral traffic). Decodo: AI agent traffic grew ~7,851% year-over-year. The implication: the web is now majority-bot. Publishers need AI agent policies differentiated by agent purpose and business value, not blanket blocks.
