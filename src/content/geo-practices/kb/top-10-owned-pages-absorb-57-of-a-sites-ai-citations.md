## Summary
Across 98 brands and 49,114 distinct owned URLs, the median site had 193 pages cited at least once, but its 10 most-cited pages took 57% of all owned citations (middle half 42%-73%). Only 5% of owned citations went to pages first cited in the previous 90 days, so new pages break in slowly while favourites compound.

## Details
- Roughly 24 new owned pages were cited for the first time each month across 31 brands, directional per 3RD.
- The loop: keep the top 10 cited pages accurate and well structured, then link new pages from them so retrievers reach new material through an already-trusted node.
- Consistent with Ansvisor's single listicle earning 7,007 citations, more than its next five pages combined.
- Caveat: first cited means first seen since tracking began, not time since publication, so publish-to-citation lag cannot be measured here.
