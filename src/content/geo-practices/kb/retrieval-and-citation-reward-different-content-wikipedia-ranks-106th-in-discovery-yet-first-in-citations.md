## Summary
DataForSEO studied 100,249 fan-out queries from 100,000 ChatGPT prompts. The top 10 cited domains account for about 52.7% of all cited sources, while the top 10 discovery domains make up 29.9%. Wikipedia is cited 17,000 times yet ranks 106th in AI search appearances — retrieval and citation reward different content.

## Details
- The gap metric reveals domains that are found often but cited rarely; discovery pulls broadly indexed and popular domains, while citation favors reference-like, extractable and trust-signaling content.
- The findings are specific to ChatGPT-based datasets.
- Practical implication: measure both AI search appearances and AI citations separately — they expose where a brand wins or loses visibility, and content optimized for ranking alone will not necessarily be cited.
