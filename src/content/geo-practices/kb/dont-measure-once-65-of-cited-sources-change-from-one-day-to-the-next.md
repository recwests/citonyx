## Summary
An arXiv measurement study of Google AI Mode, AI Overviews, Perplexity and ChatGPT found day-to-day overlap of cited sources of only 0.34-0.42 Jaccard, meaning roughly 65% of sources change daily. Citation concentration is extreme: the mean Gini is 0.715, highest on Google AI Mode at 0.782.

## Details
- "Don't Measure Once: Measuring Visibility in AI Search (GEO)" (arXiv:2604.07585, 2026) tracked four campaigns over a 45-46 day window from 24 January to 20 March 2026 across four engines.
- Day-to-day Jaccard similarity for cited sources averaged 0.34-0.42 and rank-biased overlap only 0.21-0.26.
- Mean citation-concentration Gini was 0.715: Google AI Mode 0.782 (most concentrated) and Perplexity 0.671 (least).
- Same-day repeated runs overlapped just 32-43% on sources and 33-48% on brands. Inference: single-run visibility snapshots are not evidence - measure repeatedly and report concentration alongside share.
