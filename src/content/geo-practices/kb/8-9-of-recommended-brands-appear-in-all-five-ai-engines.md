## Summary
Minuttia's five-engine study found 51.6% of brands recommended on category prompts and 53.7% on alternative prompts appeared in only one engine's answer. Just 9.1% and 8.1% respectively were named by all five engines. Overlap between ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews ranged from roughly 35% to 54%, depending on prompt type.

## Details
Engines: Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity; one snapshot per prompt, US English desktop.

- The authors caution that Claude and Perplexity ran through APIs on lighter models and Claude did not always search, which lowers its citation rates.
- Implication: keep separate prompt sets and share-of-voice baselines per engine.
- Never report a blended multi-engine score as the only KPI; the single-engine share is where most brands actually live.
