## Summary
A 2026 HubSpot-cited study found 60% of content cited by AI includes proprietary data or unique insights. Recycled summaries and generic content are far less likely to be cited. Original research, survey data, and unique analysis are the strongest AI citation magnets.

## Details
This finding reinforces the Princeton study's data showing statistics with named sources boost citations 40%. Brands that invest in original research, customer surveys, industry benchmarks, and data-backed thought leadership consistently outperform content farms that recycle existing information. The practical implication: every brand should aim to produce at least 1-2 original data assets per quarter as cornerstone content for their GEO program. The data also serves as a link-building asset and earns media coverage that further compounds AI citation probability.
