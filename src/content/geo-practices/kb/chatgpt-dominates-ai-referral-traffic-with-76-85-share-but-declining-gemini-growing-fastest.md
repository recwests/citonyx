## Summary

Statcounter April 2026: ChatGPT = 76.85% AI referral share (down from 84.21% Apr 2025), Gemini = 9.00% (up from 2.31%), Perplexity = 7.73%, Copilot = 3.76%, Claude = 2.66%. Gemini's 4x growth YoY means dedicated tracking is now warranted. GA4 misclassifies 35-70% of AI referrals as Direct due to stripped referrer headers. Custom regex channel groups are essential for accurate attribution.

## Details

Livesov (2026) estimates total AI surface users at 2.4B monthly — Google AIO 780M+, ChatGPT 1.1B+, Perplexity 230M+, Claude 120M+, Gemini app 95M+, Grok 60M+, Copilot 50M+. Key insight: ChatGPT has highest per-user engagement, Google has largest reach. For GEO measurement, brands need to track citation share across at least 4 engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) because single-platform tracking misses most opportunities.
