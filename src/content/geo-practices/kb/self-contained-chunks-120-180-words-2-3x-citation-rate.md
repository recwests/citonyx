## Summary

Keep sections between 120-180 words with at least one verifiable fact per 60 words. Each section must be self-contained and quotable without surrounding context — AI chunks at section boundaries.

## Details

Wellows chunk optimization research found 2.3x citation rate for 120-180 word chunks. Princeton GEO study complementary metric: 1:60 fact-to-word ratio yields 4.2x citation rate vs pages above 1:120. For a 2,500-word article, that means 40+ distinct data points. Heading hierarchy is a 2.8x citation multiplier (Discovered Labs). 87% of AI-cited pages use a single H1 tag (SE Ranking). AI retrieval systems use headings as chunk boundaries.
