## Summary

Seer Interactive (Sept 2025, 3,119 queries, 42 organizations, 25.1M organic + 1.1M paid impressions) found AI Overviews reduce organic CTR by 61% (1.76% to 0.61%) and paid CTR by 68% (19.7% to 6.34%). However, brands cited inside the AI Overview earn +35% more organic clicks and +91% more paid clicks than uncited competitors on the same SERPs. Citation is the only defensible position.

## Details

- Organic CTR fell from 1.76% (baseline) to 0.61% (with AIO) — a 65% relative decline.
- Paid CTR fell from 19.7% to 6.34% — a 68% decline.
- Cited brands: +35% organic, +91% paid vs uncited on same SERPs.
- Confirmed by Pew Research panel (68,879 real searches, 900 US adults): 8% click rate with AI summary vs 15% without (47% relative reduction).
- 2026 showed partial CTR rebound to ~2.4% (Seer April 2026 update) but the citation advantage persists.
- Strategic takeaway: uncited = worst position. Being on SERP without AIO citation means suffering full CTR loss.
