## Summary
Google now labels a searcher's Preferred Sources inside AI Overviews and AI Mode answers. People are twice as likely to click a Preferred Source, and more than 345,000 unique sources had been selected by late May 2026. The labels appear when a chosen site naturally surfaces, with more frequent appearances planned.

## Details
This is a rare direct publisher lever inside AI answers. Readers manage picks at google.com/preferences/source; Google expanded the feature to every supported language on April 30, 2026, before bringing it into AI features on May 27. Google documents that the picks work alongside ranking systems to up-rank a site the user asked for but do not override relevance. Action for publishers: actively encourage loyal readers to register the site as a preferred source, and build first-party reader relationships rather than relying on aggregation.
