## Summary

Princeton's GEO study found keyword stuffing ranked dead last among 9 tested methods — actively harmful to AI citation performance. The methods that worked: cite sources, statistics addition, quotation addition. For B2B: 300-word FAQ pages with schema generate more citations than comprehensive 3,000-word articles. E-E-A-T principles transfer to AI: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trustworthiness.

## Details

AI engines prefer factual, attributable information over subjective viewpoints. Case studies need substantial quantitative data to be citable ('reduced failures by 64%' works; 'improved efficiency' doesn't). G2 and Capterra reviews improve AI visibility — these platforms are frequently cited in recommendation queries. Reddit, YouTube, and LinkedIn now function as citation channels with measurable ROI.
