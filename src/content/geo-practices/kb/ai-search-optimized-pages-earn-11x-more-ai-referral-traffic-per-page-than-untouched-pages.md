## Summary
Optimizing pages for AI search pays off in traffic: across 116 WordPress sites and 108,834 pages, optimized pages earned 173.5 AI referral visits per 1,000 pages over 90 days versus 15.8 for untouched pages on the same domains, an 11x lift (LovedByAI, Aug 2026). The gain mostly comes from getting picked up at all — 4.47% of optimized pages earned a visit versus 0.59% of untouched pages.

## Details
- Natural experiment: sites optimized only some pages, leaving optimized and untouched pages side by side on the same domain; homepages excluded; identical 90-day window.
- Optimized pages won on 89 of 102 sites with any AI traffic, and volume tracked time since optimization (dose-response).
- Caveats: observational with no pre-treatment baseline, vendor's own plugin logs, and thin low-priority pages gained nothing.
- Source: LovedByAI (5 Aug 2026).
