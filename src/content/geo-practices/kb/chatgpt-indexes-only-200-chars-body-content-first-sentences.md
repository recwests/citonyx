## Summary
Analysis of ChatGPT's free-tier index found it stores only a page's title and approximately 200 characters of body content — roughly two sentences. Boilerplate or disclaimers appearing before substantive content eat into this limited index space.

## Details
GPO's September 2026 analysis found that ChatGPT's index stores only a page's title and approximately 200 characters of body content. Any boilerplate, generic introductions, brand statements, or navigation text appearing before the first substantive paragraph consumes the most valuable index real estate. On pages where AI citation matters — location pages, product pages, key service pages — the first sentence of real substance should appear as early in the body as possible. Page templates that open with a brand statement or disclaimer before the actual answer waste the space that determines citability. This is separate from ChatGPT's browse mode (which fetches full pages) — this applies to the free-tier default index used for most queries.
