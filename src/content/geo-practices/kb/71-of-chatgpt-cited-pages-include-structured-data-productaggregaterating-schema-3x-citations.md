## Summary
71% of pages cited by ChatGPT include structured data, yet only 18% of ecommerce product pages have complete schema. Product+AggregateRating schema makes products 3x more likely in AI recommendations. FAQPage markup makes pages 3x more citable. JSON-LD must be server-side rendered.

## Details
Veritas/Optivus research (May 2026) confirms schema matters more under GEO than SEO. Highest-impact types: FAQPage (maps to answer extraction), HowTo (step-by-step), Article with complete author/date fields, Organization with sameAs links. 89% of valid schema implementations use JSON-LD. Emerging llms.txt standard is adopted by Anthropic, Cloudflare, Vercel, Cursor, and Mintlify but no major search engine has committed to honoring it as authoritative.
