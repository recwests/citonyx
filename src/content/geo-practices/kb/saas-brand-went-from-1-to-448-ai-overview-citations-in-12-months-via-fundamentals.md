## Summary
A brand-new SaaS domain grew from 1 to 448 Google AI Overview appearances in 12 months (Click Intelligence, March 2025-March 2026). AI visibility arrived as a byproduct of fundamentals: 210 landing pages, topic clustering, 516 referring domains and Domain Rating climbing 0 to 59, while organic traffic rose 206% year over year.

## Details
The campaign never optimized for AI directly; it built technical SEO, schema recommendations, semantic optimization and content clusters from day one. 210 new landing pages plus 57 SEO-focused pages expanded topical authority; Digital PR and link building delivered 516 referring domains. Results moved together: keyword positions 1-3 grew 96 to 866, positions 4-10 253 to 935, monthly organic traffic 10,890 to 33,393. New pages alone drive 14,591 monthly visits and 842 rankings. Authors admit content published early took months to gain rankings and they would accelerate topical expansion sooner. Caveat: agency-published case study on its own client, single brand, no control.
