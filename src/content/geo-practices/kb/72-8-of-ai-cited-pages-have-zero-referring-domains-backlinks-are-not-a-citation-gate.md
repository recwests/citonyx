## Summary
Rankability's August 2026 citation snapshot found 72.8% of AI-cited pages had zero page-level referring domains and one in four came from domains rated below DR 40. Pages cited across eight or more queries were nearly three times as likely to reach the AI top ten, so breadth of relevance beat link volume.

## Details
- From 1,645 AI citations across eleven platforms (rankability.com, August 2026): 72.8% of cited pages had no page-level referring domains, the median cited page had zero, and 25% of cited pages came from domains with a domain rating below 40 (median DR 63).
- Domain authority showed almost no relationship with citation position among pages that were already cited.
- Pages cited across eight or more queries reached the AI top ten 43.3% of the time versus 15.5% for single-query pages.
- Interpretation: backlinks are not a citation prerequisite; relevance breadth across queries matters more. This sits alongside SE Ranking's domain-level finding that heavy referring-domain profiles correlate with more citations, so page-level and domain-level effects should not be conflated.
