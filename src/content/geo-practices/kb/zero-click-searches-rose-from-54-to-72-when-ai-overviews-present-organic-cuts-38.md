## Summary
A randomized field study (Jan-Feb 2026) found AI Overviews reduced outbound organic clicks by 38% on triggered queries. Zero-click searches rose from 54% to 72%. Removing the AI Overview nearly doubled outbound clicks. Brands cited inside AI Overviews earn 35% more organic clicks than non-cited competitors.

## Details
The study randomly assigned users to see or hide AI Overviews during real searches, isolating the causal effect. Conductor's analysis of 21.9M queries found 25.11% trigger AI Overviews as of early 2026. BrightEdge research documented 58% year-over-year increase in AI Overview coverage between Feb 2025 and Feb 2026. In B2B tech specifically, AI Overview presence jumped from 36% to 82% in one year. Google's own data shows impressions rising while clicks and CTR fall.
