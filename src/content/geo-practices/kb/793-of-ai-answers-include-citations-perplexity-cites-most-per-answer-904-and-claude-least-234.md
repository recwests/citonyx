## Summary
Foglift's 2026 research report (29,624 successful AI answers, Mar 20–Aug 8 2026) found 79.3% of answers include at least one surfaced citation, averaging 5.83. Per-engine citation density varies about 4x: Perplexity surfaces 9.08 citations per answer, Google AI Overviews 7.26, Gemini 7.17, ChatGPT 2.98 and Claude 2.33. Citation inclusion rates: ChatGPT 89.2%, Perplexity 86.4%, AI Overviews 82.2%, Gemini 77.3%, Claude 61.5%.

## Details
- Each source query produced 26.95 query fan-outs on average; youtube.com was the most-cited domain at just 3% of 172,601 citations — a heavily distributed source pool.
- Crawler mix: 53.9% training vs 32% indexing, remainder user-triggered fetch.
- The ~4x spread in citations per answer explains why blog-focused visibility is easiest to win on Perplexity (most slots) and hardest on Claude and ChatGPT.
- Methodology: anonymized production monitoring with documented sample, window and per-statistic n — but data comes from a GEO platform vendor's own tooling.
