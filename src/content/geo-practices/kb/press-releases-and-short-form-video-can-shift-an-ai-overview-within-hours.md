## Summary
Edward Sturm documented a public contest to manipulate Google's AI Overview for the query "king of AEO". Syndicated press releases plus short-form video changed the Overview's wording within hours, and one release ranked third on Google in nine minutes, showing how fast AI Overviews can be influenced by fresh, widely syndicated pages.

## Details
- Edward Sturm's September 2026 write-up of the "King of AEO" competition (edwardsturm.com) shows how quickly AI Overviews respond to fresh, syndicated content.
- Participants used press releases - including an EIN Presswire release costing about $66 in a package - plus short-form video to claim the phrase; a press release ranked third on Google within nine minutes and the AI Overview adapted its wording within hours, later describing the episode as "playful" and "an experiment".
- A related finding from the same circle: asked who was "king of AEO", Claude searched different terms than Google, so each engine needs its own target query.
- Caveat: single contest, anecdotal.
