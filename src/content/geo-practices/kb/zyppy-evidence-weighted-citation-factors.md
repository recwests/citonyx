## Summary

Cyrus Shepard's Zyppy Signal meta-analysis (May 7, 2026) synthesized 54 experiments, patents, and case studies scoring 23 AI citation factors 0–10. Top: URL accessibility 9.5, search rank 9.4, fan-out rank 9.3, preview control 9.2, query-answer match 9.2. Bottom: LLMs.txt at 2.0/10 — lowest of all factors with no credible evidence it influences citations.

## Details

The 23-factor ranking grouped by category: Technical — URL accessibility 9.5, search rank 9.4, fan-out rank 9.3, preview control 9.2, topic cluster ranking 8.9. Content — query-answer match 9.2, answer near top 8.8, AI-ready structure 8.6, self-contained passages 8.0, internal citations 8.0, freshness 7.0, structured data 5.6. Off-page — brand web mentions (0.664 Ahrefs correlation). Lowest: LLMs.txt 2.0/10. Cross-validated against Ahrefs (75K brands, 17M citations, 863K keywords) and Seer (5.47M queries). Practical takeaway: fix crawlability first, build brand mentions second, structure content third. Skip llms.txt.
