## Summary
Entity authority remains the top AI visibility factor. The AEO Partners 2026 report (5,000+ prompts, 1,000 sites, 50 verticals) ranks entity authority first; only ~15% of brands have dedicated AEO strategies, and early adopters saw 470% visibility gains over 12 months. Clean entity data outperforms strong backlink profiles.

## Details
- Sources: The AEO Partners 2026 AI Search Visibility Report; Jingrey technical GEO tactics.
- Action: keep brand name and description consistent across the web (entity consistency), use Organization schema with sameAs, build knowledge-graph presence, own concepts via pillar content, map entities (brands, authors, concepts).
- Yext: 86% of 6.8M AI citations come from brand-managed sources (first-party 44%, listings 42%); structured entity data raised small-brand appearances by 36%.
