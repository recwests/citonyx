## Summary
5WPR's AI Platform Citation Source Index 2026, built from 680 million citations, claims the top 15 domains capture 68% of AI citation share. Rerunning the identical math on 312 buyer-intent citations gave 23%, spread across 220 distinct domains, 77% of which appeared only once.

## Details
Reddit was the single most-cited domain in both datasets but only 3.8% of buyer-intent sources, because engines cite roughly 17 domains per answer: presence is not dominance. The gap comes from query mix, since aggregate indexes blend informational with commercial queries, from frequency-versus-share counting, and from volatility, as ChatGPT's Reddit share reportedly fell from about 60% to 10% in six weeks.
