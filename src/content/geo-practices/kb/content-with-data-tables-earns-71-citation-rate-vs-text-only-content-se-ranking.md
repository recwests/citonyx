## Summary

Pages with data tables and specific factual claims earn 71% citation rates vs text-only alternatives. Cited pages average 13.75 list sections (Sellm 2025 study of 400K+ pages). Structure is critical: modular content blocks with descriptive headings function as standalone extractable units. The Princeton GEO research (KDD 2024) showed nested schema + structural clarity gives 40% higher visibility.

## Details

Content types that get cited most: how-to guides with specific steps + data, comparison pages with structured tables, FAQ pages with schema markup, industry state-of-the-market reports with year-specific data. Content types that underperform: opinion pieces without factual backing, generic case studies lacking quantitative data, press releases without unique info. 3 focused 500-word posts with high extractability outperform one 3,000-word article.
