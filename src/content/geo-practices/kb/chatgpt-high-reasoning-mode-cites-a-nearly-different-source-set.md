## Summary
Across 100 prompts, Semrush found ChatGPT minimal reasoning ran 245 web searches while high reasoning ran 1,130 — nearly 5x more. Only 25.6% of cited domains were shared between the two modes. Citations per response nearly doubled from 2.6 to 4.5, and Reddit and UGC sources lost roughly half their citation share in thinking mode.

## Details
- Government, academic and official documentation sources quadrupled from 1.9% to 8.8% of citations when reasoning increased, while UGC and review sites shrank from 14.3% to 6%.
- The 25.6% overlap means a single tracking bucket hides most of the citation landscape; multi-criteria and comparison queries diverge most between modes.
- Recommendation: split citation tracking by prompt complexity, since high-reasoning queries favor authoritative, cited source sets over UGC-heavy ones.
