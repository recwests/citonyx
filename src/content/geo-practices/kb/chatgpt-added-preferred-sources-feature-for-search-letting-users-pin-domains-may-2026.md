## Summary
ChatGPT Search introduced a Preferred Sources feature (May 2026) allowing users to pin specific domains for prioritized citation. If users pin your domain, it consistently appears in ChatGPT Search answers for relevant queries, bypassing normal citation competition.

## Details
- Mirrors Google's Preferred Sources feature (previously available in AI Mode/AI Overviews).
- For brands with strong customer loyalty, prompting users to add the brand domain as a Preferred Source in ChatGPT can create a persistent citation advantage.
- The feature is user-driven, so brand awareness and user activation campaigns become a direct GEO lever.
- Impact: shifts some citation power from content optimization to brand loyalty.
