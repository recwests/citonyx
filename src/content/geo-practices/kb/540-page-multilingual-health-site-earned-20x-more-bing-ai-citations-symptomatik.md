## Summary
SEO Savages built Symptomatik, a 540-page health site in English, Polish and Spanish, in six weeks. Bing's AI answers went from 102 citations a day in June 2026 to 2,073 in 9-15 September, with Bing clicks up 6.3x, while Google still ranked the four-month-old site at average position 59.3.

## Details
The play: 98 lab-test explainers of about 2,000 words with reference lists and pre-checked sources, 15 validated mental-health questionnaires with scoring, medical and FAQ structured data on every health page, and per-language hreflang. Grounding queries show name-the-reference demand: 'pcl 5 scoring' alone drew 1,934 citations at 12.4% share, and half of the top ten queries were Spanish. Vendor case study of a single site.
