## Summary
Perplexity cites at least one source in 60.6% of its answers versus 32.6% for ChatGPT, 32.4% for Claude and 43.5% for Gemini, per ZenoX Media's study of 543 buying-intent answers across four engines (June-July 2026). About a third (34.3%) of citation losses happened because the AI never searched the web at all.

## Details
- 543 real answers to 193 commercial-intent queries, run in fresh consumer browser sessions (not APIs), June 13 - July 16 2026.
- Loss reasons on 265 answers: 34.3% no web search, 23.2% page not retrieved, 17.2% forum consensus won, 14.1% cited but not recommended.
- Implication: Perplexity and Gemini reward fresh, crawlable, source-shaped pages; ChatGPT and Claude reward entity presence and training-data reputation.
- Reddit out-cited YouTube, Wikipedia and social platforms, coming within two citations of Google's own pages.
- Caveat: single-vendor sample; engines change retrieval behavior between passes, so treat the snapshot as dated.
