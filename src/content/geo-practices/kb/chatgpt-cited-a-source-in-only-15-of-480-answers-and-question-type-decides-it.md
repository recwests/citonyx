## Summary
In one 2026-09-01 capture, Hendricks sent 480 questions to Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini and logged 16,069 citations. ChatGPT cited a source in just 72 of 480 answers (15%): provider-discovery 35/48 and cost 34/48, but zero of 48 on selection criteria, explainer, problem diagnosis, timing, trust and definition questions.

## Details
- The other three engines cited on 93-100% of their eligible answers, so the zeroes are a ChatGPT behaviour, not a panel artefact.
- When ChatGPT did cite, its answers averaged 6,194 characters versus 2,303 when it did not, so uncited answers are not failures: the model answered from memory and no brand could be cited.
- Because the question-type mix was identical across all eight industries (six questions per type), the effect is a property of question type, not industry.
- Practical rule: a brand can only win citations on prompts that trigger retrieval, and definitional or explainer prompts currently give ChatGPT no citation surface.
