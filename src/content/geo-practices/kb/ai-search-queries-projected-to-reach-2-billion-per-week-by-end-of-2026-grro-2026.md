## Summary
GRRO (Feb 2026): AI search queries are projected to surpass 2 billion per week by end of 2026, up from 800M/week. 527% YoY growth. 40% of Gen Z uses AI search as their first research tool. 97% of businesses have measurable AI visibility gaps.

## Details
- The 527% YoY growth rate coupled with 40% Gen Z first-use signals a permanent shift in search behavior.
- Google is projected to lose 15-20% of informational queries to AI platforms by end of 2026.
- AI search referrals convert 4.4× better than traditional organic.
- The window for early-mover advantage is narrowing — brands that establish AI visibility now will compound returns as the channel grows 2-3× by year-end.
