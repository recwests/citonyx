## Summary
3RD analysed 5,926,953 citations for 100 brands and found a median of only 6.8% pointed to the brand's own domains, with the middle half of brands at 3.4%-10.1%. Google's surfaces cited owned pages more often: AI Mode 12.0% and AI Overviews 9.7%, against ChatGPT 5.8% and Claude 3.2%.

## Details
- Per engine: AI Mode 12.0%, AI Overviews 9.7%, Gemini 6.0%, ChatGPT 5.8%, Perplexity 5.5%, Copilot 4.6%, Claude 3.2%.
- Competitors' own sites took 8.3% of citations against 7.3% for the tracked brand, and for 41 of 100 brands rivals were cited more often than the brand itself.
- Source mix: review and comparison sites 4.9%, government bodies 4.7%, Reddit 2.2%, news 1.0%, YouTube 0.6%, Wikipedia 0.3%, with roughly two-thirds in a niche long tail.
- Caveat: conflicts with Profound's 57% owned share because prompts, categories and the definition of a brand site differ; vendor-collected, Nordic-leaning sample.
