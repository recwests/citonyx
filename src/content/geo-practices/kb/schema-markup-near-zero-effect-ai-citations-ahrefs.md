## Summary
Ahrefs conducted the industry's first controlled schema study: 1,885 pages that added JSON-LD schema between Aug 2025-Mar 2026 were matched against ~4,000 controls. Result: AI Overview citations actually dropped 4.6%, AI Mode rose only 2.4% and ChatGPT 2.2% — all within random noise. Schema is a supporting signal (rich results, agent parseability, discovery), not a direct citation lever.

## Details
Google AI Overviews: -4.6% citation change after adding schema vs controls (Ahrefs, 2026). Google AI Mode: +2.4% — too small to separate from random noise. ChatGPT: +2.2% — too small to separate from random noise. Why cited sites still have schema: they tend to be well-built sites with mature SEO programs. Schema rides along with what actually earns citations — authority, depth, freshness, clean structure. Schema still does three jobs: (1) earns rich results in Google/Bing that feed AI search, (2) aids agent parseability when structured data is read, (3) helps discovery and indexing for pages AI systems haven't seen. Complementary finding (NAV43, 2026): generic/minimal schema performs worse than no schema at all (41.6% citation rate vs 59.8% for no schema). Only attribute-rich, fully populated schema outperforms. Don't remove schema, but stop expecting it to move citation metrics — focus effort on content quality, authority signals and entity consistency instead.
