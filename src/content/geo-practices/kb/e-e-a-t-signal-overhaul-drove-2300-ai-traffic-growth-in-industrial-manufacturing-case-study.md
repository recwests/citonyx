## Summary
Diggity Marketing added E-E-A-T signals (named author bios with credentials, publication dates, editorial standards pages, reviewer disclosures) plus Organization/Author/Article structured data site-wide. AI Overview citation rate on target keywords rose from near-zero to consistent inclusion, and total AI-sourced traffic grew 23x over 6 months. Manufacturing is a strong test case because most competitors are weak on these signals.

## Details
- What changed: E-E-A-T signal overhaul — named author bios with verifiable credentials, publication dates, editorial standards pages, reviewer disclosures, and Organization/Author/Article schema on every canonical page.
- Result: AI Overview citation rate rose from near-zero to consistent inclusion; total AI-sourced traffic grew 23x over 6 months.
- What transfers: E-E-A-T signals are cheap to implement (days, not months) and produce measurable AI-visibility lift, especially in industries with low baseline authority signal.
- Caveat: single agency case study with publication bias — brands that invested in GEO and saw no lift tend not to publish.
