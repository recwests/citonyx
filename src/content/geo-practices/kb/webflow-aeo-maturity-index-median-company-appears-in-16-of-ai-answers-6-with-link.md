## Summary

Webflow's AEO Maturity Index (2026) analyzed 2,000 websites across content, technical GEO readiness, brand authority, and measurement. The median company appeared in just 16% of relevant AI answers and in a citation with a link only 6% of the time. Large companies had 2× higher mention rates, 60% higher citation rates, and 80% higher share of voice.

## Details

Webflow's Guy Yalif: fundamental SEO basics (broken links, missing metadata, stale content) were the most common culprits for underperforming GEO. The Maturity Model scores four categories: published content, technical GEO readiness, brand authority, and measurement. Companies scoring higher on all four saw proportionally higher mention and citation rates. This provides a baseline: appearing in just 16% of answers is the median, meaning most brands have substantial room for improvement before hitting any ceiling.
