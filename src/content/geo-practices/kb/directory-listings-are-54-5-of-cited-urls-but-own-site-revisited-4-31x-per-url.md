## Summary
Across 680M citations, directory listings are 54.53% of all distinct URLs the engines cite, but a brand's own website gets revisited more per URL — 4.31 times vs 2.46 for listings (TLSOLS synthesis of Profound/Averi data). Fresh, structure-extractable owned pages are the higher-leverage citation asset.

## Details
TLSOLS, an SEO agency, reviewed 500+ real prompts and the largest published citation studies (Yext 17.2M, Profound/Averi 680M, Slate HQ 300K). Directories dominate the distinct-URL pool at 54.53%, yet repeated retrieval favors owned content: a first-party URL is revisited 4.31x per URL vs 2.46x for a listing. Perplexity pulls much of its directory share from MapQuest (364K citations) and TripAdvisor (239K). Practical read: maintain consistent structured listings for the breadth play, but invest in citable, updated pages on your own domain for repeated AI retrieval. Caveat: aggregate of vendor studies; agency synthesis rather than a single controlled experiment.
