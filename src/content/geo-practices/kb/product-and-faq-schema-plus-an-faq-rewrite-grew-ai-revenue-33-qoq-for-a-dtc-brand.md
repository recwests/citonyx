## Summary
WITHIN's DTC personal-care client made three changes: product and FAQ schema on all product pages, FAQ answers rewritten around long-tail questions shoppers ask AI platforms, and internal links from high-intent buying guides to priority products. Over a year the brand reported +78% visibility in AI answers, +83% ChatGPT traffic and +33% AI revenue quarter over quarter.

## Details
- Order of work mattered in this case: technical structure first so platforms can parse products, then content rewrites, then link equity routed to priority SKUs.
- The FAQ rewrite targeted question phrasing consumers actually use with assistants rather than site-search phrasing.
- Seasonality decided which products the guides point at during each period, so internal links change across the year.
- Caveat: agency-published case for an unnamed client, no control group, self-reported and rounded metrics; read it as an implementation pattern, not proof of lift.
