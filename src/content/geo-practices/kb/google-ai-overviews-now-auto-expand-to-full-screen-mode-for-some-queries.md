## Summary
Google confirmed on Aug 28 2026 that AI Overviews now dynamically expand for some queries, replacing the compact snippet with a full-page AI Mode experience. This eliminates the Show More button, pushes organic results below the fold, and reveals the AI Mode prompt box by default — accelerating Google's push toward AI as the default search experience.

## Details
Google spokesperson: 'For some queries, AI Overviews may dynamically expand for topics where our systems determine it's most useful for people' (Search Engine Land, Aug 28 2026). Chris Long first spotted the change, noting the AI Mode prompt box appears by default for some queries — previously only visible after clicking into AI Mode. Google cancels the expansion if the user has already scrolled past the AI Overview to avoid losing reading position. For definitions queries, AI Overviews consistently take up the entire screen. Implication: SEOs tracking CTR on top-position pages need to monitor for expanded AIO queries separately — the click loss from expanded AIOs is significantly larger than from compact ones. This is distinct from the May 2026 updates (inline links, hover previews, subscription labels); this is a new structural change to how AIOs render.
