## Summary
Adobe Analytics data (EMARKETER, Aug 19 2026): AI-referred traffic to US retail sites grew 62% YoY in July, marking the 11th consecutive month AI conversion outpaced non-AI sources. AI visitors convert 60% better, spend 59% more time on-site, browse 23% more pages per visit, and are 33% less likely to bounce. This is a sustained structural shift, not a one-month spike.

## Details
AI traffic to retail sites grew 138% YoY in May 2026 and has grown 1,324% since Oct 2024 when Adobe began tracking (Adobe Analytics, Digital Commerce 360). In May 2026, AI-referred visitors were 15% more engaged and spent 53% more time on retailers' sites — the largest engagement difference since tracking began. Shoppers add items to cart at higher rates from AI referrals than non-AI. Walmart's AI assistant 'Sparky' users have 35% higher AOV; Amazon Alexa for Shopping sessions convert at 44% (Sensor Tower data). However, less than 17% of US internet users use genAI weekly for shopping — adoption is growing but remains a small share of total traffic. Implication: even a small volume of AI traffic delivers disproportionate value — GEO ROI should be measured by revenue per visit, not just session count.
