## Summary

Pages with comprehensive, well-implemented schema markup are roughly 3x more likely to earn AI citations. But generic boilerplate schema actually lowers citation odds to 41.6% vs 59.8% for sites with no schema — precision matters more than presence.

## Details

Audit of 500+ sites found comprehensive schema pages are ~3x more likely to earn AI citations. Separately, a Growth Marshal study of 730 sites found generic boilerplate schema drops citation probability to 41.6% vs 59.8% for no schema. Key schema priority order: (1) Organization — foundational entity anchor; (2) FAQPage — highest citation impact, pre-structured answers AI can extract verbatim; (3) Article/BlogPosting — signals authorship and freshness; (4) Person — verifies author credibility; (5) Dataset — makes original research machine-readable. Critical underused properties: sameAs (links to LinkedIn, Crunchbase, G2 — fewer than 3 links is a missed opportunity) and knowsAbout (declares topical expertise). The connected graph approach — using @id to link Article→Person→Organization — changes how crawlers interpret the site.
