## Summary

Universal AI optimization is a myth. ChatGPT pulls from Wikipedia (47.9%), Perplexity from Reddit (46.7%), Google AIO from diversified sources. Optimize per-platform: Perplexity first for niche sites, ChatGPT via Reddit seeding, Gemini via on-site expertise, Google AIO via traditional SEO.

## Details

GNW Consulting's honest assessment: anyone claiming universal ranking factors is 'making it up.' Known signals that are stable: authority/recency weights, specificity of claims, structural cleanliness, off-site ecosystems, first-party data. Unknown: exact weighting of signal types, how signals interact, citation half-life by category, displacement cost. Four investment principles: optimize for stable factors, test in actual buyer environment, watch trend not level, hedge across platforms.
