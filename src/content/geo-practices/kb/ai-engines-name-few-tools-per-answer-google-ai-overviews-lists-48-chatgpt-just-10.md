## Summary
Foglift's June 2026 benchmark across five engines found tool recommendations are downstream of a source layer: Otterly (58%), Profound (54%), and Semrush (49%) dominated share of voice. Engines disagree sharply — Google AI Overviews named 4.8 tools per answer versus 1.0 for ChatGPT. A brand absent from the cited source layer rarely appears in recommendations.

## Details
The July refresh (536 answers, July 10-19) mapped the source layer: YouTube dominated with 603 citation URLs, ahead of Otterly (243), Reddit (124), and Profound (106). Top-15 source domains split 52.5% vendor-owned versus 47.5% off-vendor platforms. Implication: getting named in roundups, reviewer blogs, and aggregators is the mechanism by which a tool enters an AI answer; own pages help but third-party source coverage is part of the recommendation graph. General SEO suites (Semrush, Ahrefs) now appear at similar rates to dedicated AI-visibility tools.
