## Summary
ChatGPT and Perplexity share only ~11% of cited domains. Averi's 680M citation audit and CiteMetrix's State of AI Search 2026 both confirm the figure. Reddit accounts for 46.7% of Perplexity top-10 citation share (vs ~21% for AI Overviews, ~5% for Gemini), and even Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode share just 13.7% of cited URLs. Sites with 32,000+ referring domains are 3.5x more likely to be cited.

## Details
- Averi (March 2026): 680M AI citations, 11% domain overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity; Passionfruit found 12% across 3 engines.
- Alexandrya 2026 benchmarks (340 B2B brands, 4,200 queries): Perplexity cites 34% avg, ChatGPT 21%. Top quartile: 71% on Perplexity vs 52% on ChatGPT. Only 12% of brands maintain >50% consistency across platforms.
- CiteMetrix: ChatGPT's Reddit citation share dropped from ~60% to 10% in two weeks (Sept 2025); Reddit = 46.7% of Perplexity top-10 share. Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode share only 13.7% of cited URLs.
- Superlines documented up to 615x citation volume variance for the same brand between platforms; one-size-fits-all GEO does not work — measure each engine separately.
- Caveat: CiteMetrix sells its own monitoring tool, so vendor bias applies.
