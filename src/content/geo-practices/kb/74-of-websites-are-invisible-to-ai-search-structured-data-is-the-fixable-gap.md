## Summary
Most of the web is AI-blind: 74.2% of 850,000+ audited sites are Invisible or Low Visibility to AI search. SearchScore's SAVI report shows technical health averaging 70.1 while AI visibility averages just 34.1 — and structured data is the lowest-scoring, most fixable category at 23.1/100.

## Details
- AI-Ready sites are ~0.021% of the corpus (about 1 in 4,900); no site has yet reached the Verified tier (90+), and the current ceiling is 86.6.
- Large brands score poorly (WSJ 12, Reddit 13) while a small Scottish CBD retailer scores 74 — AI visibility is structural, not brand-size-driven.
- Organization schema is absent from roughly 9 in 10 websites; adding valid JSON-LD takes hours and is the highest-leverage action.
- The average AI visibility score dropped from 41.4 to 34 as the audit expanded into the long tail — most sites simply aren't built for AI retrieval.
