## Summary

Perplexity's real-time web retrieval architecture has no knowledge cutoff. 82% of its cited content was published within the last 30 days, and content under 30 days old is cited at roughly 3.2x the rate of older pages (Otterly.ai 2026). Year signals in titles and headings ('2026') improve citation rates by ~30%. Fresh content within hours of indexing can earn citations.

## Details

- Perplexity averages 21.87 citations per response — highest of any major AI platform.
- Favors pages with question-format H2/H3 headings, proprietary statistics, named sources, and verifiable methodology.
- Reddit accounts for 46.7% of Perplexity's top citation sources; upvoted community threads are treated as independent corroboration, so a single new Reddit thread with specific numbers can outrank a two-year-old guide.
- Practical cadence: refresh high-value pages or publish a fresh linked discussion at least monthly to stay inside the freshness window.
- Consequence: Perplexity visibility is volatile week-to-week — track trends, not single checks. Freshness weighting on ChatGPT is moderate, so a Perplexity-first refresh strategy does not transfer proportionally to other engines.
- Unlike ChatGPT's static training data — Perplexity performs live web search per query.
