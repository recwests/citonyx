## Summary
Surfer's September 2026 top-50 lists are shaped by its own users: marketing and SEO domains earned 14.6% of ChatGPT's top-50 citations, Semrush and Ahrefs alone 7.9%, while six Polish domains took 8.2% of Perplexity's. Public citation rankings describe panels, not the open web.

## Details
- Semrush took 20,992 ChatGPT citations and Ahrefs 17,268; on the Perplexity list allegro.pl, ceneo.pl, gov.pl, oferteo.pl, panoramafirm.pl, skapiec.pl and znanylekarz.pl all appeared.
- The top 50 captured only 8.4% of ChatGPT and 9.0% of Perplexity citations, so most of the distribution sits outside the published table anyway.
- Practical read: build source lists from your own prompt set and locale, and check a tracker's customer mix before copying an industry top-50.
- Caveat: Surfer discloses this bias openly; other vendors may not.
