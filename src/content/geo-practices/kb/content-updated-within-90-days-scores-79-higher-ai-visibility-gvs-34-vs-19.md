## Summary
Freshness is the largest single-variable visibility gap in Profound's Q1 2026 GVS dataset: pages updated within 90 days hold a median Generative Visibility Score of 34 versus 19 for pages untouched 180+ days, a 79% premium that applies across AI platforms.

## Details
- Substantive edits (new facts, updated statistics), not cosmetic tweaks, drive the lift; citation authority decays over time and the gap compounds.
- Corroborating data: Authoritas Q1 2026 found structured-data-first page redesigns produced a median 31% AI Overview inclusion increase within 8 weeks.
- Consistent with Kevin Indig's finding (via Writer, July 2026) that pages not updated quarterly are 3x more likely to lose AI citations entirely.
- Practical ordering: refresh high-traffic, already-crawled pages first - existing authority amplifies freshness ROI far more than publishing new pages.
