## Summary
In 3RD's Q4 2026 sample, 37 of 60 brands (62%) had a different competitor leading on different assistants for the same prompts, and 31 of 60 led on some assistants but not others. Across 83 brands tracking three or more assistants, the median best-to-worst gap was 15.7 points, a 2.1x ratio.

## Details
- Recommendation leaders also differed by assistant for 27 of 57 brands (47%).
- Directional generosity: Gemini was the most generous assistant for 39 of 77 brands tracking it, ChatGPT the least generous for 28 of 99; the authors say this is not a general ranking.
- Action: read per-model results before any blended score, and weight effort toward the assistants your buyers actually use.
- Caveat: brands choose which assistants they track, so model mix varies by brand, and the sample leans Nordic.
