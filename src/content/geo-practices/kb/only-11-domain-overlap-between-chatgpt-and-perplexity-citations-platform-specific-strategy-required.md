## Summary

A 2026 study of 680M citations across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity found only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity. Each platform operates on fundamentally different citation logic. ChatGPT favors Wikipedia (7.8%) and encyclopedic sources via Bing+training data hybrid. Perplexity favors Reddit (46.7% of top sources) via real-time multi-API retrieval. Google AI Overviews favors YouTube and organic top-10. A brand invisible on one platform may be well-cited on another.

## Details

- 680M citations analyzed (5WPR/Profound, 2026): 11% cross-platform domain overlap.
- ChatGPT top source: Wikipedia (7.8% of all citations). Source philosophy: encyclopedic authority + training data depth.
- Perplexity top source: Reddit (6.6%). Source philosophy: real-time community + recency.
- Google AI Overviews top source: YouTube + Reddit (2.2% each). Balanced social + professional.
- Google AI Mode and Google AI Overviews cite same URLs only 13.7% of the time (Tinuiti Q2 2026).
- ChatGPT cites brands 0.59% of the time vs Perplexity 13.05% — a 22x gap.
- Practical takeaway: audit each platform separately. Optimize content type per platform — Wikipedia-style for ChatGPT, fresh community content for Perplexity, structured extractable content for Google AI.
