## Summary

Add at least one semantic HTML comparison table per page. Tables encode entity-attribute relationships in a format requiring zero interpretation by AI models, yielding 400% more citations than equivalent prose.

## Details

Bigeye Agency 2026 analysis and TryProfound found comparison pages with semantic HTML tables achieve 67% citation rate. Lily Ray (VP SEO, Amsive): 'Tables are the single most underused citation asset. AI models parse structured tabular data with near-perfect accuracy compared to 60-70% accuracy on equivalent prose paragraphs.' Lists appear on 80% of all AI-cited pages (SE Ranking 129K domain study). Semrush 304K URL study: listicle-format content accounts for ~50% of top AI citations.
