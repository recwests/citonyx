## Summary
Google AI Mode is the most extreme zero-click surface: 93% of sessions end with zero external clicks (Quantumrun). Overall 60% of Google searches in 2026 end without a click, and when AI Overviews appear the top organic result loses 58% of its clicks. Influence becomes invisible in standard web analytics.

## Details
- Data from Quantumrun (via CiteGap), SparkToro, Ahrefs, Semrush, and Marketech APAC; zero-click rate averaged 83% when AI Overviews appeared.
- Implication: the brand is recommended but no click is attributed — shift the KPI mix from clicks to presence in answers (mention, citation, recommendation), brand consideration and share of voice.
- Seer Interactive: cited brands earn 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks than uncited competitors — citation is the only defensible position on those queries.
- 94% of B2B buyers used generative AI during purchase (6sense); 68.94% of websites now receive some AI traffic (SE Ranking).
