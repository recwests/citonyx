## Summary
Index Lab tested five AI visibility tools (Rankshift, Peec AI, Lua, Searchable, Semrush) and found engine coverage is a commercial pricing lever, not a capability gap: Peec caps plans at 3 of 7 engines, Semrush is marketed at 7 but reviewers count 3, in US English only. None explain why you are missing.

## Details
Tested July 2026 with paid plans. Rankshift (about 69 EUR/mo) is the value pick with full engine list on every tier; Peec AI (89 EUR/mo) reads the real interface rather than APIs but caps engines and does monitoring only; Searchable (about 50 USD/mo) tracks ChatGPT only on entry tier; Semrush effective floor about 239 USD/mo per domain with volatile metrics and reported 3 tracked engines. The shared gap: every tool reports that you are absent from an answer, but none diagnose why (stale page, lost source, competitor definitive guide). Practical takeaway: the subscription is the cheap part — content, citations, entity consistency and first-party evidence change the number, and that work stays manual. Caveat: vendor-authored comparison; prices verified July 2026.
