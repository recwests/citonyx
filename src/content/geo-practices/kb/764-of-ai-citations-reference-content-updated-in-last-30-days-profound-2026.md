## Summary
76.4% of Profound-tracked citations reference content updated in last 30 days. Ahrefs study of 17M citations confirms AI assistants strongly prefer fresh content. Content 0-30 days old has peak citation potential; 90-180 day decay begins; 365+ day severe decay for commercial content. Perplexity cites sources 16x fresher than Google for high-velocity topics.

## Details
Cosmetic date changes without substantive updates harm retrieval trust. Refresh cadence: Tier 1 (monthly) for pricing/comparison pages; Tier 2 (quarterly) for how-to guides; Tier 3 (biannual) for conceptual definitions. Seer Interactive (July 2026): 'publish and forget loses, publish and maintain wins.' AI crawlers compare page snapshots over time. The 'Lazy Gap' — 76-day window where Perplexity prefers fresh content before Google's authority signals dominate.
