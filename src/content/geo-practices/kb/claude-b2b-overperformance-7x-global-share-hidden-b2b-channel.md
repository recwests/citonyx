## Summary

B2B companies should prioritize Claude alongside ChatGPT. Claude's 7x B2B overperformance means it captures disproportionate B2B research traffic despite low global market share. Most GEO strategies focus only on ChatGPT and miss this channel.

## Details

Axis Intelligence AI Search Statistics Report (June 2026). Goodie Wave 2 study (Mar-Apr 2026 average). ChatGPT: 62.6% B2B share vs 76.85% global (0.81x underperformance). Claude: 18.5% B2B share vs 2.66% global (7.0x overperformance). Gemini: 10.6% B2B vs 9.00% global (1.18x). Perplexity: 7.1% B2B vs 7.73% global (0.92x). AI traffic grew 16x from 2024 to 2026. ChatGPT referral traffic surged 157.7% week-over-week after May 7, 2026 link update, with homepage referrals up 354.7%.
