## Summary
On 15 October 2026 Google updated AI Mode and AI Overviews to show inline source links higher in responses more often, plus a dedicated top-of-answer link block for one or two official pages, prominent article carousels, and clickable creator names in video results.

## Details
Google says inline links are being substantially increased and surfaced earlier, and it is testing a section at the top of a response listing the one or two pages needed to complete a task or find exact information, with context on why each link helps. Timely-article carousels highlighting Preferred Sources are rolling out globally, and Google reports Preferred Sources usage grew more than 7x since the start of 2026, with over 400 publications in its News AI licensing pilot.

- Which pages qualify: booking, refund policy, application, official documentation and exact specifications are candidates for the new top slot; reference and explainer pages are less likely to be the right page for a transactional query.
- Search Console now emails the preferred-source subscriber count, giving publishers a first native signal on allowlist growth.
- Caveat: Google made no click-volume commitment, so treat this as a placement change to monitor rather than a traffic fix. The announcement is Google's own blog; secondary reports (Nick Fox attribution, pilot counts) are press coverage of the same update.
