## Summary

Pages with nested schema AND structural clarity (prose organized to confirm schema claims) achieve 40% higher visibility in AI responses vs schema alone, per Princeton's KDD-2024 GEO paper.

## Details

The Princeton GEO research team (Aggarwal et al.) published at KDD-2024 demonstrating that schema divorced from aligned prose has limited citation impact. The 'structural clarity' component — prose organized to confirm the claims that schema marks up — is the underexplored half. Misaligned implementation (FAQ schema with answers not visible in page text) reduces citation probability. Entity graph connections (@id links between Article, Person, Organization) are critical but often deferred.
