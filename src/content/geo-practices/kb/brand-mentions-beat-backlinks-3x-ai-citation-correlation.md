## Summary

Brand mentions across the web are the strongest predictor of AI citation at r=0.664, 3x stronger than backlinks (r=0.218) and 4x stronger than Domain Authority (r=0.18). 85% of citation-driving mentions come from third-party sources.

## Details

Six studies covering 2.4M+ data points (AirOps, Indig/Gauge, SE Ranking, Growth Marshal, Princeton GEO, Semrush). The full citation signal hierarchy: brand mentions #1 (r=0.664), entity density 15+/page #2 (4.8x), original data #3 (4.1x), topic clusters 5+ pages #4 (3.2x), freshness updated <30 days #5 (3.2x), page speed FCP<0.4s #6 (3.2x), expert quotes +41%, statistics +40%, comparison tables +400% vs prose. Domain Authority at r=0.18 is nearly irrelevant for AI citation — the biggest structural shift from SEO to GEO.
