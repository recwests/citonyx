## Summary
Kynection reached 20.9% AI share of voice and first place in its B2B category on a domain rating in the high 30s, using a distributed citation portfolio of 71 focused pages rather than a single flagship asset.

## Details
- Intelligent Resourcing's AEO Tracker sample (16 June 2026) recorded Kynection at 20.9% AI share of voice, first in category, ~2.4x the nearest incumbent (8.63%), across 198 monitored buyer prompts, 71 focused content pieces and citations across 25+ URLs.
- By 22 July a live reading showed 29.5% mention rate and 35.2% citation rate across 4,363 runs.
- Mechanism was portfolio breadth: product integration pages, compliance guides, comparisons and technical articles each answered different buyer questions, giving retrieval systems more contexts in which to cite the brand.
- GEO results are point-in-time and drift as models and competitors change.
