## Summary
Gartner 2026: 34% of complex purchases involve AI agents doing preliminary research. Google's SAGE paper (Jan 2026, Google Cloud AI + NYU) reveals agents pull from top-3 ranked pages per sub-query. Four 'shortcut' patterns identified: Information Co-Location (35% of cases — all info in one document), Immediate Answer, Known Entity, Direct Source. Content comprehensiveness, clear structure, strong internal linking = 3 pillars of agentic readiness.

## Details
Three search paradigms now coexist: Traditional (click+browse), Answer-based AI (get cited in summary), Agentic AI (influence AI recommendation). Google's AI Agent Mode (Q1 2026 Labs testing) analyzes up to 50 sources simultaneously, cross-references pricing in real-time. Per McKinsey, agents evaluate 15-40 sources per task vs 3-5 by humans. Key optimization moves: structured comparison data, clear value propositions, specific use case examples, real-time pricing feeds. Brands need to track 'recommendation frequency' not just citation rate.
