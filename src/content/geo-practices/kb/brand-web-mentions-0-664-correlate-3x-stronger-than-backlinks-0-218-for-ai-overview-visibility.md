## Summary
Brand web mentions show a 0.664 Spearman correlation with AI Overview visibility vs 0.218 for backlinks — off-site brand presence matters about 3x more than link building for AI citations. Ahrefs analyzed 75,000 brands; brands in the top 25% for mention volume appeared 169 times in AI answers while the bottom half were near zero.

## Details
- Branded web mentions (0.664), branded anchors (0.527) and branded search volume (0.392) top the factor list; Domain Rating (0.326), referring domains (0.295) and backlinks (0.218) are weaker.
- YouTube mentions correlate even higher (~0.74); the top 25% by mentions average 169 AI Overview mentions vs 14 for the next quartile; 26% of brands have zero.
- Corroborated by Seer Interactive, Shadow, Muck Rack and Zyppy's meta-analysis of 54 experiments.
- Caveat: correlation, not causation — mentions may proxy topical expertise density.
- Action: build mention equity (unlinked mentions in trade press, reviews, directories) as infrastructure; track AI share of voice monthly; treat backlinks and content volume as secondary.
