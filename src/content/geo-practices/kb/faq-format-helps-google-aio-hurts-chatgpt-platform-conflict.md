## Summary

No content-format signal is universally positive. Use declarative headings on ChatGPT-priority pages and FAQ/Q&A formatting on Google-AIO-priority pages. The conflict is about visible content structure, not schema markup.

## Details

TryProfound citation pattern study, Surfer SEO AI citation report, Qwairy Q3 2025 analysis. ChatGPT cites 7.92 sources per response; Perplexity cites 21.87. Only 11% of domains are cited by both ChatGPT and Perplexity for the same query. ChatGPT favors Wikipedia-style neutral prose and penalizes FAQ formatting. Google AI Overviews do the opposite. Gemini shows strongest brand-domain preference at 52.15%. JSON-LD schema markup shows no measurable citation lift on any platform per 2026 Ahrefs causal study.
