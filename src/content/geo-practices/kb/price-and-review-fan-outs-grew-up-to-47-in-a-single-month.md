## Summary
ChatGPT's fan-out vocabulary moved toward commercial evaluation between August and September 2026: price rose from 6.04% to 8.88% of fan-outs, a 47% relative gain, reviews grew by a third, and specifications entered the top 50 for the first time, while definitional terms fell about a fifth.

## Details
Ahrefs' 15-million fan-out dataset, GPT-5.6 Luna, free and logged out.

- The token 2026 stayed most common at 22.9%, up 5.2 points, and official rose to 13.1%.
- Read this as a retrieval brief: pages that state current prices, model numbers and tested verdicts are being looked for by name.
- Category pages without a price or specification table are structurally unable to satisfy these fan-outs. Single-month movement can reverse after a model update, so re-pull the numbers monthly.
