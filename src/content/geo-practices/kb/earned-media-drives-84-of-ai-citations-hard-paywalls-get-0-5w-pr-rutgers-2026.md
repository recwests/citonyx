## Summary

5W Public Relations (July 2026, 40 queries across 6 categories on Claude with real-time search): hard-paywalled publishers (WSJ, FT, Bloomberg) received 0% of AI citations. Metered publishers (NYT, WaPo) also 0%. Open-web publishers captured 91.3%. Muckrack: earned media drives 84% of AI citations. Discovered Labs: AI-perceived domain authority is 6× more influential than the strongest page-level signal.

## Details

Rutgers/Wharton study (April 2026): major publishers blocking LLM crawlers lose ~23% of weekly traffic. AuthorityTech's meta-analysis: URL accessibility/crawlability scores 9.5/10 as a citation signal. AI-perceived domain authority: 9.4/10. Prompt-content alignment: 9.2/10. Structured data: only 5.6/10 after controlling for domain authority. llms.txt: 2.0/10. The highest-leverage activity is building third-party evidence that makes AI engines trust a domain — not adding more schema or optimizing on-page content. Citation requires external corroboration from independent sources.
