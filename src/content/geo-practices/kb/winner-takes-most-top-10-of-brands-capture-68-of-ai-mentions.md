## Summary
AI visibility is severely concentrated: the top 10% of brands capture 68% of all AI mentions in a category while the bottom 50% receive less than 5%. Geonimo's analysis of 50,000+ AI responses across five platforms shows AI search is winner-takes-most, making early visibility investment critical.

## Details
- Platform results diverge widely: only 36 brands maintain top-100 visibility across all four platforms every month (Semrush's 'Universal 36').
- Concentration varies by industry: the top 3 brands hold 82.9% of visibility in News & Media vs 41.4% in Finance — less concentrated categories offer entry points.
- Winner-takes-most means late entrants face compounding citation gaps; the fix is sustained brand mentions, not page volume.
