## Summary
Broworks documented a 90-day AEO program on a 210-page B2B SaaS site. Brand visibility across 120 prompts and four engines rose from 14% to 30%, citation rate from 6% to 19%, and AI referral sessions grew 182%. Organic clicks on the rewritten pages rose only 8.0% and CTR fell from 2.39% to 1.97%.

## Details
The audit found a CDN bot-management rule blocking two AI crawlers despite robots.txt.

- Fixes: 15 rewritten pages plus 3 new ones, Organization, SoftwareApplication and Article schema, tab content moved into prose, and a company facts page.
- Weak spots: best-of prompts 10% to 13%, pricing prompts flat at 10%, llms.txt showed no measurable effect, and no closed revenue in 90 days.
- Single client, vendor-authored; visibility gains did not translate into click gains.
