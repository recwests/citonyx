## Summary
Similarweb's 2026 analysis argues the behavior changing is not whether people search but whether there is a click at all. The KPIs that capture the new environment are citation frequency (how often your content is named in an AI answer), share of model (share of AI responses in your category mentioning your brand) and branded search lift (users who later search you directly). Traffic stays relevant but is no longer complete.

## Details
- Citation presence inside ChatGPT prompts climbed from about 1.6% (Jun 2025) to 6.8% (May 2026); AI Overviews appear on 40%+ of US searches, up from about a third a year earlier.
- The pages AI cites and the pages that get the click are frequently different pages.
- Keep the organic foundation and add the three AI-presence metrics alongside it; similar frameworks now appear in Profound, Semrush AI Toolkit and share-of-answer guidance.
- Source: Similarweb (Aug 2026).
