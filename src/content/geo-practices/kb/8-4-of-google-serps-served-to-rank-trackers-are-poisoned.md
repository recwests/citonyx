## Summary
Nozzle analysed 27.0 million SERPs collected from 20 August to 18 October 2026. Since 13 September, 8.4% were poisoned: complete, normal-looking pages whose organic results had been replaced, peaking at 28.6% on 29 September. A tracked site missed the top 10 on 60.2% of poisoned SERPs, against 2.7% at baseline.

## Details
A SERP counts as poisoned when fewer than half of the keyword's usual sites appear anywhere on it.

- Organic results kept just 22.0% of the previous SERP's sites, while ads (83.7%) and shopping (93.3%) held steady and AI Overview sources kept 38.7%.
- 68.9% of spells recover within three days and 92.7% within two weeks, so most episodes self-heal.
- Diagnostic rule: never report a ranking crash from a single day; cross-check Search Console and re-pull the SERP before escalating.
