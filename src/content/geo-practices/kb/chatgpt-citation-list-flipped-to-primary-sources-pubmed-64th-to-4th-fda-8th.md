## Summary
ChatGPT's most-cited domains rotated toward original sources between March and September 2026. PubMed rose from 64th to 4th, the FDA entered at 8th, and regulators and professional bodies gained, while Reddit citations fell 88%, Wikipedia 80% and YouTube 27%, and explainer sites such as WebMD dropped.

## Details
Ahrefs compared Brand Radar citation data across 2.4 million March answers and 3.8 million September answers, then re-ran the check on about 25,000 prompts present in both months to rule out prompt-set drift; every domain in the chart moved the same direction.

- Reddit fell from about 1 in 5 answers to 1 in 43; Wikipedia from 1 in 7 to 1 in 35.
- Implication: summary and aggregator pages are losing ground to whoever publishes the underlying fact.
- Original research, primary documentation and hands-on testing gain relative value under this regime.
