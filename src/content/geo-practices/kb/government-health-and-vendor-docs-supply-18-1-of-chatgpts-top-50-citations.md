## Summary
In Surfer's September 2026 ChatGPT data, government and health-research sites held 10.1% of top-50 citations and developer or help documentation another 8.0%, 18.1% combined. For factual, regulated or product-support questions, citing the authoritative primary source is the documented citation route.

## Details
- Buckets: PubMed 11,124 citations, gov.uk 9,142, PMC 8,880, fda.gov 6,999, gov.pl 6,824, irs.gov 5,742; developers.google.com 13,707, support.google.com 13,292, learn.microsoft.com 11,888.
- Surfer's advice: on regulated topics quote and link the official source; keep your own documentation current and answer in the first paragraph before elaborating.
- Review and directory sites were the largest single bucket at 23.6% of top-50 citations.
- Caveat: panel effects apply and subdomains are counted separately.
