## Summary

The peer-reviewed Princeton KDD 2024 paper (Aggarwal et al.) tested nine optimization methods on GEO-bench (10,000 queries across 10 engines) and found adding statistics, source citations, and quotations lifts content visibility in AI answers by up to 40%. The 'cite sources' tactic produced a +115.1% relative lift for mid-ranked pages. 2026 case studies confirm the framework across industries.

## Details

Statistics addition (specific numbers, percentages, dates) and quotation addition delivered the biggest visibility gains; adding inline citations to own claims boosted visibility most for pages ranked 5th in the SERPs (+115.1% relative). Follow-up 2026 syntheses still rank these as the highest-evidence content-level levers. Caveats: gains depend on baseline quality, retrieval engines have shifted since 2024, and the methods work best on content that already ranks. Practice: add one attributed statistic per 150-200 words and quote named experts with sources.
