## Summary
An Aug 2026 Orion study of 35,000 Google-top-20 pages found entity-optimized content earns 2.4x more AI citations than keyword-first pages, controlling for backlinks and domain authority. Citation rates: entity-only 34.2%, keyword-only 14.3%, both 41.7%, neither 8.1%. Perplexity rewards entities most (3.1x), then Claude 2.6x, ChatGPT 1.9x.

## Details
- Method: 35,000 pages across 5,000 commercial/informational queries in 12 industries, tested against ChatGPT (GPT-4), Claude and Perplexity. Entity signals: schema.org markup, clear entity definition, explicit relationship statements, knowledge-base mentions, entity co-mention density.
- Strongest single signal: 3+ explicit relationship statements give an 81% higher citation rate; entity definitions in the first 200 words give +67%; Product/Organization/Service schema gives +52%; pages mentioning 5+ related entities hit 38.7% vs 11.2% for 0-1.
- Perplexity shows the strongest entity preference (3.1x), likely because its retrieval pipeline uses knowledge-graph signals; for Perplexity specifically prioritize entity definitions in the first 200 words, 3+ relationship statements, and schema markup.
- Caveats: single-vendor study with no published backend; Perplexity's retrieval is closed, so treat the 3.1x ratio as directional until independently replicated.
