## Summary
Princeton's foundational GEO study (Aggarwal et al., 2024) measured: adding statistics with named sources = 40% citation lift; verbatim quotes from credible sources = 30%; fluent low-perplexity prose = 17%; first-sentence direct answers = 14%; keyword repetition = only 2%. Practical target: 1+ verifiable stat per 100 words.

## Details
The Princeton study found GEO-optimized content saw up to 40% lift over unoptimized content. The +41% Position-Adjusted Word Count and +28% Subjective Impression were average improvements across all queries. A 2026 critical survey of 45 GEO studies (arXiv 2607.14035, July 2026) confirmed these findings while noting terminology and metrics remain heterogeneous across the field. Two years of public citation studies from Authoritas, Zyppy, BrightEdge, Lantern, SE Ranking, Conductor, and Seer Interactive have surfaced fact density and direct-answer lead as additional factors.
