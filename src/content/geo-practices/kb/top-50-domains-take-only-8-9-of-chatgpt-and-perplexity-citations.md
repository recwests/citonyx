## Summary
The 50 most-cited domains earned only 8.4% of roughly 5.73 million ChatGPT citations and 9.0% of about 10.37 million Perplexity citations in Surfer's 1-28 September 2026 scrape. The other 91% went to domains outside any public top-50 list, so citation strategy has a long and reachable tail.

## Details
- Surfer's panel tracked 47,213 prompts (1.23M ChatGPT answers) and 57,327 prompts (978,402 Perplexity answers), scraping consumer UIs rather than APIs.
- ChatGPT's top 10 took 39.7% of top-50 citations; Perplexity's top 10 took 48.1% and its top 3 alone 30.4%.
- Reddit led both lists but held only 0.74% of all ChatGPT citations and 1.70% of all Perplexity citations, about 1 in 59.
- Conflicts with 5WPR-style concentration claims (top-15 = 68%) because those measure share within a top-N table, not share of all citations. Caveat: Surfer's panel skews SEO and Polish domains.
