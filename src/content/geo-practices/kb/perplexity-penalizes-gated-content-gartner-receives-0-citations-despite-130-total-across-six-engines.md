## Summary
MRI measurement across 6,020 domains (17,540 citation events) shows Perplexity gives 0 citations to Gartner (130 total across 6 engines). Perplexity favors structured, open market databases like G2, Grand View Research, and Crunchbase. Wire distribution (PRNewswire) gets just 1 Perplexity citation vs 16 from ChatGPT.

## Details
Five factors drive Perplexity source selection: (1) structured extractable content with tables/definitions, (2) open accessibility — no paywalls/registration, (3) factual density with numbers, (4) query-friendly headings, (5) self-contained passages. Perplexity's breadth-first retrieval amplifies structured content effects. For simpler queries, Perplexity cites 3-4 sources after visiting ~10 web pages. For complex multi-constraint queries, it cites 17.7 sources on average. 40% of Perplexity citations come from outside Google's top 10 organic results.
