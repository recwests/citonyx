## Summary
Whitespark's local-search study found AI Overviews appear on 68% of local business-type queries versus just 39% for the traditional local pack. Rates climb to 92% for informational queries and 97% for hybrid-intent searches, while direct near-me searches still favour the map pack 93% of the time.

## Details
- Whitespark measured local results across Houston, Phoenix and Denver (cited by Search Engine Journal, July 2026).
- AI Overviews appeared on 68% of local business-type queries against 39% for the local pack, a 29-point gap; 92% of informational and 97% of hybrid-intent queries triggered one, but only 15% of direct near-me searches did, where the local pack appeared 93% of the time.
- HigherVisibility found businesses ranking well in the local pack were absent from AI Overviews across more than 15 client markets, and Houston plumber answers cited third-party publishers 60% of the time versus 40% local businesses.
- Caveat: an older core dataset reported through agency analysis.
