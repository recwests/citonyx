## Summary
ClearBrand's October 2026 study ran 453 US SaaS buyer-intent searches plus 100 prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude. Google showed an AI Overview on 99.1% of the keywords, each citing 10.6 links on average. YouTube earned 20.9% of those citations, and only 36.3% of cited domains also ranked in the organic top ten.

## Details
Method: 453 SaaS software searches on Google US desktop, October 12-13 2026, plus 100 buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude (clearbrand.com). AI Overviews cited 4,587 links across 432 keywords, i.e. 10.6 per answer. YouTube captured 20.9% of all AI Overview citations and appeared on 72% of keywords with cited sources. Only 36.3% of AI Overview citations pointed to a domain that also ranked in the organic top ten (224 keywords with complete data). Caveat: single-vendor study, one market and a one-week window, so treat as directional.
