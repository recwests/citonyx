## Summary
AI systems crawl product pages but cite blog posts. Trakkr's Page Type Performance Matrix cross-referenced 337K AI citations with 11.4M crawler visits and found a systematic mismatch between what crawlers fetch and what answers cite. Measuring citations alone — or crawler visits alone — gives a misleading picture of AI visibility.

## Details
Trakkr Study 006 (2026): 337K+ AI citations cross-referenced with 11.4M crawler visits. Headline finding (from the research index): "AI crawls your product pages. It cites your blog." Practical split: product pages must stay crawlable (feeds, schema, server-side rendering) for retrieval, while blog/guide content earns the citations. Complements Trakkr Study 003, where ChatGPT Search enters via blog pages 21% of the time. Caveat: the study's full methodology page is JS-rendered; figures here come from the published research index.
