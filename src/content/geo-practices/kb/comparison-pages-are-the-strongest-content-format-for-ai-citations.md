## Summary
Comparison pages are the most trusted format AI engines rarely get to use: 1.87 citations per retrieval, 45% above the 1.29 portfolio average, yet only 4.1% of 25,337 citations went to them (DeltaV Digital, 2026). Superlines found 8 of the 10 most-cited URLs across AI platforms are 'Best X' listicles, and a Profound study of 177 million sources found comparison and list formats make up over 32% of AI citations. Recommendation queries are the highest-intent AI surfaces.

## Details
- DeltaV Digital tracked 21,075 AI engine responses across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews and AI Mode (Apr-Jul 2026): a 'GCC High vs GCC vs Commercial' comparison in B2B cybersecurity earned 4.09 citations per retrieval, the highest of any URL in its industry.
- Winning formats differ by industry: listicles 61% of B2B tech citations, homepages 55% for local services, program pages 53% for higher education.
- Analysis of 116 B2B sites found X-vs-Y pages the strongest content predictor of AI search traffic; Ranqo's 100K-prompt study put best-of listicles at ~21% of all citations.
- Recommendation queries ('best tools for…', 'X vs Y', 'alternatives to…') are the highest-intent AI surfaces.
- Action: create genuinely useful comparison pages, tables and listicles; mention your brand in helpful, specific contexts on your own site; get into third-party 'best X' roundups AI already cites.
- Content cited in AI responses is 25.7% fresher than organic top-10; 'answer capsules' (5+ sentence standalone quotes) get cited 3.2x more; 44% of ChatGPT citations come from the first third of content.
- Caveat: listicles only win if useful — thin list spam is filtered out by quality gates.
