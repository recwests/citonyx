## Summary
Presenc AI's 2026 Comet tracker reports 18 million monthly active users for Perplexity's browser, concentrated among research-heavy and technical users. Agentic-task usage inside Comet grew 860% year over year, inline AI summaries grew 520%, and Comet Pro users made 41% fewer Google search queries than their pre-Comet baseline.

## Details
Comet MAU is about 18 million versus Chrome's roughly 3.4 billion, but the audience concentrates research-heavy, power and B2B/technical buyers. Agentic-task surface grew 860% year over year and inline-summary usage grew 520%, per presenc.ai. Comet Pro users reduced Google Search queries 41% versus their own pre-Comet baseline, with the displaced volume moving to the browser's address-bar AI search and summaries. Implication: citation visibility is shifting into in-browser sidebars, where answer-first, cleanly extractable passages win. Caveat: vendor-published, self-selected panel data.
