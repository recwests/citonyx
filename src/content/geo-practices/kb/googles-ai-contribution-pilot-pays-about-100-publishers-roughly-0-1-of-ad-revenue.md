## Summary
Google's AI contribution pilot pays roughly 100 publishers when their content materially contributes to AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini answers. Reported payments equal about one-tenth of one percent of advertising revenue for several small and mid-size publishers, while one early participant was on track to earn over $1 million a year.

## Details
- Earnings accrue only when content contributes significantly during generation; links added after generation do not count, so the October 2026 wave of richer inline links, carousels and creator-name links sits outside the payment rule.
- For several small and mid-size sites payments equal roughly one-tenth of one percent of advertising revenue; some larger publishers declined to join, hoping to force a better offer.
- One early participant is on track to earn more than $1 million a year, while a later addition earned $50,000-$60,000; publishers describe the contribution mechanism as a black box.
- First reported by Digiday on 14 September 2026 and detailed by The Information on 29 September.
- Context: AI Mode passed one billion monthly users by May 2026, and a randomised study found AI Overviews cut outbound organic clicks by 39.8%.
