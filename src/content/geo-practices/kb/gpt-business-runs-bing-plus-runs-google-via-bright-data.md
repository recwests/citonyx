## Summary

ChatGPT Business search citations are 95% Bing-sourced; ChatGPT Plus citations are 94% from Google via Bright Data scraper. On Business/Bing, 94% of citations come from top 10 results. Year-injected queries grew from 5% to 80% of runs, making year-tagged listicles dominant.

## Details

TUM master's thesis analyzed 79 prompts × 3 runs across ChatGPT Plus, ChatGPT Business, and Gemini — 1,650 runs, 370K+ search results, 13K+ citations. Key findings: (1) ~80% of AI product recommendations trace directly back to search rankings; (2) Business citations are 95% Bing, Plus citations 94% Bright Data (Google scraper); (3) On Business/Bing, 94% of citations come from top 10, 99% within rank 12; Plus cites deeper into low 20s; (4) ~12-15% of citations bypass search entirely through 'labrador' channel (Wikipedia, arXiv, major tech press); (5) Listicle position decides: #1 item selected at 1.7-2.5x chance, top 5 take 76-81% of selections; (6) Year injection grew from 5% to 80% of runs; (7) Gemini ran 97.8% year-injected queries vs ChatGPT's 80%.
