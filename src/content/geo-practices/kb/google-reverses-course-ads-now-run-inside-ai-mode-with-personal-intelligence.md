## Summary
Google confirmed on 19 October 2026 that AI Mode will show ads when Personal Intelligence is enabled, after saying in March that the personalised mode would stay ad-free. Personalised AI answers are now a paid surface, so organic citation share alone no longer describes who appears in front of high-intent users.

## Details
Reported by Search Engine Roundtable from Google's confirmation.

- The change matters for budgeting because one session can now contain a personalised answer, an ad slot and organic citations, so paid and earned measurement have to be joined rather than reported separately.
- Caveat: rollout scope, formats and pricing are not public, and personalised inventory may not be comparable to standard AI Mode ads.
- Track CPM and share of voice separately for personal-intelligence sessions.
