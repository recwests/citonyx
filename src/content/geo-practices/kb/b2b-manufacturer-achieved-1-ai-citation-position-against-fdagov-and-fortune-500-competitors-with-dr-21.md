## Summary
A B2B supplier with DR 21 achieved #1 AI search position ahead of FDA.gov and Fortune 500 competitors in 7 months: DR grew 67% (21→35), referring domains 117→339, branded search up 587%, leads up 60%. First-mover window still open in early 2026 but compressing.

## Details
The Radiant Elephant case study documents 100,000+ specialized content pieces, entity-rich schema markup connecting the brand to regulatory terms, and multi-tiered link building targeting industry authority sites. The client achieved 1.69% market share in Google AI Overview across 200 prompts. Researchers identified a 12-18 month first-mover window. Parallel drawn to mobile optimization 2010-2014: by 2027-2028, AI optimization may become table stakes. Ramp (fintech) achieved 7x visibility improvement (3.2% to 22.2%) through coordinated owned content, G2/Capterra optimization, and earned media.
