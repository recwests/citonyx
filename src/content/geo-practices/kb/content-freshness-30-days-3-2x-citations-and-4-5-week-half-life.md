## Summary

Monthly refresh cadence for high-priority pages is the practical baseline. AI models detect cosmetic-only updates — freshness must be substantive (new stats, new cases, corrected claims). Citations have a ~4.5-week half-life.

## Details

NinjaPromo study cross-validated across 3 studies. 65% of AI bot traffic targets content published/updated within last year (Wellows). 79% references content refreshed within past two years. Only 6% of citations originate from content older than 6 years. AirOps research: pages not updated quarterly were 3x more likely to lose citations. HubSpot's Aja Frost: half of cited pages change every single month; ~60% of pages earning a citation appear once and never return the following month.
