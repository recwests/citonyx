## Summary
ClearBrand's October 2026 SaaS study found that 75% of the domains Perplexity cited also appeared in Google's AI Overviews, against 63% for Gemini, 50% for Claude and 35% for ChatGPT. If AI Overviews are the priority, Perplexity-visible sources are the closest proxy for where to place them.

## Details
- 100 buyer prompts per engine (400 answers) collected via DataForSEO on 12-13 October 2026, compared with the 1,045 domains cited across 453 SaaS AI Overviews.
- Cited domains per answer: Gemini 9.8, Perplexity 8.8, Claude 5.8, ChatGPT 5.7.
- Top domains diverge: ChatGPT leaned on techradar.com, Perplexity on g2.com, Gemini on zapier.com, Claude on technologyadvice.com; review directories were 11% of Perplexity citations but roughly 4-5% for ChatGPT and Gemini.
- Caveat: one snapshot, API-collected answers, single run per prompt.
