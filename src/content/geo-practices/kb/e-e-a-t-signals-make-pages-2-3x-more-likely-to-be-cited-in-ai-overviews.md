## Summary

Pages with strong E-E-A-T signals are 2.3x more likely to be cited in AI Overviews (Wellows study of 2,400 citations). Correctly structured pages had 73% higher selection rate. Danny Sullivan confirmed: 'SEO for AI is still SEO.'

## Details

96% of cited pages carry strong E-E-A-T signals per analysis of 15,847 AI Overview results (UnoSearch). Cited pages also pull 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks (Seer Interactive). Person Schema, Knowledge Graph presence, and author entities are increasingly important trust signals. Google's January 2025 QRG update shifted evaluation from 'who wrote it' to 'does it demonstrate genuine value.' E-E-A-T is not a ranking factor but a quality framework — the signals it describes correlate with rankings, AI citations, and Discover visibility.
