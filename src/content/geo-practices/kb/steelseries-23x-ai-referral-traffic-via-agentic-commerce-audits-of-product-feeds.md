## Summary
SteelSeries dominated Google yet was invisible in ChatGPT/Perplexity headset recommendations. A 10-month GEO campaign — restructuring content, PDPs and product feeds for machine extraction plus per-platform optimization — produced 23x AI search traffic, 27x conversions and 3.7x AI-referred revenue (NoGood case study).

## Details
- Core pillars: AEO writing for extraction (entity-rich, direct answers to conversational questions); agentic-commerce audit of product feeds so specs, price and availability are instantly parseable; per-engine tuning — ChatGPT social trust, Perplexity structured data and real-time citations, Gemini ecosystem integration, Claude legacy authority.
- Results: +75% Perplexity visibility score, 6.3x overall brand visibility; by October 2025 AI traffic reached 1,500+ daily visitors.
- Attribution via real-time AEO monitoring from a January 2025 baseline.
- Caveats: agency-published results and a 10-month window; reuse the article's PDP-audit prompt to check your own category for machine readability.
