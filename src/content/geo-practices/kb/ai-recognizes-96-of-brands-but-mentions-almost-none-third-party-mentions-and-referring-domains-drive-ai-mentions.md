## Summary
Victorious's Q2 2026 study across eight AI platforms and 175 brands found recognition and mention are different behaviors: AI described 96% of brands accurately when asked directly, yet 89% never appeared in answers to category research questions. The strongest correlating signals were referring domains (0.49) and third-party web mentions (0.45); brands with fewer than 2,000 indexed pages mentioning them were named in AI answers just 3% of the time.

## Details
- Recognition accuracy varied by platform: Google AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT, AI Overviews and Copilot exceeded 83% in every vertical; Perplexity correctly recognized fewer than 55% of SaaS and ecommerce brands, and Meta AI 46% of SaaS.
- High-authority links alone added only a modest lift.
- Practical read: brand visibility is a whole-web problem — build a third-party mention footprint and referring domains, not just on-site optimization.
- Caveat: sponsored study by Victorious. Source: SEJ (Jul 2026).
