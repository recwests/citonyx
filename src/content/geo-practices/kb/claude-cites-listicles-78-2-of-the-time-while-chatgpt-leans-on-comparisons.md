## Summary
One content plan cannot serve seven engines. In OtterlyAI's September 2026 corpus, Claude citations were 78.2% listicles, ChatGPT gave 16.1% to head-to-head comparisons and about 6% to documentation, roughly triple the average, while Google AI Overviews and AI Mode gave 23-28% combined to forums and video.

## Details
The three patterns barely moved across the month, which makes them usable planning inputs rather than noise.

- If buyers use Claude, Perplexity or Gemini, most of the work is earning inclusion in someone else's ranked list.
- If they use ChatGPT, owned comparison pages and help-centre documentation carry real share.
- If they use Google AI surfaces, Reddit and YouTube are the channels, supplying 92% and 98% of forum and video citations respectively. Re-check quarterly, because engine mix shifts with model releases.
