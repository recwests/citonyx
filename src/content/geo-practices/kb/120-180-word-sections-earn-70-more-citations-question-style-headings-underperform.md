## Summary
SE Ranking's analysis of 129,000 domains / 216,524 pages (via Averi) found pages with 120-180 words between headings receive 70% more citations than sections under 50 words, and question-style headings underperform straightforward ones (3.4 vs 4.3 citations). Extractable section length and plain headings drive ChatGPT citations.

## Details
SE Ranking analyzed 129,000 unique domains across 216,524 pages to identify content characteristics predicting ChatGPT citation probability. Pages with 120-180 words between headings earned 70% more citations than sections under 50 words; proper H1 to H2 to H3 hierarchy materially improved citation rates; content with consistent heading levels is 40% more likely cited. Counter-intuitive: question-style headings underperform straightforward headings (3.4 vs 4.3 citations), and broad topic-describing URLs outperform keyword-optimized ones. Caveat: correlational; the 70% figure reflects section length alone while other factors (authority, freshness) co-vary. Use section length as a content-structure lever, not a standalone ranking tactic.
