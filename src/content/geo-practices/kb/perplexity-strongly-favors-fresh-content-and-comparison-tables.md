## Summary
Perplexity strongly favors fresh, comparison-table content. Analysis of 847,000 Perplexity citations found 76.4% of cited pages were published or updated within 90 days, and 76.4% contain comparison tables versus 23.1% of Google top-10 results. Perplexity weights freshness 3.5x more heavily than Google ranking does (Georion/Profound).

## Details
- Sources: Georion/Profound 2026 analysis; SE Ranking.
- Perplexity weights semantic relevance 2.4x more than traditional SEO signals; FAQ-structured content earns 4.1x more citations than standard articles.
- Citation momentum: sources cited 10+ times monthly achieve 9.2x higher future citation probability.
- Practical: refresh 'last updated' dates, publish current-year data, and build comparison tables for 'best X' and 'X vs Y' queries.
