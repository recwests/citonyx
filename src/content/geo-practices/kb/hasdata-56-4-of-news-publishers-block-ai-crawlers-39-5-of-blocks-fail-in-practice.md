## Summary

HasData (July 2026) analyzed 10,894 domains: 56.4% of news publishers now block at least one AI crawler in robots.txt (vs 10.3% of the open web). GPTBot specifically is banned by 50.5% of publishers vs 7.9% of the web. But 39.5% of GPTBot-blocking sites still served a page when live-tested. Blocks only work against polite bots — aggressive crawlers bypass them.

## Details

Reuters and Time have moved to a nuanced approach: block all AI bots by default but whitelist approved ones. DataDome's Segura: 'The question needs to evolve from allowing or blocking AI agents to determining which ones should be trusted and why.' ChatGPT-User visits dropped 6% QoB in Q2 2026 while human click-throughs grew 17%. The implication: blanket blocking harms visibility without fully stopping unwanted crawling. Differentiated agent policies based on purpose and business value are the emerging best practice.
