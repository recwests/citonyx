## Summary

Muck Rack (December 2025, 1M+ citations across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity): only 2% of journalists PR teams pitch are the journalists AI platforms cite. Journalism accounts for 20-30% of all AI citations. Press release citations grew 5x in 6 months (July-December 2025). 82% of AI citations come from earned media, only 6% from paid. (Source: Muck Rack Generative Pulse, July + December 2025)

## Details

- Muck Rack (December 2025, 1M+ citations): only 2% overlap between PR-pitch journalists and AI-cited journalists.
- Journalism share of all AI citations: 20-30%.
- Press release citations grew 5x between July and December 2025.
- 82% of AI citations come from earned media, 94% from non-paid sources.
- Practical takeaway: audit which outlets and journalists AI cites for your category and build a parallel outreach track targeting those sources. Standard PR targeting systematically misses the reporters that matter for AI visibility.
