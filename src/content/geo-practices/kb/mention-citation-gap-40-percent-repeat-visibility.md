## Summary

Track both mention rate and citation rate together. The gap between being mentioned (brand named) and being cited (brand linked as source) reveals a content trust gap. Close it by publishing structured, authoritative content on your own domain.

## Details

AirOps 2026 State of AI Search report. Key LLM visibility metrics: mention rate (baseline visibility), citation rate (content authority + traffic potential), sentiment (messaging health), citation share of voice (competitive position). Minimum tracking: 20-30 prompts mirroring real buyer questions, tested across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini/Claude weekly. First mention rate (how often brand appears first in answer) captures disproportionate reader attention. AI-referred leads convert 2-6x higher than other channels. Pages not updated quarterly were 3x more likely to lose citations.
