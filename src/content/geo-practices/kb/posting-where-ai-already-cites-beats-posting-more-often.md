## Summary
Researchers posted articles about a fictional Cactus Gravity Protocol on nine sites that AI tools already cited often. ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok and Google AI all cited the concept within seven days, with Grok linking in 30 of 32 answers. In paired tests the post on the already-cited source was always referenced first, while 40 posts elsewhere drew none.

## Details
Tests ran March to April 2026 across 120 questions and 10 tools; posts were AI-drafted then manually revised with planted names and numbers.

- Medium, GitHub, LinkedIn and Facebook were easy to join, while Reddit's karma rules made posting harder.
- Limits: fictional topics only, so the results do not establish behaviour for real brands.
- Implication: audit which domains each engine already cites before buying placement volume, because volume on uncited domains converted at zero here.
