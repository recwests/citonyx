## Summary
For AI tool and SaaS queries, X-vs-Y comparison pages are the single highest-ROI content type for GEO. LLMs constantly answer 'compare X vs Y' queries. Pages with comparison tables, clear 'when to choose us' vs 'when to choose them' sections, and recent pricing get cited at materially higher rates than generic category pages.

## Details
Multi-source validation — the same fact appearing across 5+ independent domains — lifts citation rate by 67%. This means earned-media footprint matters as much as owned content. Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overviews all weight Reddit threads heavily for comparison queries. Best practice: create a dedicated /your-tool-vs-competitor page for each top 3 competitors with honest, specific comparisons and opinionated positions — LLMs cite content that takes a stance.
