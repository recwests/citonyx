## Summary

Ahrefs 75,000-brand correlation study: YouTube brand mentions show the strongest single signal correlation (r=0.737) with AI Overview visibility — higher than general brand web mentions (r=0.664), editorial press, or Reddit mentions. Video transcripts are parsed by AI crawlers and feed directly into citation pools.

## Details

Transcripts of YouTube reviews, comparisons, and demos are indexed by AI platforms (Gemini, AI Overviews particularly weight YouTube content). Per Ranqo's 100K+ response study: among non-corporate sources, YouTube leads (ahead of Reddit, editorial media, Wikipedia). Action: ensure all product/brand YouTube content has accurate transcripts, structured descriptions, and captions. Invest in video content as an AI-citation asset, not just a human-engagement channel.
