## Summary

AI Overviews hit 48% query prevalence by Feb 2026 (BrightEdge). Seer's study of 3,119 queries across 42 orgs showed organic CTR dropped 61% (1.76%→0.61%) when AIO present. Paid CTR fell 68% (19.7%→6.34%). However, brands cited in AIOs earn ~120% more organic clicks per impression than uncited brands on same queries. Early 2026 showed CTR recovery to ~2.4% from a 1.3% floor in Dec 2025.

## Details

BrightEdge's 16-month study (May 2024–Sep 2025) tracked 9 industries. Key finding: AIO citations now 54.5% from organic rankings (up from 32.3%). But e-commerce overlap stayed flat at 22.9% — Google separates transactional queries. Healthcare (75.3%) and Education (72.6%) show strongest overlap. Comparison queries (X vs Y) trigger AIOs 95.4% of the time. Google Search revenue grew 17% to $63B in Q4 2025 even as publisher traffic fell ~33%.
