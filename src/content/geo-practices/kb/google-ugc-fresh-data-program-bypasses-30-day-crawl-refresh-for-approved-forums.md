## Summary
Google launched the UGC Fresh Data Program on 8 October 2026, letting approved forums and social platforms push new posts and engagement counters directly to Google within minutes, bypassing a normal crawl refresh that Google's Gary Illyes put at roughly 30 days for known URLs.

## Details
Eligibility requires six things: primarily UGC on stable public URLs, high volume and a significant user base, no login or paywall, OAuth 2.0 plus validated JSON-LD using SocialMediaPosting or DiscussionForumPosting, active moderation, and submission within minutes with engagement refresh inside 72 hours. Google replies in six to eight weeks and states clearly that applying or participating guarantees nothing in Search.
