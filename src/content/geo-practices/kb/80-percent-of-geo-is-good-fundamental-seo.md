## Summary
'80% of GEO is good, fundamental SEO' (Jeremy Moser, uSERP/Mersel AI 2026). 87% of ChatGPT citations match Bing top-10 results and 93.67% of AI Overview citations link to at least one top-10 organic result. GEO is the additional layer you build on an already-healthy SEO foundation.

## Details
- Sources: Mersel AI 2026 framework via Aithinker Lab; ConvertMate GEO Benchmark 2026.
- Nuance: ConvertMate also reports 83% of AI Overview citations come from pages outside the organic top 10, so the overlap is real but shrinking.
- Fix crawlability, sitemaps, E-E-A-T and clean technical SEO before layering answer-first formatting and entity work.
- Fundamentals are necessary but no longer sufficient.
