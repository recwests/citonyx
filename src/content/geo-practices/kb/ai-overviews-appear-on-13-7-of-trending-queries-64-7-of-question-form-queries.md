## Summary
A 2026 measurement study of Google AI Overviews (arXiv:2605.14021) found activation on 13.7% of trending queries overall and 64.7% of question-form queries. It also assessed source quality, claim fidelity and publisher impact, finding Google most willing to synthesize high-intent informational searches that previously sent traffic to content sites.

## Details
Study measures activation rates, source quality, claim fidelity and publisher impact of Google AI Overviews. Overall activation on 13.7% of trending queries, jumping to 64.7% on question-form queries — the query class most likely to have once driven high-intent traffic to informational publishers. Also pressures citation quality by examining how often cited sources align with claims. Placed alongside Similarweb's 40%+ overall prevalence estimate, the range shows AIO moved from a query-type-heavy feature to a broadly deployed one. Caveat: preprint; trending-query sample, US; complementary rather than identical to clickstream prevalence estimates.
