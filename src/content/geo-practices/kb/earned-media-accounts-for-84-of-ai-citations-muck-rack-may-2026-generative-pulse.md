## Summary

Muck Rack's May 2026 Generative Pulse analyzed 25M+ cited links across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini across 17 industries. Findings: earned media = 84% of AI citations, journalism = 27%, press releases = 0.04%, paid/advertorial = 0.3%. Ranqo data shows only 2.9% of citations point to a brand's own domain. Three consecutive editions show this pattern is stable infrastructure, not a snapshot.

## Details

The finding has been consistent across 3 editions (July 2025–May 2026) with earned media ranging 82-89%. Practical takeaway: AI citations depend on evidence architecture — independent corroboration from credible third parties. Owned content defines the entity but earned media provides the trust layer. GEO programs focused exclusively on owned content optimize for <3% of where citations land. Brands need coordinated owned + earned + third-party strategies.
