## Summary
BrandGEO's July 2026 index found the language of a query can change the competitive set entirely. Asking for Paris wealth managers in French surfaced boutique patrimoine firms (Cheval Blanc Patrimoine in 3 of 4 responses), while the identical question in English surfaced international banks like Societe Generale Private Banking. A French-only firm is structurally invisible to English-language AI searchers.

## Details
The 7-city, 419-response study also found near-unanimous cross-engine consensus for well-documented software and fintech products (Qonto, Starling, Monzo, Lexware at 4-of-4 engines), while professional services with long tails of local firms stayed fragmented in every city. Rome showed within-engine stability: each engine built its own consistent worldview that doesn't transfer. Caveats: single collection pass per city on July 10, a ChatGPT collection gap, and one snapshot rather than a trend. Implication: publish in the languages your buyers actually query.
