## Summary

The foundational GEO paper (Aggarwal et al., ACM KDD 2024) tested 9 optimization methods: Quotation Addition +41% visibility (PAWC), Statistics Addition +31%, Cite Sources +28%, Fluency Optimization +28%. Only tactic that hurt was keyword stuffing (-8 to -10%). These findings have been confirmed by multiple 2025-2026 replication studies. Practical implication: injecting specific numbers and attributable quotes is the single highest-ROI content change for AI visibility.

## Details

- Princeton/Georgia Tech (Aggarwal et al., ACM KDD 2024, 250K+ responses).
- Visibility lift measured on Position-Adjusted Word Count (PAWC).
- Top tactics: Quotation Addition +41%, Statistics Addition +31%, Cite Sources +28%, Fluency Optimization +28%.
- Negative tactic: keyword stuffing -8 to -10%.
- Confirmed in 2025-2026 by: Ahrefs (mentions correlate 0.664), Aixel (18 factors, 252K trials), multiple GEO vendors.
- Practical takeaway: for every new page, add (1) a specific statistic with source, (2) a direct attributable quote from a credible third party, (3) inline source citations for claims.
