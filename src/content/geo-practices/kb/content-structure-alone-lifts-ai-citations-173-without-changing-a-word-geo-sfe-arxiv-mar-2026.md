## Summary
GEO-SFE (Yu et al., arXiv:2603.29979, Mar 2026): restructured a page without changing a single word and measured 17.3% citation lift across six generative engines. Three structural levels: macro (heading hierarchy), meso (chunking), micro (emphasis). Structure and content are independent levers — they compound when both are optimized.

## Details
Macro-structure (heading hierarchy) had the broadest cross-engine effect. Meso-structure (proper chunking with one claim per block, data in tables rather than inline) produced strongest passage-level extraction. Practical checklist: no paragraphs >100 words containing factual claims, comparisons of 3+ items go in tables, key statistics get their own sentence, step-by-step uses numbered lists. 17% lift compounds with content quality gains.
