## Summary
GEO measurement framework: Citation Rate (% of target queries where brand is cited at least once across AI platforms) — baseline 5-15%, target 40-50% within 6 months. Share of Model (brand citations / total category citations × 100). Citation Share of Voice (fixed prompt set run monthly). Track 50-100 prospect questions weekly across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity.

## Details
- Measuring citation rate requires starting with 50-100 questions prospects ask during vendor research. Query each across ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity weekly.
- Most B2B companies start at 5-15% citation rate. Systematic GEO programs move clients to 40-50% within 6 months.
- AI referral traffic from analytics and brand mention velocity are secondary leading indicators.
- Manual sampling is free; paid tools (Otterly, Presenc, RankScope) add scale for enterprise programs.
