## Summary

Adobe Q1 2026 data covering 1T+ US retail visits shows AI-referred traffic converted 42% better than non-AI traffic in March 2026 — a reversal from 38% worse in March 2025. AI visits grew 393% YoY in Q1, with 37% higher revenue per visit and 48% longer time on site. Shopify independently corroborated ~50% higher conversion rates on product pages.

## Details

Adobe Digital Insights analyzed 1T+ visits across 130+ top North American retailers. Key metrics: AI traffic grew 393% YoY Q1 2026, conversion flipped from -38% to +42% in 12 months, engagement rate +12%, pages/visit +13%, revenue/visit +37%. Shopify's independent data (May 2026) showed AI sessions convert ~50% above organic on product pages, with 14% higher AOV and 8x session growth YoY. The mechanism is 'journey compression' — AI collapses the discovery-to-decision funnel within the conversation, so clicks land closer to purchase intent.
