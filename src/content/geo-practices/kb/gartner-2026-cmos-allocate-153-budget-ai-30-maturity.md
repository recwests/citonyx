## Summary
Gartner's 2026 CMO Spend Survey (401 CMOs, Jan-Mar 2026): CMOs allocate 15.3% of marketing budgets to AI initiatives, but only 30% report mature AI readiness capabilities. 70% consider becoming AI leaders critical, yet 70% also acknowledge internal processes aren't ready. AI-mature organizations allocate 21.3% to AI (vs 15.3% average) and have larger overall budgets (8.9% of revenue vs 7.8%).

## Details
Marketing budgets sit at 7.8% of company revenue in 2026, up slightly from 7.7% in 2025 but still well below 9.5% from four years ago (Gartner, May 11 2026). The gap between AI ambition and readiness is the key risk: 'CMOs invest in AI tools faster than they build the data foundations, processes, governance and talent required to scale them' (Ewan McIntyre, Gartner). AI-mature orgs are establishing early advantage by pairing investment with budget agility, innovation capacity and operating discipline. For GEO practitioners: the budget allocation gap means most teams are under-resourced for AI search optimization — the 15.3% includes all AI, not just GEO. Actual GEO budgets are likely a fraction of this. Implication: GEO needs to demonstrate ROI quickly to justify dedicated budget lines within the constrained marketing budget environment.
