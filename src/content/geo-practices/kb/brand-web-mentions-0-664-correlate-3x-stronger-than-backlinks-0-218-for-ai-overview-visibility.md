## Summary
Brand web mentions show a 0.664 Spearman correlation with AI visibility vs 0.218 for backlinks and 0.27 for domain rating — off-site brand presence matters about 3x more than link building for AI citations. Ahrefs analyzed 75,000 brands; YouTube mentions were the single strongest factor at roughly 0.737. Earned media should precede or accompany technical GEO work.

## Details
- Ahrefs' Spearman ranking of 75,000 brands: YouTube mentions 0.737, YouTube mention impressions 0.717, branded web mentions 0.656–0.709, branded anchors 0.51–0.628, brand search volume 0.352–0.466, Domain Rating ~0.33, backlinks 0.218, page count 0.194.
- Branded web mentions (0.664), branded anchors (0.527) and branded search volume (0.392) top the factor list; Domain Rating (0.27), referring domains (0.295) and backlinks (0.218) are weaker.
- The top 25% by mentions average 169 AI Overview mentions vs 14 for the next quartile; 26% of brands have zero.
- ChatGPT showed the weakest link to classic authority signals (DR 0.266, branded search volume 0.352); correlation is not causation.
- Corroborated by Seer Interactive, Shadow, Muck Rack and Digital Applied's meta-analysis of 54 studies — they converge on the same hierarchy.
- Caveat: correlation, not causation — mentions may proxy topical expertise density.
- Action: build mention equity (unlinked mentions in trade press, reviews, directories, especially YouTube and earned media) as infrastructure; track AI share of voice monthly; treat backlinks and content volume as secondary.
