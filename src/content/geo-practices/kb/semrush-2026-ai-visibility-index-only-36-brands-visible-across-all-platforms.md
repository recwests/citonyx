## Summary
Semrush's 2026 AI Visibility Index expanded from 2,500 to 126M US AI search prompts (Jan-Apr 2026). Only 36 brands (Universal 36) maintained top-100 visibility across all platforms every month. On Gemini, overlap between mentioned brands and cited domains is as low as 30%. ChatGPT cites 15 sources per response on average; Gemini cites only 3. Brands in less concentrated categories (Finance top-3 = 41.4%, Industrial = 42.2%) have more opportunity.

## Details
- Visibility concentration varies sharply by industry: News/Media top-3 brands = 82.9% of visibility, Consumer Electronics top-3 = 76.9%, Finance top-3 = 41.4% — less concentrated categories offer more room.
- 81% of organizations that fully integrate SEO and AI visibility reported increased traffic/leads vs 36% for siloed approaches; 45% of marketing leaders cannot measure brand visibility in AI answers.
- Patagonia maintained ~79-80 AI visibility score supported by consistent descriptions across OutdoorGearLab, REI, Switchback Travel, GearJunkie, and Reddit — third-party sources shape AI narratives.
- Adobe data: AI traffic to US retail sites surged 1,324% (Oct 2024-May 2026).
- Action: measure per platform, strengthen third-party brand signals, and unify SEO + AI strategy (Semrush, Jun 26 2026).
- Merged detail from MarTech (14 October 2026, reporting the same Semrush 2026 AI Visibility Index): across 22 categories and four AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews), fewer than half of the top-100 mentioned brands also appeared in the corresponding top-100 citation list on average in every month measured.
- Rankings diverge by engine: on ChatGPT, Tesco ranked 25th among cited domains while Asda ranked 606th, but on Gemini the two sat at 29th and 60th.
- Implication: track mentions and citations as separate metrics and diagnose which one is weak before choosing a fix.
