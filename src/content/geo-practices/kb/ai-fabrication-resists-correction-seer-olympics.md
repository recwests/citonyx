## Summary

Seer's 2026 Olympics study (231K+ LLM responses, 7 platforms, 52 days) documented three failure types: (1) narrative completion — ChatGPT declared a winner 6 days early; (2) plausible hallucination — Meta AI reported score 194.63 vs actual 156.33; (3) persistence — 1 in 5 correct responses still told the old story 3+ weeks post-event.

## Details

Three documented failure types: (1) Narrative completion — ChatGPT 'predicted' Chloé Kim's three-peat 6 days early because the dominant narrative arc made it 'inevitable' in training data. (2) Plausible hallucination — Meta AI reported Ilia Malinin's score as 194.63 (actual: 156.33) with confident delivery. (3) Persistence — Meta and Claude would not update until next training cycle regardless of brand efforts. Three weeks later, 20% of correct AI responses still told the old story. Practical takeaway: brands facing negative AI misrepresentation face correction timelines measured in months. Visibility-focused GEO alone cannot solve this.
