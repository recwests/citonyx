## Summary
Major studies (SE Ranking 300K domains, Trakkr 37,894 domains, 515M-event analysis) converge: llms.txt has no measurable effect on AI citations. Adoption is 7.4% of top 10K sites. John Mueller (Google) compared it to the deprecated keywords meta tag. Major LLM crawlers don't fetch it. Real value is in developer documentation and agent behavior shaping (Stripe play).

## Details
XGBoost model improved when llms.txt variable was removed. Trakkr scan: sites with llms.txt averaged 6.8 citations vs 6.7 without (p=0.85). 55% use full recommended structure. 12% are plugin-generated. Adoption concentrated in tech (36% of top 1K sites). Mintlify raised $45M Series B at $500M valuation for auto-generating llms.txt. Stripe uses it to steer AI away from deprecated APIs. Google's John Mueller: 'comparable to keywords meta tag.'
