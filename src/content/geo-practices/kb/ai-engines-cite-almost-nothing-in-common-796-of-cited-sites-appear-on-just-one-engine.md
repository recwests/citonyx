## Summary
Across 22.7 million citations and five engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode), Wellows found 79.6% of websites cited for a question appear on only one engine, with just 0.31% cited on all five (Jan-Jun 2026). ChatGPT is the most isolated: Perplexity never touches 89.1% of the sites it cites.

## Details
- Exact-page agreement between any two engines is 6.8%; website 10.9%; brand 30.3% — the brand layer is the easiest to win across engines (Wellows, Jul 29 2026).
- Against a random baseline the order flips: a shared page citation is 42x above chance, a shared brand mention 17x.
- Cross-engine agreement rose 33% in six months (8.49% Jan -> 11.25% Jun 2026), so the 'own a niche only one engine sees' window is closing.
- Category matters more than intent: settled commercial categories agree 10.1-15.5%, emerging AEO/GEO only 6.3%.
- Implication: blended AI-visibility scores hide which surface moved; report per engine plus a separate brand line.
