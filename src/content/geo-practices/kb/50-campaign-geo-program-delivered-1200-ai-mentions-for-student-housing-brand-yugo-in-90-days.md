## Summary
Threshold ran 50+ concurrent GEO campaigns for student housing brand Yugo, restructuring content and schema for conversational queries. In 90 days Yugo logged 1,200+ AI mentions, 8 high-value direct citations, a 0.35% share of voice in student housing search, and 10.9K monthly organic sessions fueled by 27.2K ranking keywords.

## Details
The playbook targeted conversational, high-intent queries ('best budget-friendly student housing near UT Austin') and organized assets into 'chunkable' authoritative answers with deep technical schema. Visibility was secured concurrently across AI Overview, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity for algorithmic diversification. Threshold maps AI visibility to pipeline: mentions lead to high-intent traffic, qualified leads, closed revenue, arguing AI acts as trusted peer review. Agency-reported, no independent verification; treat the multi-platform structure as the transferable pattern (July 2026).
