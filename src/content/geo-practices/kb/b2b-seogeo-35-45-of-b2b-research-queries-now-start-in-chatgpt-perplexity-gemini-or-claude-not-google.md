## Summary
35-45% of B2B research queries start in AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) rather than Google. Top-performing content hubs achieve 35-55% citation rates on relevant queries; mid-performers 15-30%; generic thin content just 2-8%.

## Details
Conductor analysis found ChatGPT accounts for 87.4% of all AI referral traffic. ChatGPT has 900M weekly active users (Feb 2026). Google AI Overviews reach 1.5B monthly users. Google AI Mode has 75M daily active users. Perplexity has 45M monthly active users. B2B tech benchmark citations (Ahrefs Brand Radar): newsletter advertising (Morning Brew, Dupple, TLDR) = ~60% of citations; developer tools = Stack Overflow/official docs/Reddit; security = Krebs/Bleeping Computer/vendor whitepapers. The 90-day GEO playbook: weeks 1-2 audit (test 10-20 key queries across all engines), weeks 3-6 rewrite top pages with extractable content, weeks 7-10 build entity profile, weeks 11-12 measure and iterate.
