## Summary

Ramp (B2B fintech) case study from Profound: AI visibility score rose from 3.2% to 22.2% (7x improvement) across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity. Three pillars: comprehensive category/comparison content, G2/Capterra profile optimization, strategic earned media in Tier-1 fintech press. Treatment of AI visibility as a dedicated channel (not side project) was key. Timeline: 6-12 months for meaningful lift.

## Details

Cross-case patterns that replicate across all documented GEO success stories: 1) Structured data = cheapest unlock; 2) E-E-A-T signals compound; 3) Content breadth > depth on single pages — AI cites the specific page answering the specific question, not generic 'ultimate guides'; 4) Third-party signals non-negotiable — G2, Capterra, Wikipedia, press coverage, industry directories all part of the mix. Caveat from Presenc AI: case studies have publication bias and attribution is hard (channel expansion inflates point-in-time numbers).
