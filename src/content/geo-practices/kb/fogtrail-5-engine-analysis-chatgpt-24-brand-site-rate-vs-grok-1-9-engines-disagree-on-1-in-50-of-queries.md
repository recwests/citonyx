## Summary

FogTrail (Feb 2026, 1,122 citation URLs, 100 engine-query pairs, 5 B2B SaaS categories): only 6.3% of all citation URLs pointed to tracked brand websites. ChatGPT links to brand sites in 24.2% of citations (highest) vs Grok at 1.9% (lowest). Engines disagree on the #1 recommendation in 50% of queries. Grok averages 21.5 citations per response but only 2 brand citations across 20 queries.

## Details

Each engine has a fundamentally different authority model. ChatGPT: domain authority + brand-site bias. Perplexity: lowest authority threshold (cites startups at #1 in 0% of queries). Grok: volume-driven, brand-agnostic, Reddit-heavy (13 Reddit URLs vs 2 for all other engines combined). Gemini: recency as primary signal. Claude: quality filter, near-exclusive citation of individual company domains. 'Alternative to X' queries give the incumbent #1 in 93% of responses. Perplexity shows notable inconsistency: same query can surface different sources minutes apart.
