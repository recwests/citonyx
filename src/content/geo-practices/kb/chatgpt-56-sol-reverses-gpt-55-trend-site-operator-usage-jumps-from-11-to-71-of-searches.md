## Summary
Writesonic's July 2026 study shows GPT-5.6 Sol reverses GPT-5.5's biggest finding: site: operator queries jumped from 11% to 71% of searches. Brand citations, content freshness, and answer length all moved with this shift. Model behavior changes are frequent and dramatic.

## Details
The rapid reversal demonstrates the volatility of ChatGPT citation patterns across minor model versions. GPT-5.4 → 5.3 → 5.5 → 5.6 each showed materially different citation behaviors within months. This volatility is a key finding for GEO practitioners: tactics that work on one model version may not transfer to the next. Continuous monitoring and model-specific tracking are essential, not periodic audits. The '15 unique domains per answer' figure from early 2026 is now specific to the GPT-5.3 era.
