## Summary
Ranking #1 on Google no longer guarantees AI citation. Overlap between top-10 organic results and AI Overview citations collapsed from ~75% (mid-2025) to 17-38% (early 2026), and ConvertMate reports 83% of AIO citations come from pages outside the organic top 10 (12,500+ queries, 8,000 domains). This breaks a decade of SEO assumptions about rank being the primary lever.

## Details
- BrightEdge 16-month study: top-10 overlap fell to 17-38%, yet 54.5% of citations still come from pages ranking 1-100 organically; position 21-100 is the sweet spot for overlap growth.
- By industry: healthcare 75.3% overlap, education 72.6%, insurance 68.6%, e-commerce only 22.9%.
- Google's FastSearch (RankEmbed model) retrieves differently from organic ranking, prioritizing semantic matching and user intent over traditional factors like links.
- Note: 83% outside top-10 (ConvertMate) and 54.5% from 1-100 (BrightEdge) are different measures and both can hold.
- Implication: SEO reporting must separate AI citation tracking from rank tracking; track impressions vs clicks per keyword and add AI-citation KPIs to dashboards.
