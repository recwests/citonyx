## Summary
AI search crawlers account for 3.4% of all web traffic as of July 2026. 12% of top 1000 websites block OAI-SearchBot. BrightEdge tracks 12+ AI agents. Key insight: training crawlers (GPTBot), search crawlers (OAI-SearchBot), and user-action crawlers (ChatGPT-User) serve different purposes and should be managed separately.

## Details
Imperva's 2026 Bad Bot Report says automated traffic accounted for 53%+ of all web traffic in 2025, with humans at 47%. Cloudflare's crawl-purpose breakdown separates Training, Search, User action, and Undeclared crawlers. OpenAI uses three agents: GPTBot (training, 51% of training crawls), OAI-SearchBot (search, 49% of AI search crawls), ChatGPT-User (user actions, 96% of AI user traffic). BrightEdge recommends allowing search/user crawlers while optionally restricting training crawlers. Review robots.txt quarterly as new AI agents emerge regularly.
