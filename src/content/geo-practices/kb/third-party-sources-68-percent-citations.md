## Summary
Two major 2026 studies converge: Erlin (500+ brands) found 68% of AI citations come from third-party sources, with only 32% from brand-owned websites. Analyze AI (83,670 citations across ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity) found 82.9% from third party — Claude cites brand sites most (22.2%), ChatGPT least (13.5%). Reddit provides 3.4x more citations than owned content, Wikipedia 2.9x, review platforms 2.6x.

## Details
- Erlin (500+ brands, 2026): 68% of citations from third-party, 32% from owned sites. Citation lift vs owned: Reddit 3.4x, Wikipedia 2.9x, G2/Capterra 2.6x, YouTube 2.1x.
- Source diversity compounds: 1 source type = 18% coverage, 2 = 35%, 3 = 58%, 5+ = 78%.
- Analyze AI (Nov 2025-Jan 2026): 82.9% from third-party sources. Top 10 brands captured 30% of all mentions (Salesforce 6.3%).
- Muck Rack confirmed 82% from earned media. Stacker: distribution to publications increases citations up to 325% vs own-site only.
- Implication: earned media and review platforms are higher leverage than on-page optimization alone.
