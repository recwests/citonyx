## Summary
Of 117 completed GEO tasks across 44 brands, 34 (29%) beat their control group by more than 5 points, 52 (44%) stayed within plus or minus 5 points and 31 (26%) moved down. The median relative change across all 117 tasks was zero.

## Details
- Method: each task's targeted prompts were compared with the same brand's untouched prompts over equal windows; the median control set was 37 prompts.
- 106 of the 117 tasks were content-gap work; outreach and rewrite tasks were too few to split out.
- Implication: without a control set and enough wait time, it rose after we published is indistinguishable from the 12.5-point monthly noise in the same report.
- Caveat: the authors call this correlation, since competitors publish and models change mid-test, and each task covers only a handful of noisy prompts.
