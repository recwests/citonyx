## Summary
SimilarWeb analysis (June 2026): Perplexity Pro subscribers generate 2.1× more search queries per session than free-tier users. With an average of 7 queries per session, paid users display deeper exploration behavior — they are more likely to follow citation chains and engage with sourced content.

## Details
- Paid users are more valuable both as a traffic source and as potential customers.
- Paired with Perplexity's higher citation density (5.8 avg citations per answer vs ChatGPT's 4.1 per Presenc AI data), Perplexity citations may represent disproportionate value per query.
- Brands should prioritize Perplexity visibility for B2B and niche audiences.
