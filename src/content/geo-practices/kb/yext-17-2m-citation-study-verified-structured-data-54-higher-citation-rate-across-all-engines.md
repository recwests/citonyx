## Summary

Yext Research analyzed 17.2M AI citations: verified structured data gives 54% higher citation odds across all four major engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude). 91% of AI citations come from brand-managed sources. AI models prioritize structured, verifiable facts. Clean, consistent structured data across surfaces is the primary driver of AI visibility.

## Details

Yext confirmed AI engines differ in retrieval logic but converge on structured, verifiable data. Brand-owned data is the most reliable citation source across platforms. This validates the schema-first approach to GEO: mark up entity relationships before chasing citations. Organization schema + SameAs identifiers, FAQPage for Q&A, HowTo for instructions, Product with price/availability for commerce.
