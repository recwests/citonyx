## Summary

Across 200 Stripe-connected sites (41.2M sessions, 168K payments), AI traffic converts at 2.3% median. By engine: Claude 3.4%, Perplexity 3.1%, ChatGPT 2.5%, Gemini 1.4%, AI Overviews 1.1%. Deep blog pages with inline CTA convert 3.5x higher than without — the single largest controllable conversion lever.

## Details

Attrifast studied 200 anonymized Stripe-connected SMB sites. B2B SaaS AI conversion: 2.7% vs 1.4% Google organic (1.9x lift). Ecommerce AI: 1.6% vs 2.1% Google organic (Google leads). Perplexity RPV highest at $1.42, Claude $1.18, ChatGPT $0.87. AI traffic compounded 13.4% monthly (Dec 2025-May 2026). ChatGPT 71% of AI sessions, Gemini 12%, Perplexity 8%, Claude 6%. 64% of AI sessions land on deep blog pages. Landing page conversion: pricing/checkout 8.1%, feature/comparison 4.7%, deep blog with inline CTA 3.2%, deep blog without 0.9% (3.5x gap). Branded queries convert at 6.4% vs 2.0% non-branded. Desktop converts 1.6x higher than mobile for AI traffic.
