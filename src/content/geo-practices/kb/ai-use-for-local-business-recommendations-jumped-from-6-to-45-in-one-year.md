## Summary
BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026, a panel of 1,002 US adults, found 45% now use ChatGPT or another generative AI tool for local business recommendations, up from 6% a year earlier. 42% trust AI recommendations as much as traditional reviews, and 82% read AI-generated review summaries.

## Details
- Method: representative panel of 1,002 US adults via SurveyMonkey, with a subset of 455 respondents who used an AI tool for local business recommendations in the last 12 months (brightlocal.com).
- Besides the jump from 6% to 45%, 40% trust AI platforms to give business recommendations and 42% trust AI as much as traditional reviews.
- 82% read AI-generated review summaries and 23% would rely solely on those summaries.
- On reviews themselves, 74% only care about reviews from the last 90 days and 31% ignore businesses rated below 4.5 stars.
