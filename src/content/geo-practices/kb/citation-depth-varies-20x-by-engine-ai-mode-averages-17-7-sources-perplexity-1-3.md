## Summary
Across ~13,000 AI answers on six engines and six verticals (July 2026), Google AI Mode averages 17.7 citations per answer, ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot 4-8, and Perplexity just 1.3 — dropping to 0.0 for dining queries. AI Overviews trigger on 98% of shopping queries but only 3% of restaurant queries, per cloro.

## Details
- Perplexity's citation surface is topic-gated: 2.3 for commerce, 3.8 for voice/finance, 0.0 for dining, 0.01 for cross-category product picks.
- Brand-intent queries suppress citations across all engines.
- Reddit is a top-2 cited domain in every vertical (22% of restaurant answers, 19% of commerce); YouTube leads commerce (19%) and voice.
- Each vertical has its own authority layer — RTINGS/NerdWallet/OpenTable/Eater — so a single get-cited playbook does not exist.
- Being one of 20 AI Mode citations is far weaker than being one of ChatGPT's four. Caveats: vendor corpus, US/English, commercial-skewed prompts.
