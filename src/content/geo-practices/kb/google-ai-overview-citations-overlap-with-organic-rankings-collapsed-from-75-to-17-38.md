## Summary

BrightEdge's 16-month study documented: AIO citation overlap with organic top-10 collapsed from ~75% (mid-2025) to 17-38% (early 2026). However, 54.5% of citations come from pages ranking 1-100 organically. The overlap grew from 32.3% to 54.5% for top-100. Position 21-100 is the sweet spot for overlap growth. Only 16.7% of AIO citations come from top-10 results — Google sources diverse ranked content.

## Details

The apparent contradiction (75%→17-38% collapse for top-10 vs 32%→54% growth for top-100) reflects that AIO diversity moved from a narrow top-10 pool to a broader top-100 pool. By industry: healthcare 75.3% overlap, education 72.6%, insurance 68.6%, e-commerce only 22.9%. Google's FastSearch algorithm (RankEmbed model) retrieves differently from organic ranking signals, prioritizing semantic matching and user intent over traditional ranking factors like links.
