## Summary
EMGI crawled 3,254 B2B SaaS sites: 42.3% serve a valid llms.txt (5x the general web rate of ~8.7%). Yet companies with llms.txt were cited by ChatGPT at 61.5% vs 58.2% without — a gap that vanishes when controlling for company size.

## Details
Adoption rises with company size: 53.9% among enterprise leaders vs 41.1% mid-market. At least 17.4% of files were auto-generated by SEO plugins (Yoast, AIOSEO, Rank Math), not deliberately created. Only 7% of SaaS sites serve llms-full.txt. 95% of SaaS sites blocking AI crawlers block only training-class bots while leaving retrieval bots free. Ahrefs' June 2026 analysis of 137,210 domains found 97% of llms.txt files received zero traffic. SE Ranking's XGBoost model found removing llms.txt actually improved citation prediction accuracy. The honest position: create llms.txt as a hedge and documentation aid, but don't expect it to move citation metrics. Invest in authority and citable content where scarcity still exists.
