## Summary
ChatGPT has two distinct citation surfaces: default mode (parametric, no citations, favors Wikipedia/established brands) and Search mode (live retrieval via Bing, 4.1 avg citations, rewards fresh structured content). Brands must optimize for both simultaneously. ChatGPT accounts for 87.4% of all AI referral traffic.

## Details
Presenc AI's analysis of 5,200 ChatGPT Search responses (Q1 2026) shows ChatGPT Search cites 4.1 sources per answer vs Perplexity's 5.8. GPT-5 models have shown volatile citation breadth: GPT-5.3 era averaged ~15 unique domains per answer; GPT-5.4 cited brand sites at 57% with 8.5 sub-queries; GPT-5.5 reversed to 47% with site: operator usage dropping from 40.5% to 12.6%. ChatGPT-5.6 Sol (July 2026) re-introduced site: operators on up to 71% of searches (Writesonic). Bing Webmaster Tools submission directly raises citation probability.
