## Summary
Across 432 creator questions run on 9 September 2026, Gemini cited YouTube in 102 answers, and ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity cited it in 0, 0 and 16 respectively. Perplexity's most-cited domains were reddit.com, cited in 230 of 432 answers, and linkedin.com, 216; ChatGPT favoured official documentation over community platforms.

## Details
The 15 most-cited domains overall were reddit.com, seen in 16.4% of 1,728 answers, linkedin.com at 12.9%, medium.com at 7.1% and youtube.com at 6.9%: four of the top five are places where people write about other people. ChatGPT's top sources were help.openai.com (24), developers.google.com (23) and blog.google (13); Claude spread across semrush.com and searchengineland.com at 12 each. Perplexity's social counts are floors, because its 16.4 sources per answer exceeded the ten domains recorded per response.
