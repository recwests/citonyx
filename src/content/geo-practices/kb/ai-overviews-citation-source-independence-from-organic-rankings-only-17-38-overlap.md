## Summary

The relationship between organic ranking and AI Overview citation is deteriorating. In mid-2025, 76% of AIO citations came from top-10 organic results. By early 2026, this dropped to 38% (Ahrefs) and as low as 17% (BrightEdge). This is the clearest evidence that GEO is a distinct discipline from SEO. 46x difference in brand citation rates between ChatGPT (0.59%) and Perplexity (13.05%).

## Details

Top 15 domains capture 68% of all consolidated AI citation share — more extreme than Google PageRank ever produced. 62% of brands are completely absent from AI-generated answers. Only 23% of marketers invest in GEO measurement. First-mover advantage: dominant citation positions calcifying around early adopters through mid-2026. Citation patterns compound and self-reinforce.
