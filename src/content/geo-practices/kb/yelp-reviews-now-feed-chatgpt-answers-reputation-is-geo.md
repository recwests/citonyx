## Summary
On July 23 2026 Yelp confirmed licensing its reviews, photos and business data to OpenAI, and ChatGPT now pulls them into local answers; Request-a-Quote comes next. Unanswered one-star reviews and repeated complaints can teach AI models to associate a brand with those problems — a brand's online reputation is now its GEO.

## Details
- Go Fish Digital reads the deal as confirmation that a website is no longer the only source a model reads: reviews, forums and third-party profiles are synthesized into one answer.
- Practical levers: claim and complete every profile (hours, categories, services, photos); respond to critical reviews so the model has context rather than an unanswered complaint; watch complaint patterns, not just star average; keep data consistent across Yelp, Google and the site; treat Request-a-Quote as a live sales channel.
- Reddit has made similar licensing deals, so reputation platforms are becoming foundational inputs to AI search.
- Caveat: agency commentary, though the deal itself is confirmed by Search Engine Land coverage.
