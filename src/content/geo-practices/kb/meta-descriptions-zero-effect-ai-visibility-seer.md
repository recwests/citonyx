## Summary

Seer Interactive's 6-week controlled experiment (21 treated pages + 10 controls) found meta descriptions have zero measurable effect on AI visibility. Replacing optimized meta descriptions with a period or neutral placeholder yielded −1.3% effect on AI crawls (95% CI: −5.9% to +3.3%) — statistically zero.

## Details

The experiment used Bayesian counterfactual modeling across May–June 2026. Blanked pages rose +22% in AI bot traffic, placeholder +12.6%, control +21.7% — all moved together with sitewide trends. AI citation behavior was unchanged. Google already rewrites ~70% of meta descriptions in traditional search. Practical takeaway: stop optimizing meta descriptions for AI. Focus on answer-first structure, schema markup, and brand mentions instead.
