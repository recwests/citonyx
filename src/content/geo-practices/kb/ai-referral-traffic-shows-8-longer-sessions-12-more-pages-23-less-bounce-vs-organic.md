## Summary

Adobe Analytics reports AI-referred visits have stronger engagement: 8% longer sessions, 12% more pages viewed, 23% lower bounce rates compared to regular search referrals. However, AI-sourced traffic shows lower purchase intent — users are further along in research but hesitate to finalize transactions due to lingering trust issues.

## Details

AI assistants refine user needs before sending clicks, producing more engaged but more cautious visitors. Average organic CTR drops ~35% when AI Overview is present. Perplexity saw +524% usage surge in 2024. ChatGPT has ~800M weekly active users. 'No click searches' increasing. The two-step journey: AI for first answer, search engine for verification.
