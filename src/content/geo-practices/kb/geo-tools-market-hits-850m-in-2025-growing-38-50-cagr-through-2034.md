## Summary

The GEO tools market reached ~$850M in 2025 with 38-50% annual growth forecast through 2034. Over $300M in venture funding flowed between mid-2025 and spring 2026. Entry pricing ranges from $29/mo (Otterly) to $250/mo (Scrunch), with enterprise tools like Profound costing $499-$1,499/mo.

## Details

Seven tools ranked by weighted fit score: Analytika leads at 66/100, followed by Writesonic 52, Profound 50, Peec AI 48, Scrunch 45, Searchable 42, Otterly 37. Peec AI covers 8 engines with 4-hour refresh; Profound monitors 10+ engines with 90-second refresh cycles. Profound captured 99.8% of citation events; Peec achieves 94.7% at 4.9x lower cost. G2 review counts vary (Profound cited as 140+ to ~303 reviews). Otterly $29/mo best for solo founders; Profound enterprise for Fortune 500.
