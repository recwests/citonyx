## Summary
In the Tow Center's March 2025 citation audit, Perplexity answered with incorrect citations 37% of the time, the lowest error rate of eight engines, while the Arena Search leaderboard still puts GPT-5.6 Sol at 1257 against Perplexity's best Sonar model at 1139.

## Details
Perplexity's product layer complicates vendor comparisons: its model picker lists Sonar 2, GPT-5.6 Terra, Gemini 3.7 Flash and Claude Sonnet 5, so a Perplexity answer often comes from an OpenAI or Anthropic model rather than a Perplexity one. NeuralTrust ran no tests of its own and compiled vendor pricing, help pages and named press reports between 21 September and 5 October 2026. The Tow Center data predates current models.
