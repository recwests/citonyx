## Summary
A $50M ARR B2B SaaS revenue operations platform ran a 90-day GEO program targeting ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Three workstreams: pillar content rebuild around a single canonical framework, 8 deep comparison articles, and Person+Organization schema sitewide. Citation rate lifted from 8% to 24% across 240 priority prompts. Influenced pipeline: $1.4M during the program.

## Details
The brand had strong traditional SEO (top 3 for 40% of keywords) but lagged in AI visibility. Comparison articles (with quick-verdict tables and 60-word AI summaries) earned 31% citation rate within 45 days. Adding Person schema with sameAs to LinkedIn/Wikidata/GitHub lifted Perplexity citation rate ~7pp. Total program cost ~$120k (1 GEO lead + 1 senior writer + 1 editor + 0.2 FTE engineering).
