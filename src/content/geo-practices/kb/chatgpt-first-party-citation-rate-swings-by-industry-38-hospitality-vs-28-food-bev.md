## Summary
ChatGPT's first-party citation rate swings sharply by industry: it cited official hotel websites 38% of the time in hospitality, but first-party dropped to 28% in food & beverage and rose to 44% for nonprofits (TLSOLS 500-prompt review). Generic ChatGPT playbooks fail because sourcing instinct is category-dependent.

## Details
Observed across 500+ real prompts logged by TLSOLS across SaaS, local business, ecommerce, healthcare, finance, travel and marketing. ChatGPT also shows the widest industry-to-industry citation swing of any engine, and its listing citations concentrate heavily on Google. Sourcing is category-driven: 48.73% of its citations come from listings overall, but first-party share ranges 28-44% depending on vertical. Implication: audit how ChatGPT actually behaves in your category before copying a playbook written for someone else's — the right move in your space may be the wrong one elsewhere. Caveat: agency sample, not a controlled multi-domain study; directional rather than definitive.
