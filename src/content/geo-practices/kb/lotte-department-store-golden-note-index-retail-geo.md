## Summary

Lotte Department Store is implementing GEO to appear in ChatGPT recommendations for foreign tourists visiting Seoul. Their retail AI task force built the Golden Note Index to structure brand info so AI accurately provides store locations and benefits in travel itinerary answers, optimized in 4 languages.

## Details

According to Focusrite research, 56% of global travelers used generative AI for trip planning, rising to 72% among Gen Z. Lotte's approach: restructure content so when users ask ChatGPT about 'good shopping places in Seoul' or 'indoor tourist attractions near Jamsil,' Lotte information naturally appears. The Golden Note Index structures brand info (store locations, foreign tourist benefits, tourism content) across English, Japanese, simplified Chinese, and traditional Chinese. Additional tactics: outdoor ads at Incheon Airport and tourist areas prompting AI searches, and enhanced multilingual AI chatbot 'Dustin' with QR codes in stores. One of the first documented cases of a major Korean retailer implementing GEO for international tourism.
