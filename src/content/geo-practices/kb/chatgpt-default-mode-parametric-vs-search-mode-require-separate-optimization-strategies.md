## Summary
ChatGPT has two distinct citation surfaces: default mode (parametric, no citations, favors Wikipedia/established brands) and Search mode (live retrieval via Bing, 4.1 avg citations, rewards fresh structured content). In 1,800 default-mode responses, 73% of brand mentions reflected training-time content (Wikipedia, major press), 19% community discussion, and only 8% the brand's own site — search-mode wins don't transfer to default mode (Presenc, 2026). Brands must optimize for both simultaneously. ChatGPT accounts for 87.4% of all AI referral traffic.

## Details
Presenc AI's analysis of 5,200 ChatGPT Search responses (Q1 2026) shows ChatGPT Search cites 4.1 sources per answer vs Perplexity's 5.8. Wikipedia is the single largest cited domain at 14%, followed by major news (22%), industry blogs (17%) and brand/product sites (11%). The default-mode surface skews to incumbents; freshness and structure win in Search mode, where citations are exposed — earning Wikipedia and major-press presence is the lever that works across both modes. GPT-5 models have shown volatile citation breadth: GPT-5.3 era averaged ~15 unique domains per answer; GPT-5.4 cited brand sites at 57% with 8.5 sub-queries; GPT-5.5 reversed to 47% with site: operator usage dropping from 40.5% to 12.6%. ChatGPT-5.6 Sol (July 2026) re-introduced site: operators on up to 71% of searches (Writesonic). Bing Webmaster Tools submission directly raises citation probability.
