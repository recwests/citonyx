## Summary

82-84% of AI citations come from earned media sources rather than brand-owned content, per Muck Rack's analysis of 25+ million links across major AI engines.

## Details

Muck Rack's Generative Pulse report analyzed 25 million links across AI engines. 82% of AI citations came from earned media, 94% from non-paid sources. Stacker and Scrunch ran a controlled study: identical content distributed through third-party news outlets produced a 239% median lift in AI search visibility vs brand-only distribution. Ahrefs found web mentions correlate 3x stronger than backlinks. Digital PR is the highest-yield input to AI visibility.
