## Summary
In Ahrefs' 15-million fan-out sample, 6.8% of queries used the site: operator across roughly 116,000 domains, and 75.9% of the most common site: searches pointed at a brand's own site, against 11.9% at retailers and 1.9% at Reddit and Facebook combined.

## Details
Top targets were apple.com with 9,442 site: mentions, ikea.com at 7,361 and nike.com at 5,210, with gov.br and gov.uk also in the top 15.

- The word official appeared in 13.1% of English fan-outs in September, up from 10.1% in August.
- Practical read: prices, specifications, compatibility, warranty and support answers must sit on indexable, plainly written brand pages.
- If those facts live only in PDFs, chat widgets or gated portals, ChatGPT resolves the question elsewhere.
