## Summary
Traditional SEO did not transfer to AI: Juicebox ranked #9-11 on Google yet was invisible in ChatGPT and Perplexity answers. After an 8-month GEO program it became the second most-cited source (behind Wikipedia), lifted Perplexity traffic +234% and ChatGPT +336%, and earned 4,500+ new sign-ups — 20% of all inbound.

## Details
- Brand visibility rose +56% across priority discovery queries; 5+ blog posts became top-cited pages on Perplexity for 40+ high-intent queries.
- Listicle-format articles, competitor comparisons and query-shaped content drove citations; long-form educational content alone was insufficient.
- Sentiment improved from 2.35 to 3.1 on a five-point scale after resolving negative talking points surfaced by LLMs.
- The team adapted within 2 days when ChatGPT moved from GPT-4o to GPT-5 — AI search is a dynamic channel, not set-and-forget.
