## Summary
Machine Relations Index (28,870 source events, 7,124 domains, 6 engines, June 2026): 71% of all cited sources appear in exactly one AI engine's responses. Even the most overlapping pair (Google AI Mode + Grok) agrees on just ~20% of sources. SurfacedBy's parallel study (16,400 answers, 127,198 citations) corroborates the finding: 69.6% of cited domains appear in only one engine and just 2.7% in all five.

## Details
The 71% single-engine exclusivity figure is the most striking measure of platform divergence. Perplexity cited Gartner 0 times while Google AI Mode cited it 98 times — a complete blind spot. Claude cited Crunchbase 51 times versus Grand View Research 5 times, revealing per-engine domain preferences. SurfacedBy adds volume and source-type data: Gemini cites 11.0 sources per answer versus 3.7 for ChatGPT; Google AI Mode sent 11.2% of citations to YouTube (Perplexity 8.8%, Claude 0.02%); top-10 domains took 20.6% of citations. Strategic implication: brands must track and optimize for each engine independently — a brand cited by ChatGPT may be completely invisible to Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, or Grok.
