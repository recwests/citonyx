## Summary
BugViso ran 50 buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude with web search on, collecting 400 answers and 2,525 citations. The brand's own domain was cited in 97.5% of branded answers and in all 21 unbranded answers that named the brand, but in only 10.7% of unbranded answers.

## Details
- Per engine, unbranded own-site citation ranged from 4% (Gemini, Claude) to 26% (Perplexity); the brand's established competitors were linked on only 30% of their mentions.
- On unbranded prompts third-party pages carried 75.9% of citations and the brand's own site 2.2%.
- Actionable form: pages that state plainly what you offer, for whom and at what price are what earn both the mention and the link, because the engine names a brand when a retrieved page supports the claim.
- Caveat: one small brand, one category (website-audit software), two runs, single vendor test; directional, not a benchmark.
