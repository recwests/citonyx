## Summary

BrightEdge surveyed 750+ search/content/digital marketers in 2026. 68% making strategic adjustments for AI search, 57% still figuring it out. Strategic response split: 33% not yet acting, 27% dual focus (AIO+ChatGPT), 20% AIO-only, 18% comprehensive (ChatGPT+AIO+others). 54% say SEO/digital marketing owns AI search efforts. Muck Rack study (Jul 2026): 73% of PR pros say GEO is important, but 29% say no one owns it, 39% not measuring it.

## Details

Key organizational insight: GEO success requires cross-functional strategy — SEO alone is insufficient, needs PR, content, product, and executive leadership involvement. 54% of companies rely on SEO teams for AI search — a concentration risk because SEO teams often lack organizational support for cross-functional execution. Muck Rack State of PR 2026: 45% of PR pros cite media measurement as major work, yet 39% don't measure GEO at all. The 'ownership gap' is the main operational bottleneck for most organizations.
