## Summary
Across 100 SaaS buyer prompts per engine in October 2026, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini named 5.4 distinct brands per prompt combined but only 2.6 in all three; mean pairwise Jaccard on brands was 0.69. Brand shortlists converge far more than the sources behind them do.

## Details
- ClearBrand, 12 October 2026, 300 answers across three engines. HubSpot led with 77 of 400 answers, then Zoho 75, Salesforce 63, QuickBooks 62 and NetSuite 46.
- Contrast with source-level disagreement: Hendricks' October 2026 index put cross-engine cited-domain Jaccard at 0.181, and Trakkr found models agreeing on the number-one brand only 4% of the time.
- Implication: track brand share separately from citation share, since a stable brand set can hide churn in the pages and third-party lists that justify it.
- Caveat: single run, SaaS only, curated brand list, three engines.
