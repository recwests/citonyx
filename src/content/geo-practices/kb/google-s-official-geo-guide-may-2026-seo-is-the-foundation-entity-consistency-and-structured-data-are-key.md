## Summary

On May 15, 2026, Google published its first official guide on optimizing for generative AI features in Search. Key pillars: (1) quality content that demonstrates first-hand expertise, (2) E-E-A-T signals (author credentials, expert citations), (3) structured data that helps AI understand page context, (4) entity consistency across the web so Google can connect brand references. llms.txt is notably absent from Google's recommendations. SEO remains the foundation; GEO adds AI-specific layers.

## Details

- Published May 15, 2026 on Google Search Central.
- Google's framework: traditional SEO + structured data + E-E-A-T + entity consistency.
- Entity consistency: brand name, logo, social profiles, and descriptions should be identical across all platforms.
- Structured data: populate Article, Organization, Product, FAQ, and HowTo schema types.
- E-E-A-T: content by named experts with verifiable credentials, cited authoritative sources.
- llms.txt: NOT mentioned — confirming Google does not consider it relevant.
- Practical takeaway: follow Google's official framework. Solidify traditional SEO, add structured data, ensure entity consistency across the web, and demonstrate expertise through named bylines.
