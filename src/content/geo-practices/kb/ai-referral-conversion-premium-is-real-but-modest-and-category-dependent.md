## Summary
AI-referred traffic converts higher than organic search, but the premium is modest and category-dependent: ChatGPT ecommerce referrals convert at 1.81% versus 1.39% for non-branded organic (+31% lift), while average order value runs 14.3% lower, per a Search Engine Land study of 94 brands. AI referral traffic remains ~1% of total ecommerce sessions.

## Details
Perplexity referrals convert at 10.5% versus 1.76% for Google organic (~6x), because Perplexity surfaces few links per response so clicks carry high intent (Adweek). Semrush measured LLM visitors converting at 4.4x the organic rate. ChatGPT revenue-per-session still beats non-branded organic after the 14.3% AOV discount, but the margin is thinner than conversion rates alone suggest. Practical advice: model both conversion rate and AOV per platform, track each engine's referrer separately in GA4, and treat GEO as incremental to organic, not a replacement.
