## Summary
Seer Interactive restructured 39 Trex pages — direct answers, clear definitions, step-by-step guides and comparison tables — targeting queries that trigger Google AI Overviews. Year over year, owned AIO references grew 2.8x (71 to 199 keywords), impression-weighted CTR rose from 0.78% to 1.36%, and average SERP position improved from 6.28 to 3.08.

## Details
- 17 of 39 optimized pages (44%) showed both AIO citation and click growth simultaneously; only 3 pages showed AIO gains with click declines (Seer Interactive, Jun 2026 data).
- Owned AIO reference rate ran 53-78% on optimized pages vs 43-66% on the full tracked keyword universe — content optimization lifts citation rate.
- One page gained +12 AIO references yet lost 5.2% clicks: citations can reinforce brand authority even when the answer suppresses a click.
- Repeatable elements: search-intent audits, H1/H2 structure, directly answering the triggering question, internal linking, and keyword-level AIO tracking.
- Agency case study with real GSC data and a clear method; single client, so directional rather than proof.
