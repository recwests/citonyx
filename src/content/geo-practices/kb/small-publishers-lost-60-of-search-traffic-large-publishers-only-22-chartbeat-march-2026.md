## Summary
Chartbeat (March 2026, reported by Axios): small publishers (1,000-10,000 daily pageviews) lost 60% of search referral traffic over two years. Medium publishers lost 47%. Large publishers (100,000+ daily pageviews) lost just 22%. AI chatbot referrals grew 200%+ but still account for less than 1% of total publisher pageviews.

## Details
The disparity is structural: large publishers with brand recognition and E-E-A-T signals retain more AI citation share. The generative search gap favors established domains. Top 50 global domains capture ~29% of all AIO mentions (BrightEdge). Small publishers must prioritize niche authority, original data, and structured content optimized for direct extraction rather than broad informational coverage.
