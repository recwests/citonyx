## Summary
After the 15 October link redesign, Google again declined to add click data to AI Performance reports, saying it is working with site owners to decide which insights help and will add metrics over time. Until then, AI visibility has to be planned on impressions, citation share and branded-search lift rather than clicks.

## Details
Google's stated rationale is that it is still deciding what to expose; Search Engine Roundtable notes that clicks are what publishers most want and the primary metric in Google Ads.

- Practical workaround: pair Search Console AI Performance impressions with server-log or CDN referrer data, branded-search volume and prompt-level citation tracking, then triangulate.
- Budgeting on AI clicks will systematically understate the channel.
- Any vendor claiming per-URL AI click data is inferring it rather than measuring it.
