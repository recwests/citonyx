## Summary
A 10,000-prompt benchmark across six engines (January to April 2026) found recency weighting differs about fourfold: Grok drew roughly 85% of citations from content under 90 days old, Perplexity about 70%, Gemini 50%, ChatGPT with browsing 40%, Copilot 40% and Claude about 30%. On time-sensitive topics freshness is the strongest single lever.

## Details
- The source recommends a refresh cadence of at least quarterly where recency matters, and faster for engines such as Grok that lean on very recent posts.
- The same benchmark reported Reddit at 47% of citation instances and llms.txt at a 12% citation lift, and both clash with later 2026 measurement: Hendricks found Reddit present in 68.1% of Perplexity answers but holding only 4.45% of its citations, and Otterly's 90-day test found llms.txt had negligible short-term impact.
- Confidence is conflicting for that reason, though the per-engine recency ordering is consistent with other 2026 datasets showing a Perplexity freshness premium.
- Vendor benchmark: 10,000 prompts with a documented window and method, but produced by a GEO consultancy.
