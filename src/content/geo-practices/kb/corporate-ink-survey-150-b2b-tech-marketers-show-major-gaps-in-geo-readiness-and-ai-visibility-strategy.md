## Summary
Corporate Ink's 2026 survey of 150 B2B tech marketers found systematic gaps in AI visibility strategy and GEO execution. Most B2B brands lack dedicated GEO programs, measurement frameworks, and understanding of how AI engines select and cite sources.

## Details
The report highlights that the gap between market presence and AI presence is invisible pipeline loss. BCG found GenAI is the #2 most influential purchase touchpoint. Bain reports 85% of B2B buyers purchase from the shortlist AI helped shape. A brand with 30% market share can show up in just 5% of relevant AI responses (CiteGap). The 'GEO and AI Visibility in 2026 Report' documents that most brands cannot answer basic questions about their AI citation rate, which engines cite them, or what content changes would improve visibility.
