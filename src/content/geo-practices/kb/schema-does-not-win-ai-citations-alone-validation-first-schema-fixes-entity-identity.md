## Summary
Search Engine Journal argues schema markup will not win AI citations by itself but helps engines verify who you are, and should be built validation-first: pick the most specific honest entity type, assign a stable ID, and validate one page before scaling, because scaling early multiplies the same error.

## Details
The reported case: a complete Person schema on an executive bio page linked to the correct professional profile and company details, after which the AI Overview identified the correct individual within days. Schema cannot fix thin content or invented credentials and does not replace visible HTML. Third-party data cited alongside it: pages with three or more applicable schema types earned about 13% more Perplexity citations, though a controlled Otterly AI test found limited lift from schema alone.
